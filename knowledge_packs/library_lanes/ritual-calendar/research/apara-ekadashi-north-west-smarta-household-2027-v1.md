## Fixed source-labelled content and absence of a narrative

The reviewed N. A. Deshpande English expression of *Padma Purana*, Uttara
Khanda chapter 50, lines 54-65, is Yudhishthira's question and Krishna's
mahatmya response. It contains no developed origin story. It names Apara,
promises unending merit, fame, freedom from sin, heaven and Vishnu's world, and
lists acts it treats as grave wrongs: killing a brahmana or family member,
procuring an abortion, censuring others, sexual conduct, false testimony,
fraudulent weights and measures, purported false texts, fraudulent astrology
and medicine, a kshatriya fleeing battle, and a disciple censuring a teacher.

The chapter then compares Apara's promised result with pilgrimage bathing at
Prayaga, Kashi, Gaya, Gautami, Krishnaveni, Kumbhakedara, Badarika and
Kurukshetra; sacrifice; and gifts of elephants, horses, gold, a cow, land and
other presents. It uses violent metaphors for destroying sin, denigrates people
who do not observe the vow, and concludes with fasting, Vishnu worship and
promised absolution and merit from recitation/listening.

## Consumer treatment of source tensions

- There is no beginning-to-end Apara character story in this reviewed chapter.
  Consumer copy should label it as a source mahatmya/list rather than fabricate
  a narrative or import a popular tale from an unreviewed expression.
- Killing, violence, false testimony, fraudulent measurement and deceptive
  professional claims can cause serious harm. Ritual does not cancel safety,
  evidence, restitution, reporting, professional discipline or law.
- The phrase translated as "procured an abortion" is historical source
  language, not Devam's medical or moral classification. Do not ask whether a
  user has had, sought, supported or provided an abortion; do not shame pregnancy
  outcomes; do not expose reproductive information; do not obstruct lawful
  care. India Code's Medical Termination of Pregnancy Act provides a framework
  for termination by registered medical practitioners and includes protection
  of a woman's privacy. Case-specific medical and legal questions require
  confidential qualified care.
- Brahmana/kshatriya caste ranking, battlefield duty and preceptor obedience are
  source-era normative claims, not universal rules of human worth, occupation,
  military conduct, criticism, education or citizenship. Do not shame a person
  for caste, leaving combat, questioning authority or reporting misconduct.
- Criticism is not automatically wrongdoing. Distinguish malicious falsehood
  from evidence-based complaint, whistleblowing, review, disagreement and
  safeguarding disclosure.
- "Fraudulent physician" and "fraudulent astrologer" refer to deceptive claims
  in the text. Do not accuse or diagnose an individual from suspicion. Use
  evidence, accurate credentials, transparent limits and proper complaint routes.
- The pilgrimage, eclipse bath, ancestor offering, sacrifice and animal/land/
  gold gifts are comparison claims, not household instructions or requirements.
- Hell, caste expulsion, heaven, sin destruction and ritual absolution are
  theological source claims, not threats, guarantees or legal outcomes.

- Do not begin or intensify fasting. Never change insulin, medicine, meals,
  hydration, hygiene or sleep; inherited fasting requires qualified advice.
- No eclipse bath, pilgrimage, ancestor rite, animal/cow/land/gold gift,
  sacrifice, public confession, disclosure, contact or payment is required.
- Never ask about pregnancy, abortion, miscarriage, fertility, sexual history or
  medical records in this route. Preserve privacy and confidential qualified
  care; never delay or obstruct urgent care.
- Do not rank dignity by caste, occupation, military service, teacher status,
  religious learning, disability, wealth, sex, pregnancy outcome or observance.
- Do not silence complaint, whistleblowing, review, disagreement or safeguarding
  disclosure by calling criticism a sin. Protect evidence and use safe routes.
- Do not accuse a doctor, astrologer, teacher, merchant, witness or anyone else

- Serious violence, killing, false testimony, fraud or professional misconduct
  is not erased by fasting, prayer or regret. Preserve safety, restitution,

- No sin removal, acquittal, professional licence/clearance, fame, heaven,
