# Ugadi / Yugadi Karnataka-Andhra-Telangana household lane: research dossier v1

Checked: 2026-08-13

## Frozen applicability denominator

This dossier supports one combined household lane for Kannada-calendar Yugadi in Karnataka and Telugu-calendar Ugadi in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana on Chaitra Shukla Pratipada. It answers individual, family-led and institution-participant use. A family, sampradaya, temple or community form overrides this bounded guide.

Only one material state difference is routed inside the lane: a Karnataka household may use family-known **Bevu Bella**, while an Andhra Pradesh or Telangana household may use family-known six-taste **Ugadi Pachadi**. Neither food is required. Ingredient lists, prayers, feast dishes, rangoli designs, dress, oil-bath details and exact mango-leaf arrangements are minor family/material differences and do not create state-copy lanes.

The lane does not teach priest-led temple liturgy, consecration, mantra counts, public-event operations, a forecast as fact, Gudi Padwa, Chaitra Navaratri, Cheti Chand or a solar new year. A temple or public Panchanga Sravanam is participant-only and requires the institution's live schedule and directions.

## 2027 timing fixture

Practitioner calendar results resolve Ugadi/Yugadi to Wednesday 7 April 2027, Chaitra Shukla Pratipada. Reference results for Hyderabad and Andhra/Karnataka locations agree on the civil date; quoted tithi intervals are location-specific and must not be copied across cities. The product rule is to resolve Chaitra Shukla Pratipada under the user's accepted Kannada or Telugu Panchang and local sunrise/day-boundary convention. Every temple, public Panchanga reading or cultural programme time is a live institutional fact.

## Meaning and source-labelled accounts

- Karnataka Tourism describes Yugadi/Ugadi as the Kannada New Year and the start of the Chaitra lunar-calendar year. Its “yuga + adi” explanation is a current public etymological account, not a proof that every tradition uses the same New Year.
- The same page records a living belief that Brahma began creation on this day. Devam labels this as a contemporary Hindu tradition account from that source; it is not verified history and is not silently assigned to an unnamed scripture.
- Karnataka Tourism distinguishes Bevu Bella—bitter neem and sweet jaggery as challenges and happiness—from the six-taste Ugadi Pachadi especially associated with Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. Telangana district evidence independently records Ugadi Pachadi and six flavours as a metaphor for varied feelings. These are living metaphors, not predictions, medicine or dietary obligations.
- Karnataka Tourism and Telangana public sources describe cleaning/decorating, mango-leaf torana, rangoli, festive food, family gathering, temple visits and Panchanga Sravanam. Andhra Pradesh school material gives a family-life example involving festive dress, pachadi, sweets, relatives and elders' blessings. These sources establish common practice, not a universal ordered vidhi.
- Panchanga Sravanam is an almanac reading by a priest or scholar in some temples, community gatherings and public programmes. Astronomical calendar elements and event dates can be deterministic; annual forecasts remain tradition-specific interpretation and must not be presented as guaranteed weather, crop, financial, medical or personal outcomes.

## Safe consumer boundary

The complete minimum form requires no edible mixture, image, lamp, mango leaf, rangoli, new clothes or purchase: confirm the day, clean one safe space, read a source-labelled account, offer a family-known greeting/prayer or silence, choose one practical new-year action and close.

The standard household form may add a reusable doorway decoration or safely reached mango-leaf torana, a no-powder rangoli, family prayer, state-routed food reflection and a safe meal. Nobody climbs an unstable chair or leans from a balcony/window. Decorations stay away from flame, exits, children, animals and mobility paths. An ordinary bath replaces an oil bath when heat, slipping, skin sensitivity, assistance or preference makes oil unsuitable.

Bevu Bella and Ugadi Pachadi are optional and use only a trusted family recipe with correctly identified, food-suitable ingredients. Unknown leaves are never eaten. The pack deliberately does not supply a universal recipe because official living descriptions vary and individual health/allergy contexts matter. Omit tasting for pregnancy, children, allergy, clinician-directed diet, illness, medication concerns, uncertain plant identity or simple preference. The reflection remains complete without food.

Food uses clean hands/water, safe ingredients, allergen disclosure and prompt serving/storage. Respectful greetings and elders' blessings require consent; touching feet is never compelled. No observance promises prosperity, health, marks, harvest, rain, wealth or protection.

## Major-difference routing

- Karnataka Bevu Bella versus Andhra Pradesh/Telangana Ugadi Pachadi: one major material difference, explicitly state-routed inside this combined selected lane.
- Ingredient proportions, additional tastes, feast dishes, prayer words, rangoli, clothes, oil bath and torana materials: minor family/material differences within-lane.
- No-edible-mixture, material-light and accessible household forms: complete substitutions within-lane.
- Temple/public Panchanga Sravanam or cultural programme: separate institution-participant procedure with live schedule; not a DIY priestly rite.
- Family or sampradaya deity puja: owner/family authority overrides generic steps; no duplicate universal puja is invented.
- Gudi Padwa, Chaitra Navaratri, Cheti Chand and solar new years: separate observances/owners even when a civil date or seasonal frame overlaps.
- Local agrarian omen or multi-day custom: separate only after direct regional authority and evidence establish a material difference.
