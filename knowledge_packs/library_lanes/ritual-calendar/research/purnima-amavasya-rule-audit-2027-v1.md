# Purnima and Amavasya candidate rule audit 2027

Checked on 2026-08-13 against the ten location-qualified regional calendar
pages recorded in the v3 census.

## Disposition boundary

The census contains 33 raw labels matching Purnima/Pournami/Poornima or
Amavas/Amabas. Twelve month-name Purnimas are recurring phase instances.
Named overlays are not assumed to share one practice merely because they fall
on the same full moon. Dol, Gamha, Guru, Narali, Shakambhari, Sharad, Vat,
Chitra and Madana identities therefore remain explicit candidates.

Guru Poornima is treated as a calendar-language alias of Guru Purnima. Buddha
Purnima is preserved for routing but placed outside this selected Sanatana puja
lane, not erased from the wider Devam universe. Shakambhari Purnima is routed
to the Devi owner through a proposal.

The Telugu discovery page lists Jandhyala Purnima on 2027-08-06 with no rule,
while the North reference lists Shravana Purnima on 2027-08-17. Because
Upakarma/Jandhyala eligibility and dates can depend on Vedic shakha and
sampradaya, this candidate remains blocked rather than receiving generic DIY
instructions.

For new moons, Tamil Aadi, Thai and Mahalaya Amavasai, Odia Mahalaya and
Savitri Amabasya, North/West Sarva Pitru, Maharashtra Pithori, North Mauni and
the conditional Somavati recurrence remain separately scoped when their
calendar or practice context materially changes.

## Limits

This is identity/timing normalization only. The calendar pages do not establish
who should fast, perform ancestor rites, make water offerings, visit a river or
temple, or engage a priest. Those claims need living-practice and textual or
institutional sources, with health, water, crowd and specialist boundaries.
