## Fixed source-labelled account

The reviewed N. A. Deshpande English expression of *Padma Purana*, Uttara
Khanda chapter 52, lines 54-78, begins with Yudhishthira asking Krishna about
Ashadha's dark-half Ekadashi. Krishna names Yogini and tells an old account.

In Alaka, Kubera is devoted to Shiva and employs Hemamali to collect flowers.
Hemamali brings lotuses from Manasa but stays home with his wife Vishalakshi
instead of delivering them for Kubera's midday worship. Kubera sends yakshas to
find him. They describe him through sexualised blame; Kubera summons the
frightened attendant, calls him wicked and curses him to separation, expulsion
and what the translation calls eighteen kinds of leprosy.

Hemamali suffers, remembers his earlier conduct, wanders to the Himalaya and
meets Markandeya. Asked what happened, he truthfully says that he missed the
flower delivery while absorbed in pleasure with his wife and that Kubera cursed
him. Markandeya praises his truthful account, prescribes Yogini-vrata, and the
chapter says Hemamali observes it and the disease disappears completely. It
promises merit equal to feeding eighty-eight thousand Brahmanas and freedom
from sin through the vow, recitation or listening.

## Consumer treatment of source tensions

- The account links a work lapse, sexual desire, a curse and leprosy. This is a
  source narrative, not medical causation. India's NLEP states that leprosy is a
  disease addressed through early detection, treatment, rehabilitation and
  dignified care, with zero stigma and discrimination as an objective.
- “Eighteen kinds of leprosy” is the reviewed translation's historical phrase,
  not a diagnosis or modern classification. Do not diagnose a person from a
  story, appearance, skin patch, numbness, pain, disability or rumour. Use
  qualified health services; ritual does not replace or prove treatment.
- Do not call disease a curse, sin, impurity, divine punishment, sexual result
  or evidence of bad character. Do not isolate, avoid touch, expel, shame,
  conceal, fire or deny housing, education, work, worship or relationship on
  that basis. The official clinical material explicitly rejects curse framing,
  says discrimination is unjustified and rejects distancing/isolation.
- Vishalakshi does not make Hemamali miss the delivery in the source. Adult
  consensual marital affection is not pollution or culpability. Do not blame,
  interrogate, punish or expose a spouse or sexual history.
- Missing a responsibility may require safe acknowledgement and proportionate
  repair. It does not justify insults, threats, forced medical claims,
  separation, expulsion, homelessness, discriminatory firing or disease-based
  punishment. A real workplace dispute belongs to current policy, labour,
  safeguarding or legal routes, not to Kubera's authority in the story.
- Hemamali's truthful answer can support bounded personal accountability, but
  not forced confession, surveillance, self-incrimination, employer access to
  private sexual/medical information or mandatory reconciliation.
- Flower collection at Manasa, Shiva worship, prostration, fasting and feeding
  Brahmanas are source events, not household instructions. Do not pick protected
  plants, enter unsafe or restricted places, travel to the Himalaya, prostrate,
  fast or stage mass feeding from this account.
- Cure, sin removal and merit equal to feeding eighty-eight thousand Brahmanas
  are attributed source claims, not health, legal, employment or spiritual
  guarantees.

## Bounded present-day meaning

A safe ordinary meaning is **acknowledge one missed responsibility and choose a
proportionate repair without blame or stigma**. Select only something within
one's own responsibility: notify the affected person through a safe ordinary
channel, correct a date or record, complete a still-useful task, arrange a
reasonable handover, or state a truthful limit and renegotiate a deadline.

Do not disclose sexual or medical information, confess to a powerful person in
an unsafe setting, contact someone who requested no contact, or demand that a
worker, spouse or patient participate. Serious employment, discrimination,
health, abuse or legal issues require qualified routes; ritual may accompany but
never replace them.

## Safe ordinary household route

- Confirm the local date and authority.
- Keep normal food, water, medicine, hygiene, touch, work breaks and sleep; the
  non-fasting form is complete.
- Remember Vishnu with a familiar name, prayer or silence.
- Hear a bounded Hemamali account, naming its sexual blame, curse, disease,
  expulsion, cure and promised merit as source content rather than modern fact.
- Privately choose one missed responsibility within one's control and one safe,
  proportionate repair or truthful limit. No confession or contact is required.
- Close with gratitude, normal care and no claim of cure, absolution, restored
  employment, reconciliation, merit or freedom from consequences.

No material is required. Optional: an existing Vishnu image/text, a secure
private reminder or an authoritative service page. Silence, no image, no
writing, no purchase, no flowers, no touch and no contact are complete.

## Safety and safeguarding

- Do not begin or intensify fasting. Never change insulin, medicine, meals,
  hydration, treatment, dressing/wound care, hygiene or sleep; inherited fasting
  requires qualified advice.
- Leprosy is not a curse, sexual consequence, sin, impurity or punishment. Do
  not diagnose, shame, isolate, expel or discriminate; do not stop or delay
  qualified diagnosis, treatment, rehabilitation or disability care.
- Preserve medical and sexual privacy. Do not demand skin inspection, touch,
  photographs, diagnosis disclosure, treatment proof, confession or spouse
  participation. Consent can be refused or withdrawn.
- Do not blame Vishalakshi, a spouse, woman, worker, patient or affected person
  for another person's conduct or illness. Adult consensual affection is not
  wrongdoing.
- Do not imitate Kubera's insults, curse, separation, expulsion or arbitrary
  authority. A missed deadline does not license humiliation, homelessness,
  discriminatory firing, retaliation or denial of care.
- Do not collect lotuses or other plants, travel to Manasa/Himalaya, enter
  restricted land, prostrate, stage feeding or make a purchase/donation from
  this guide.
- A private repair does not erase consequences. Preserve evidence, safe
  reporting, reasonable accommodation, restitution, employment procedures,
  medical care and law where relevant.
- No cure, sin removal, purity, restored body, job, relationship, merit,
  salvation or other result is guaranteed.

  privacy, accommodation, dismissal, retaliation or safeguarding concern.
- **Separate institution:** a named temple's current programme, priest-led rite,
  access, safeguarding, fees, flowers or offerings.
- **Separate named observance:** Nirjala, Devshayani or another Ekadashi.
