## Meaning and source-labelled story boundary

The current calendar sources identify all three with Goddess Sheetala and with
devotional hopes concerning fever and historic pox diseases. A peer-reviewed
history of epidemic-deity traditions reports Sheetala in the Skanda Purana as
a pox-related deity and records later iconography: donkey mount, broom,
winnowing fan and water pot. This is a source-labelled historical/theological
account, not a single universal birth story. No pack says that worship prevents,
diagnoses or cures smallpox, chickenpox, measles, fever or any other disease.
Smallpox eradication, vaccination, medical care and public-health advice are
never replaced by ritual.

## Living practice and food-safety boundary

Current calendar sources describe food prepared on the preceding day and no
new cooking on the observance day. That practice is optional and cannot
override food safety. FSSAI guidance says perishable cooked food should be
refrigerated within two hours (within one hour above 90 F / about 32 C), large
amounts should be divided into shallow containers, and refrigeration should be
at 40 F / about 4 C or below. FSSAI also states that food should not remain at
room temperature for more than two hours. Therefore:

- no consumer is told to eat food left overnight at room temperature;
- a no-cook household may use safely refrigerated food, shelf-stable food, or
  freshly obtained safe food without shame;
- infants, older people, pregnant people and anyone immunocompromised or ill
  should follow medical and food-safety advice;
- doubtful food is discarded safely, never offered, served or forced;
- cold food is not medicinal, purifying or mandatory;
- flame, smoke, fasting, temple attendance, animal feeding, neem use and
  previous-day cooking are optional named-family practices.

## Minimum answer route

Confirm location, calendar and whether the family calls the post-Holi event
Basoda or Sheetala Ashtami, or follows Gujarati Shitala Satam. Keep normal
medicine, vaccination, hydration and food. Optionally clean a stable place,
use an existing image or no object, hear the labelled account, pray/read/sit in
silence, share only demonstrably safe food, thank helpers and close cleanly.
Formal puja, mantra count, temple schedule and offerings require the current
named owner.
