## Major variants only

- Tamil water gratitude, the Tamil four-day Pongal sequence, Assamese
  harvest/fire customs, Bengali Haal Khata and temple customs, Kerala
  Vishukkani, Chaitra lunar-new-year indexing and the two Sabarimala events
  materially change applicability or action and are preserved.
- Language, spelling, ordinary foods, clothing colours, flower choice,
  image/no image, familiar prayer/silence and affordable decoration do not
  create separate lanes.
- Vishu is not silently equated with Chingam 1. A shared April harvest season
  does not collapse Puthandu, Vishu or Pohela Boishakha into a national ritual.
- Formal Sabarimala vrata, pilgrimage, temple liturgy, fire structures, trader
  rites and animal contact require their current named owner.

## Safety and evidence limits

No source is used to require fasting, river entry, bonfire, animal handling,
fireworks, spending, gifts, temple attendance or disclosure. Normal food,
water, medicine, insulin, sleep, accessibility, consent, animal welfare,
allergen control, fire safety and current civil instructions prevail. Living
beliefs about luck or prosperity are attributed and never offered as promises.

Completing these packs means bilingual authored-library coverage only. It does not count a lane as projected, hosted, independently released or published,
and it does not verify any of the **19,480** day-answer cells.
