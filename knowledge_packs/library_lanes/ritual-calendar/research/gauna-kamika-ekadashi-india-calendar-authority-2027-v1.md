# Gauna Kamika Ekadashi: India calendar-authority research (2027)

Checked 2026-08-13. This research freezes the evidence and consumer boundary
for `ekadashi-kamika-gauna`. It does not complete or count the separately
accepted `ekadashi-kamika-vaishnava` item.

## Applicability finding

This is not a national, state or generic household lane. It applies only when
the user's accepted local Panchang or inherited calendar authority explicitly
returns **Gauna Kamika Ekadashi** for the user's Indian city. The consumer must
not select the Gauna date merely because it is later, more convenient or also
labelled Vaishnava on a discovery calendar.

The material difference is deterministic date and authority:

- reviewed ordinary Kamika fixtures place the ordinary observance on
  **Thursday, 29 July 2027**;
- reviewed Indian regional fixtures place **Gauna Kamika Ekadashi** on
  **Friday, 30 July 2027**;
- those fixtures also place **Vaishnava Kamika Ekadashi** on 30 July, but the
  shared civil date does not merge the two frozen authority items; and
- a location-specific Panchang result, parana window or temple schedule is not
  portable to another city, family authority, institution or year.

The product question is therefore: “My accepted calendar specifically says
Gauna Kamika today. What can I safely do without pretending that the label
creates a universal fast, parana, Tulasi rite, lamp, vigil or temple liturgy?”

These sources establish discovery identity and a reviewed 2027 civil-date

The final pack must require live deterministic resolution from the user's
accepted calendar profile. It may show 30 July as a reviewed fixture, never as
a date to copy into another location or year. It must not publish a parana
window.

## Fixed source context: what can and cannot be reused

The reviewed N. A. Deshpande English expression of *Padma Purana*, Uttara
Khanda, chapter 54 remains the fixed named-Kamika source context:
<https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/the-padma-purana/d/doc365631.html>

It is a nested mahatmya dialogue:

1. Yudhishthira asks Krishna about Shravana Krishna Ekadashi.
2. Krishna recalls Brahma answering Narada's questions about its name, deity,
   manner and attributed merit.
3. Brahma names Kamika and praises Hari, Vishnu, Madhusudana and Krishna.
4. The chapter compares the observance with sacrifice, pilgrimage bathing and
   gifts of land, cow, gold, silver and gems.
5. It praises an overnight vigil, Tulasi leaves and sprouts, seeing, touching,
   saluting, sprinkling and planting Tulasi, and a continuous ghee or sesame-oil
   lamp.
6. It includes Yama, ancestor, caste/birth, killing-a-Brahmana and a phrase
   translated as causing abortion, and attributes cure, purity, absolution,
   heaven, salvation and Vishnu's world.

The chapter contains no developed origin story and does not identify a Gauna
calendar rule or a separate Gauna procedure. Its narrative and result language
must remain source-labelled. It cannot be used to derive the 30 July date or
to invent a next-day rite.

The preceding chapter 53 twice uses “Kamika” for a bright-half Ashadha
observance. That naming tension remains source-expression evidence only, not
Gauna timing authority.

## Meaning safe for consumer use

The most supportable bounded reflection remains: **modest Vishnu remembrance
and ordinary care need not become expensive display**. This is a Devam
synthesis from the chapter's comparisons, not a claim that every Gauna Kamika
observer shares one theology or procedure.

The Gauna distinction changes the day and the responsible calendar authority;
it does not license Devam to add a fast, vigil, lamp, Tulasi action, pilgrimage,
gift or parana instruction.

## Complete safe practice routes

### Minimum private route

- Confirm that the user's accepted authority explicitly returns Gauna Kamika,
  not ordinary or merely Vaishnava Kamika.
- Keep normal food, water, prescribed medicine, insulin, child feeding,
  hygiene and sleep.
- Use a familiar Vishnu name, family prayer, silence or no image.
- Hear the bounded chapter-54 mahatmya and say that it contains no developed
  origin story or Gauna calculation rule.
- Label all Tulasi, lamp, vigil, pilgrimage, gift, caste, reproductive, Yama,
  ancestor, cure and absolution language as source content.
- Optionally keep one private, no-purchase care commitment already within the
  person's responsibility; no plant action is required.
- Close with normal care and no guaranteed result.

### Consent-led family route

Use the same fed, rested and material-free core. Participation, the source
passage and any care commitment are optional. No one must disclose health,
pregnancy, abortion, miscarriage, fertility, caste, family history or reason
for declining. The family must not use a generic Panchang card to calculate or
impose a parana.

### Named institution route

Verify the institution's own current date, public programme, access, food,
plant, flame, photography, donation, crowd and safeguarding rules. Attend only
as a voluntary public participant. Do not copy its liturgy or timing into a
household guide. A cancelled, remote or complete home form is sufficient.

## Materials and substitutions

No material is required. Optional existing supports are a familiar Vishnu
image/text or a private reminder. Each has a no-image, no-writing and silent
substitution. Do not require or source Tulasi, leaves, plants, ghee, sesame oil,
lamps, flames, food, gifts, pilgrimage water or paid items.

## Safety and dignity boundary

- A complete route is non-fasting, fed, hydrated, rested, no-vigil,
  no-touch, no-flame, no-purchase, no-Tulasi, no-plant, no-leaf,
  no-disclosure, image-free, writing-free and material-free.
- Do not pluck, touch, ingest, apply, sprinkle, plant, buy, water or distribute
  Tulasi from this lane. Ritual is not diagnosis or treatment.
- Do not stay awake overnight or light a day-and-night lamp.
- Never ask about pregnancy, abortion, miscarriage, fertility, sexual history
  or medical records. Do not shame or obstruct lawful confidential care.
- Do not rank dignity by caste, birth, family, occupation, wealth, observance,
  disability, health or reproductive history.
- Serious killing, violence, abuse or other harm is not erased by ritual,
  hearing, Tulasi, a lamp, a vigil, fasting or regret. Preserve accountability,
  qualified care and law.
- Do not promise disease removal, bodily purification, calamity prevention,
  good birth, ancestor benefit, sin removal, absolution, heaven, salvation or
  Vishnu's world.

The ICMR *Guidelines for Management of Type 1 Diabetes Mellitus* support the
no-fasting-without-clinical-guidance boundary. The Medical Termination of
Pregnancy Act, 1971, including section 5A confidentiality, supports the
reproductive-privacy boundary. Neither source is individual medical or legal
advice.

## Major variants only

Keep minor language, ordinary prayer wording, image/no-image, existing
household support and voluntary non-participation inside this lane.

Separate routes are required for:

- ordinary Kamika on the separately resolved date;
- Vaishnava Kamika under a named sampradaya or institutional calendar, even
  when it shares the 30 July fixture;
- formal fasting, food restriction, parana, vigil, lamp or Tulasi procedure;
- clinician-directed fasting or other professional care;
- a named temple or institution's live programme; and
- another source story, chapter 53 naming interpretation or observance.
