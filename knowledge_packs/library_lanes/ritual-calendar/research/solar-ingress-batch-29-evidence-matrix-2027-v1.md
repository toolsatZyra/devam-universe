# Solar-ingress batch: frozen 29-item evidence matrix (2027)

Checked 2026-08-13. This matrix freezes the remaining accepted solar-ingress
authoring denominator after reuse of the completed Kanya, Kanni and Makara
lanes. The arithmetic is exact: **10 North/shared + 11 Malayalam + 8 Odia =
29**. Calendar identity does not by itself establish a puja.

## Shared evidence spine

- The location-pinned 2027 Sankranti calendar supplies a review fixture for the
  twelve sidereal solar ingresses. Product timing must recompute the ingress for
  the user's city, year, accepted ayanamsha/calendar and regional civil-day
  rule; a displayed date or clock is never portable.
- The reviewed Mesha page expressly documents different civil-day assignment
  rules for Odisha, Tamil, Malayalam and Bengali solar calendars. It also shows
  why Pana Sankranti, Puthandu, Vishu and Pohela Boishakh must not be collapsed
  into one national Mesha ritual.
- The Malayalam calendar maps Chingam through Karkatakam to the twelve sidereal
  signs. That supports month identity and date resolution, **not** a separate
  household action for every Sankramam.
- Valmiki Ramayana, Yuddha Kanda 6.107 (IIT Kanpur textual portal) supplies the
  reusable, source-labelled Agastya-Rama Aditya Hridaya story. It is a
  battlefield Surya account, not the origin of any item below and not authority
  to prescribe mantra, sun-gazing, enemy language or promised results.
- The complete generic consumer route is therefore deliberately modest:
  resolve applicability; keep food, water and medicine; sit indoors or in
  shade; hear the calendar meaning and labelled story; use familiar prayer,
  thanks or silence; choose an optional affordable care action; close without
  omen or result claims. No material, fast, bath, arghya, flame, donation or
  direct Sun viewing is required.

## Material Odisha routes

### Mahabisuba Pana Sankranti

The reviewed Mesha calendar identifies Odisha's Mesha transition as Pana
Sankranti. The official Odisha Tourism Development Corporation describes a
public Pana-mixing and sharing programme using fruit, milk, curd and related
ingredients. This supports an optional **participant** route: verify the local
date/organiser; disclose ingredients; use potable water and clean utensils;
avoid allergens and unsafe dairy; permit a sealed-water, familiar drink or
no-consumption substitute; never require purchase or distribution; clean the
area and refrigerate/discard perishables safely. A hanging dripping pot over
Tulsi is not made generic because the reviewed official evidence does not
establish it as a safe universal household step.

Official living-practice anchor: <https://otdc.odisha.gov.in/>

### Raja Sankranti

Odisha Tourism describes Raja as a multi-day seasonal/agricultural festival:
Pahili Raja, Raja Sankranti, Basi Raja and a fourth-day Basumati Puja context.
It associates the observance with the rains, a pause in agricultural work,
swings, pitha/pan and reflection on menstruation and womanhood. The Ministry of
Tourism Utsav page also identifies Raja with Mithuna Sankranti. This supports a
consent-led Odisha family/community participant route, not an assertion that
every woman menstruates, that menstruation makes anyone impure, or that women
must rest, avoid work or disclose private health information. Swings require a
sound installation, ordinary supervision and an easy no. Basumati Puja,
community programming and organiser food remain named-owner routes.

Official anchors:

- <https://odishatourism.gov.in/content/tourism/en/experience/themes/festivals.html>
- <https://www.utsav.gov.in/view-event/raja-mahotsav>

## Major variants only

Language, spelling, dress, familiar Surya name, ordinary food, image/no-image,
prayer/silence and harmless household expression stay within a lane. A separate
route is created only when timing authority, eligibility, officiant, story,
ordered steps, materials, food/allergen exposure, water/fire/crowd/swing risk,
or closing materially changes. State selection alone never creates a variant.

## Acceptance and status boundary

Generated packs must contain English and Hindi meaning, two labelled narrative
layers, who commonly uses the lane, deterministic timing dependency, typical
practice, minimum/standard/visitor steps, materials/substitutions, safety,
closing and material variants. Validation must reject portable dates, invented
national or state-wide puja, direct Sun viewing, compulsory fasting/bathing/
donation/mantra, real-person enemy metaphors, outcome guarantees, unsafe food or
swing instructions, menstrual impurity and template leakage.

This matrix does not count a lane as authored. Authored is not projected,
hosted, independently culturally reviewed or released. All 19,480 day cells
remain unprojected.
