# Thai Pongal Tamil household and participant lane: research dossier v1

Checked: 2026-08-13

## Applicability and material-difference decision

This lane is for Tamil Nadu and Tamil households elsewhere that recognise the four-day Pongal sequence. It is materially different from the North/West Makar Sankranti lane: the sequence has named day functions, a central overflowing Pongal preparation and offering to Surya, a cattle-gratitude day, and a visiting/social day. It therefore receives its own lane rather than a state-name variant.

The generic procedure must yield to a family's Tamil regional form, temple programme, farming context and actual access to animals. Andhra Pradesh/Telangana Bhogi, Pedda Panduga, Kanuma and Mukkanuma are not aliases for the Tamil sequence and require separate applicability research.

## 2027 Chennai fixture

- Bhogi: Thursday 14 January 2027.
- Thai/Surya Pongal: Friday 15 January 2027. Chennai solar ingress is 21:14 on 14 January; the cited daylight period is 06:35-18:02 on 15 January.
- Mattu Pongal: Saturday 16 January 2027.
- Kaanum Pongal: Sunday 17 January 2027.

Production must recompute for location and accepted Tamil calendar. Live temple, community, cattle-event and public-space schedules must be checked directly.

## Evidence map

- Tamil Nadu Cooperation Department December 2024 bulletin: official description of a four-day sequence beginning with Bhogi and including Thai Pongal, Uzhavar Thirunal and Kaanum Pongal; gratitude to animals supporting agriculture; contemporary ingredient-package example only.
- Tamil Nadu Raj Bhavan 2019 record: kolam, floral decoration, sweet Pongal cooked in earthen pots and offered to Surya; Mattu and Kaanum Pongal express connection with cattle and relatives/friends. It describes a public celebration, not a universal home vidhi.
- Ministry of Tourism Pongal page and Colours of India: Tamil Nadu harvest gratitude, the meaning of overflowing, boiling rice, Sun offering, kolam and cattle; descriptive practice evidence only.
- DrikPanchang Chennai and day pages: 2027 calendar fixture and ordered day sequence. Practitioner-calendar evidence, not sole ritual authority.
- President of India 2026 greeting: Pongal remains a distinct named agricultural festival connected with nature and farmers.
- FSSAI Food Safety at Home and milk guidance: boil unpasteurised milk, use clean water, prevent cross-contamination, serve or safely store cooked food, and disclose milk/nut allergens.
- India Code's Prevention of Cruelty to Animals Act provides the welfare boundary against unnecessary pain or suffering. As a conservative consumer-safety inference, no user without an established relationship and competent handler should approach, restrain, paint, feed or decorate cattle. This pack does not instruct Jallikattu or any animal sport.
- Tamil Nadu Pollution Control Board: Bhogi awareness specifically addresses the harmful burning of tyres, tubes, plastics, cloth and other waste.

## Action boundary

The household procedure supports cleaning without a bonfire; a safe kolam or symbolic drawing; cooking a small controlled pot of sweet or savoury Pongal; a plain-language Surya gratitude; safe sharing; non-contact gratitude to cattle/agricultural workers; and family/community visiting. It does not invent Tamil or Sanskrit verses, require outdoor fire, prescribe animal handling, promise prosperity, or make one caste/community/farming practice universal.

Bhogi disposal means lawful sorting, repair, donation and waste collection. Burning plastic, rubber, painted wood, electrical waste or mixed rubbish is never included. A venue's fire rules and air-quality restrictions prevail.
