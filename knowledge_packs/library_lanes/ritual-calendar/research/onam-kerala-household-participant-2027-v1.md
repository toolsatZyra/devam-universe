## Existing-material audit

No user-complete Onam pack exists. The source-vault catalog contains only an
institutional-acquisition lead for an Onam/temple-festival corpus; it is not
held consumer content and is not counted. The normalized inventory has one
Onam candidate, not separate labels requiring merger.

Kerala Tourism describes Onam as a harvest and homecoming festival celebrated
widely across Kerala and by Malayalis, including pookalam, sadya, games and
arts. It presents Mahabali's annual return and the Vamana encounter as the
central living legend. This is current state cultural evidence and a labelled
retelling, not fixed primary-text proof. Devam does not claim every Malayali is
Hindu, every household worships, or the festival proves a historical reign.

DrikPanchang supplies the deterministic local solar-calendar/nakshatra rule and
the 2027 Thiruvananthapuram fixture. Kerala Tourism itself warns that dates and
customs may vary by place of worship and must be confirmed locally.

## Claim and story ceiling

The consumer story may retell, explicitly as Kerala Tourism's current legend:
Mahabali's remembered just/prosperous reign; Vamana asking for three steps;
Mahabali granting the request and offering his head after two cosmic steps; and
permission to return to his people annually. It must not call Mahabali simply
evil, turn Vamana into an uncomplicated villain, assert the episode as history,
or promise prosperity. A future source owner should connect the full Vamana-
Bali scriptural context when a fixed lawful expression and canonical target
exist; this lane submits an unresolved typed proposal rather than a duplicate.

## Bounded action design

Minimum main-day form: confirm local Thiruvonam; clean one shared area; hear the
labelled four-part Mahabali-Vamana legend; make a small pookalam or reusable
welcome pattern; name one memory of fairness/togetherness; share a safe meal,
fruit or drink without insisting on a full sadya; include or assist someone;
sort food/flower waste and close. No deity image, worship, cow dung, clay form,
banana leaf, new clothes, gift, feast, Malayalam fluency, temple or ten-day
performance is required.

An optional Atham-to-Thiruvonam household approach may add a small design each
day without prescribed colour/shape claims. A full Thrikkakara Appan placement,
rice-batter kalam, offering, song or worship sequence is not inferred from
Kerala Tourism's descriptive page.

Ordinary visitors ask the host before joining a pookalam, meal, image or
prayer; disclose dietary needs; help with agreed work; avoid treating a family
festival as a costume or spectacle; and follow live event/temple rules.

## Safety and inclusion boundary

- Pookalam is optional. Do not pluck protected plants, enter private land, use
  pesticide-heavy material indoors, block exits, create slip hazards or force
  anyone to handle flowers. Reusable or drawn patterns are complete.
- Do not use cow dung, rice batter, clay figures, lamps or worship actions
  merely because a descriptive source mentions them; follow the household
  that owns the practice.
- A full sadya is optional. Check allergens, dietary/religious restrictions,
  swallowing needs, hygiene, hot liquids and storage. A small accessible meal
  is complete. No one must overeat or serve unpaid labour.
- Work is shared. Women, elders, domestic workers and hosts are not obligated
  to cook/clean while others celebrate. Pay workers and respect leave.
- Avoid disposable excess; segregate food, flowers and packaging under local
  rules. Do not dump offerings into water.
- For boat races, parades, crowds, elephants, performances and monsoon travel,
  use named live official guidance. Watching remotely or staying home is
  complete.
- Participation does not erase caste, religion, disability, gender, class or
  family conflict. Do not require public harmony, forgiveness, alcohol, gifts,
  spending, photographs or contact with unsafe relatives.
