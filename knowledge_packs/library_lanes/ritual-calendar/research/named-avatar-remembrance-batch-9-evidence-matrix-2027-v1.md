## Major variants only

Different date rules, Malayalam versus generic naming, disputed Dashavatara
lists, same-deity Dwadashi routes, formal fasts, temple abhisheka, full story,
mantra and institution programmes remain separate. Language, flower, ordinary
food, image/no image and familiar prayer/silence do not create state lanes.

Every pack provides a non-fasting, material-free remembrance plus optional
household and current-owner participant routes. No prediction, promised
protection, compulsory fast, vigil, weapon, water entry, animal contact,
purchase or donation is inferred. Authored does not mean projected, hosted,
released or published and verifies **0 of 19,480** day-answer cells.
