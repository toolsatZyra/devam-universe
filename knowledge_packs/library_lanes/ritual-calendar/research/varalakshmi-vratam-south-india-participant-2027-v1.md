## Existing-material audit

No user-complete Varalakshmi practice pack exists. Three calendar labels—Varad
Lakshmi Vrat, Varalakshmi Vrat and Varalakshmi Vratam—are already normalized to
one owner candidate. The census proposal is useful but not a consumer guide and
is not silently counted.

DrikPanchang describes the date as the last Friday of Shravana Shukla Paksha,
before Shravana Purnima, and says the observance is especially popular in
Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu and Maharashtra. It says the
vrat is available to women and men while also reporting that married women are
the principal observers in several regions. Devam therefore never states that
only married women may participate or that every woman should fast for a
husband. The current family or institution decides eligibility for its formal
rite; anyone may use the bounded reflection/ordinary participant route unless
the host says otherwise.

TTD's 2025 Tiruchanoor report supplies a named institutional example:
Vishwaksena worship, purification, kalasha installation, anga puja, Lakshmi
names, flowers and nine threads were performed under temple Agama authority.
The same report retells Shiva explaining the vrat to Parvati and Charumathi
receiving blessings. Those are TTD's current temple tradition and result
claims, not fixed primary-text proof or household DIY instructions. A separate
TTD notice proves tickets, participant limits, cancellations and timing can
change, so every temple programme must be checked live.

## Bounded participant action

Minimum complete form: resolve the local Friday; confirm this is a family
practice; choose a lawful owned Lakshmi image or a clean symbolic light/flower;
hear the explicitly TTD-labelled Charumathi association; name gratitude for
one form of household well-being; offer water/flower or no material; take one
ethical household-care action; close. No marriage, husband, child, pregnancy,
wealth, fast, kalasha, thread, Sanskrit, priest, temple or promised boon is
required.

The standard participant form may clean a small space, use an existing family
image/kalasha only under established family instructions, offer safe flowers,
fruit or food, listen to family-led katha/names without leading what one is not
authorized to lead, and share food after health checks. It must not fabricate
formal mantras, kalasha avahana, dorak knots, vayana recipients or visarjana.

An ordinary temple participant confirms date, booking, permitted attendees,
materials, dress, accessibility, photography and prasada live. They follow
staff directions and do not reproduce Agama procedures from observation.

## Safety and claim ceiling

- Fasting is optional in this participant lane. Medication, hydration,
  pregnancy, diabetes, illness, age and clinical advice override custom.
- Lamp, incense and camphor are optional; use LED/no flame around children,
  oxygen, loose hair or clothing, pets, crowds or fire restrictions.
- A kalasha is stable, light enough to handle and never placed where a child or
  animal can pull it down. Household water is not automatically safe to drink.
- Flowers and food do not go to a river or drain. Follow local waste rules.
- Check allergens, hygiene, dietary needs and food storage.
- Never borrow, buy gold, transfer property, pay unofficial links, share OTP,
  or expect wealth, marriage, fertility, a child's sex, health, longevity,
  employment, exam success or family harmony in return.
- A married woman is not responsible for a husband's health or lifespan; an
  unmarried, widowed, divorced, queer, childless or non-participating person is
  not deficient or inauspicious.
