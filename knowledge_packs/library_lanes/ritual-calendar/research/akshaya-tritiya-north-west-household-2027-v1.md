# Akshaya Tritiya North/West household lane research dossier

## Selected denominator

- Inventory item: `akshaya-tritiya-north-west-household-v1`.
- Proposed authored lane: `akshaya-tritiya-north-west-household-participant-2027-v1`.
- Boundary: a North/West household, individual, family-led and ordinary temple-
  participant answer for Vaishakha Shukla Tritiya. It does not prescribe a
  purchase, marriage, ancestor rite, priest-led Lakshminarayana puja, Char Dham
  opening, Odisha chariot work, Jain fast-breaking, or Parashurama Jayanti.
- 2027 Delhi fixture: Sunday 9 May; Tritiya begins 11:42 on 8 May and ends
  09:03 on 9 May. Delhi's published household window is 05:35-09:03 on 9 May.
  All clock times are local and non-portable.

## Existing-material and alias audit

No existing user-complete Akshaya Tritiya pack was found. The annual alias
inventory already routes Akha Trij, Akshaya Thiruthiyai, Akshaya Trithiya and
Akshaya Trutiya to the same candidate because spelling alone does not change
timing, eligibility or action. The denominator calls the candidate multi-
regional; this first applicability lane is explicitly North/West and does not
silently count other regional institutional forms.

The canonical registry already owns
`devam:source-expression:mahabharata-ganguli-consumer-v1`. The retained Ganguli
Mahabharata object has SHA-256
`246325dcb8966a13990ab66f38b1cab230724fe0b1ad135bd6fb12222baa4826`.
Vana Parva Section III recounts Yudhishthira seeking food for accompanying
guests, worshipping Surya, receiving a copper vessel whose food remains
inexhaustible until Draupadi eats, and feeding guests and brothers before the
household's final meals. The passage itself does **not** date the event to
Akshaya Tritiya. A 2020 Prime Minister's public address reports the modern
belief connecting the day and Akshaya Patra. Consumer copy must keep the fixed
epic account and the later festival association as separate evidence layers.

The source also assigns Draupadi the kitchen role and final meal. That gendered
arrangement is preserved as source fact and never converted into a rule that a
woman must cook, serve everyone or eat last.

## Bounded consumer answer

Minimum complete form: confirm local Vaishakha Shukla Tritiya; hear the labelled
Akshaya Patra account and the modern day-association distinction; offer a
family-known prayer or silence; make one feasible act of food-sharing, charity,
repair or service without debt or public performance; close with thanks and
proof of completion. Standard form may add a clean image/book, flower, water,
safe food and a budgeted donation. Temple participation requires live rules.

Gold, vehicle, property, business, wedding, Ganga bath, fast, Sanskrit, diya,
havan and ancestor rite are not completeness requirements. “Akshaya” is not a
financial-return guarantee. Charity must not expose a recipient's identity or
dignity, and no one is pressured to receive food, pose for images or disclose
need.

## Safety and claim ceiling

- Never borrow, overspend, rush a contract or buy gold to make the observance
  complete. If an adult independently buys jewellery, verify BIS hallmark,
  purity and six-character HUID in the official BIS Care app and retain a bill.
- No guaranteed prosperity, appreciation, inexhaustible wealth, health, merit,
  marriage success or venture success.
- Child marriage is prohibited. The Government of India's Child Helpline 1098
  is 24x7 and includes child-marriage cases. Festival auspiciousness does not
  override age law or free consent.
- Fasting is optional; prescribed medicine, water and food take priority.
- Food-sharing uses FSSAI hygiene and allergen boundaries. Direct aid is based
  on the recipient's stated needs; no photography or publicity is required.
- No DIY tarpan, havan, sacred-water immersion or temple deity service is
  inferred. River and pilgrimage attendance requires authorised areas, live
  weather/access checks and institution control.
