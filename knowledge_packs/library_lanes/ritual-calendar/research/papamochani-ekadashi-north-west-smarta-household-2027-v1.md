## Fixed source-labelled account

The reviewed N. A. Deshpande English expression of *Padma Purana*, Uttara
Khanda chapter 46, lines 54-101, is a Yudhishthira-Krishna narration of
Mandhatri and Lomasa's dialogue. In Chaitraratha forest, the celestial musician
Manjughosha seeks to attract the celibate sage Medhavin. The chapter uses an
extended sexualised battle metaphor for her body and Cupid. It says she and
Medhavin rejoice together and lose awareness of time. When she seeks permission
to leave, she fears his curse and continues for years. After learning how much
time passed, Medhavin blames and curses her into a form the translation calls a
female goblin. She asks him to soften it; he names Papamochani. Medhavin then
goes to his father Cyavana, who holds him responsible too and prescribes the
same observance. The chapter says both observe it, her form is restored, his
merit returns and serious wrongs can be removed.

Consumer treatment must not flatten these tensions:

- The body-as-weapon inventory is objectifying and omitted from ordinary copy.
- Attraction, consensual affection, sexuality, music and a woman's body are not
  sin or pollution in Devam guidance.
- The source describes Manjughosha initially seeking to influence Medhavin, but
  later explicitly fearing his curse when asking to leave. Consent is ongoing;
  fear of punishment undermines free choice. Do not turn either person into a
  simple modern victim or villain beyond what the text supports.
- Medhavin's loss of time and regret do not justify misogynistic blame,
  dehumanising labels, curse, intimidation or punishment. Cyavana's rebuke is a
  source counterweight that retains Medhavin's responsibility.
- Goblin form and curse release are theological narrative claims, not diagnoses
  of possession, mental illness, disability, trauma, sexuality or behaviour.
- The chapter's expiation claim cannot erase criminal, civil, workplace,
  relationship or safeguarding accountability; ritual does not cancel survivor
  needs, restitution, reporting, professional care or law.

## Bounded present-day meaning

A safe ordinary meaning is **accountability without dehumanisation**. Name one
harmful action or pattern in one's own conduct, distinguish responsibility from
self-hatred, identify who may be affected, and choose one proportionate repair
that does not demand contact, forgiveness or reconciliation. A useful step may
be stopping the behaviour, documenting it, apologising without excuses or a
reply demand, replacing a loss, seeking qualified treatment/advice, using a
grievance route, or respecting permanent no-contact.

Do not ask participants to confess publicly or disclose sexual, relationship,
trauma, mental-health or legal details. Do not make a survivor facilitate the
other person's absolution. If contact is unsafe, prohibited or unwanted, repair
must occur without contact and may consist of respecting boundaries and lawful
process.

## Safe ordinary household route

- Confirm the local date and authority.
- Choose normal food, water, medicine and sleep; a non-fasting form is complete.
- Remember Vishnu with a familiar name, prayer or silence.
- Hear a bounded account explicitly labelled Padma Purana chapter 46, omitting
  the sexualised body inventory and labelling curse, goblin form, ritual
  expiation and promised results as source claims.
- Privately name one action within one's own responsibility—not another
  person's supposed impurity, possession or sin.
- Choose one consent-safe, lawful repair or prevention step.
- Close with gratitude; preserve evidence, boundaries, qualified care and law.

No material is required. Optional: an existing Vishnu image/text, water/fruit,
private paper or a secure device. Silence, no image, no writing and no purchase
are complete. Destroy or securely store sensitive notes; never leave a written
confession where it exposes a participant or another person.

## Safety and safeguarding

- A non-fasting form is complete. Children, pregnant or breastfeeding people,
  older adults, people with diabetes or another condition, people taking
  medicines with food, people with eating-disorder history and people doing
  heat/heavy labour must not begin or intensify fasting from Devam. Never change
  insulin, medicine, meals or hydration.
- No vigil, deprivation, private meeting, touch, dance, music or relationship
  discussion is required. Stop before fatigue.
- Participation, story detail, writing, disclosure, apology and contact require
  consent. Children use an age-appropriate non-sexual summary with a responsible
  guardian. Ritual authority never creates private access to a child or
  vulnerable person.
- A story cannot diagnose possession, curse, mental illness, disability, trauma
  or unusual behaviour. National mental-health training materials reject evil-
  spirit and curse explanations for mental disorders. Do not restrain, beat,
  isolate, fast, stop medication or perform exorcism; seek qualified health or
  emergency help for distress or danger.
- Do not describe a woman, sexual person, survivor, accused person or anyone
  with illness/disability as a goblin, impure, temptress, fallen or sin-bearing.
- An apology is not owed access, reply, secrecy, forgiveness or reconciliation.
  Do not contact someone against their wishes, a safety plan, workplace rule or
  legal order. Never advise a survivor to return to an unsafe relationship.
- Serious harm, abuse, coercion, assault, exploitation, theft or professional
  misconduct is not erased by fasting, prayer or private regret. Preserve
  immediate safety, evidence, reporting options, restitution and legal duties.
- No sin removal, curse release, restored merit, heaven, purity, acquittal,
  forgiveness, relationship repair or other result is guaranteed.

## Major-variant decisions

- **Within lane:** language; image/no image; water/fruit/no offering; private
  writing/no writing; family-known prayer/silence; individual/family; non-fast
  or already inherited clinically safe fast.
- **Separate authority:** a formal expiation, confession, priest-led rite,
  restitution process, mantra count or parana rule.
- **Separate safety/authority:** supernatural diagnosis, curse removal,
  possession or exorcism claim. Devam does not supply such a route.
- **Separate institution:** a named temple's live programme, priest-led liturgy,
  access, safeguarding, fees or offerings.
- **Separate professional route:** legal, medical, mental-health, workplace,
  safeguarding or survivor support. Ritual may accompany but never replace it.
- **Separate named observance:** Kamada, Varuthini or another Ekadashi.
