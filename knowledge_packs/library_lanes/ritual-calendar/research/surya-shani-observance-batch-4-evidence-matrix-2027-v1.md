## Source-labelled stories and meaning

- Drik Panchang presents Ratha Saptami as Surya Jayanti and as the day Surya is believed to have begun illuminating the world. This is a current attributed observance narrative, not a demonstrated universal scriptural origin.
- The public-domain 1840 H. H. Wilson translation of *Vishnu Purana* III.2 names Sanjna as Surya's wife and Shanaishchara (Saturn/Shani) among Surya and Chhaya's children. It grounds that genealogy only; it does not establish the dates or a modern puja manual.
- Bhanu Saptami evidence establishes a recurring Surya-associated Sunday–Saptami calendar identity. No single fixed origin story was established in the reviewed sources; the pack must say so.
- Shani Trayodashi evidence establishes the Saturday Trayodashi label and frequent Shani-Pradosh overlap. The reviewed Shani-Pradosh page labels a Dharmagupta–Anshumati vrata story; it remains a current practitioner-calendar story label, not a universal origin or a generic household command.

## Living-practice boundary

Current practitioner sources describe bathing, Surya arghya, prayer, lamps/incense/red flowers, fasting and charity for Surya observances; and fasting, temple visits, oil offering/abhisheka, homa and Shani-shanti services for Shani observances. These are descriptions of current routes. A complete accessible home answer may instead use a clean place, a spoken or silent remembrance, ordinary water kept safely in a vessel, a source-labelled story, reflection, service or charity within one's means, and an orderly close. Formal mantra, vrata, homa, abhisheka, astrology/remedy, temple schedule and priestly work stay with the named family, sampradaya, temple or professional owner.

## Safety and claims

- Never instruct a user to stare at the Sun. Arghya can be offered without direct solar viewing; low vision, indoor practice and no-object practice are complete substitutions.
- No river entry, rooftop exposure, unsafe predawn travel, smoke, open flame, oil pouring, heavy lifting or crowded temple attendance is required.
- Keep normal hydration, food, prescribed medicine, infant/child feeding, pregnancy/lactation nutrition, sleep and disability access. Fasting is never the default.
- Health, longevity, sin removal, prosperity, luck, justice, horoscope change, dosha reduction, Sade Sati relief and protection are attributable beliefs or service claims, never consumer guarantees.
- No source payload is copied into Git. Citations and coordinates remain internal; consumer prose leads with meaning and usable guidance.
