## Fixed source-labelled account

The reviewed N. A. Deshpande English expression of *Padma Purana*, Uttara
Khanda chapter 51, lines 54-97, is framed by Yudhishthira asking Krishna about
Jyeshtha Shukla Ekadashi. Krishna directs him to Vyasa. Vyasa speaks of the
usual Ekadashi rule, and Bhima answers that Yudhishthira, Kunti, Draupadi,
Arjuna, Nakula and Sahadeva fast and urge him to do likewise, but that he cannot
bear hunger because of the fire in his belly called Vrka. He asks for one
observance he can complete.

Vyasa prescribes a stringent rule: no food or water from one sunrise to the
next, saying even a sip breaks the vow. The chapter gives Dwadashi bathing,
water and other gifts, Brahmana feeding, a vigil, formulas and later eating. It
attributes to this one observance the fruit of all yearly Ekadashis and promises
wealth, grain, sons, health, freedom from Yama's messengers and arrival in
Vishnu's city. It threatens hell and disparages people who eat or do not fast,
including caste-denigrating language. It says Bhima observed the vow and calls
the following Dwadashi Pandava Dwadashi.

## Consumer treatment of source tensions

- “Nirjala” is not a decorative title: chapter 51 defines a waterless fast.
  That source-defined practice is **not** an ordinary household instruction.
  A complete non-fasting, fed and hydrated form must be presented first.
- Bhima plainly says he cannot bear hunger while relatives press him to fast.
  Consumer treatment should honour honest bodily limits; it must not turn his
  eventual observance, strength, appetite or family identity into a test of
  devotion, masculinity, discipline or willpower.
- “One fast equals all Ekadashis,” heaven, family salvation, wealth, grain,
  sons, good health, freedom from Yama and Vishnu's city are attributed source
  claims, not medical, reproductive, spiritual or material guarantees.
- Hell threats, non-observer shaming and caste-denigrating words are historical
  source language, not Devam's voice. Do not quote them in ordinary guidance or
  rank dignity by caste, fasting, gender, health, family or observance.
- The water, gold, cow, ghee, food, garment, bed, seat, pitcher, umbrella and
  shoe gifts, Brahmana feeding, formulas, bathing and vigil are source actions,
  not reconstructed requirements. Do not solicit purchases or recipients.
- “Pandava Dwadashi” is a source name for the following Dwadashi, not a second
  frozen candidate in the 2027 authoring denominator. Retain it as a source
  alias/context only unless future timing and practice evidence establishes a
  materially distinct applicability lane.
- Gayatri Jayanti or another same-day observance remains separate. Shared civil
  date does not merge stories, authority or procedures.

## Bounded present-day meaning

A safe ordinary meaning is **honour bodily limits and make water care easier,
without spiritual comparison**. A user may check that drinking water is
available for themself and consenting household members, fill an existing
bottle, move an ordinary task out of peak heat, share a current official heat
advisory, or support access through an already trusted no-pressure route.

This is not a pledge to fast, a hydration challenge, a public display or a
claim to rescue someone. Do not photograph recipients, demand gratitude,
distribute unverified liquids or create obligation. Where fluid intake is
clinically restricted, follow the treating clinician rather than generic
hydration advice.

## Safe ordinary household route

- Confirm the local date and authority, then check current heat conditions.
- Eat, drink, take medicines, use insulin, feed infants, rest and sleep normally;
  the non-fasting form is complete.
- Remember Vishnu with a familiar name, prayer or silence.
- Hear a bounded Bhima-Vyasa account, explicitly labelling the waterless rule,
  threats, caste language, gifts and promised results as source content rather
  than general instructions.
- Choose one consent-based water or heat-safety action already within one's
  means and responsibility, or simply keep normal drinking water accessible.
- Close with gratitude and continued normal care, without claiming equivalence
  to all Ekadashis, health, sons, wealth, family salvation, heaven or purity.

No material is required. Optional: existing drinking water for oneself, an
existing bottle, an existing Vishnu image/text or a current official heat
advisory. Silence, no image, no writing, no purchase, no donation and no contact
are complete.

## Safety and safeguarding

- Do not begin, intensify, supervise or promote a waterless fast. Never withhold
  water, food, breastmilk or infant feeding, prescribed medicine, insulin, ORS
  or medically needed intake. Do not alter a fluid restriction without the
  treating clinician.
- Children, older adults, pregnant or breastfeeding people, outdoor/manual
  workers, people with diabetes, kidney or heart conditions, fever, vomiting,
  diarrhoea, eating-disorder risk or other illness must not be pressured. This
  list is not an eligibility test; ordinary safe care applies to everyone.
- NCDC advises regular water/electrolyte-supporting liquids in heat, not waiting
  for thirst in older adults, checking medication interactions and avoiding
  excessive heat exertion. Individual clinical restrictions override generic
  advice. Seek qualified care for symptoms or uncertainty.
- Dizziness, headache, nausea, vomiting, marked weakness, confusion, fainting,
  seizure or other heat-illness signs are not spiritual tests. Stop the ritual
  context, move to safety and use current emergency/medical guidance.
- No person should monitor another's drinking, food, urine, weight or medicine,
  demand proof of fasting, shame a broken fast, compare merit, or use Bhima's
  body, appetite or strength to coerce participation.
- Do not use caste slurs, hell threats, fear for ancestors or descendants,
  promised sons, family pressure, temple pressure or claims of lost merit.
- Do not reconstruct a vigil, strict fast, parana, bath, mantra, feeding or gift
  sequence from this record. Those require separately verified inherited,
  institutional and clinical authority.
- No health, fertility, wealth, grain, salvation, heaven, purity, family rescue
  or equivalence to all yearly Ekadashis is guaranteed.

## Major-variant decisions

- **Within lane:** language; image/no image; prayer/silence; individual/family;
  existing bottle/no object; private heat-safety action/no action. These minor
  differences do not create state lanes.
- **Separate fasting authority:** the source-defined nirjala fast, altered food
  or fluid intake, vigil and parana. It requires inherited/sampradaya authority,
  current local timing and qualified clinical advice and is not supplied here.
- **Separate timing/authority:** Gauna or Vaishnava civil date and parana rules.
- **Separate institution:** a named temple's current programme, priest-led
  rite, access, safeguarding, fees, offerings or water-distribution programme.
- **Separate professional route:** heat illness, dehydration, diabetes,
  pregnancy, lactation, eating disorder, kidney/heart disease, medicine or
  emergency care. Ritual may accompany but never replace it.
- **Separate named observance:** Gayatri Jayanti, Yogini or another Ekadashi.
