## Major variants only

Different tithi/day assignment, multi-day duration, public procession, idol
lifecycle, formal vrata story, moon arghya, sacred thread, inherited food,
spouse-specific prayer, swing event and organiser programme are material and
remain with their owners. Clothing colour, ordinary food, flower choice,
mehndi/no mehndi, image/no image, language, familiar prayer or silence do not
create separate state lanes.

## Dignity and safe complete route

No woman, girl, wife, unmarried person or spouse is obligated to participate,
fast, seek marriage, preserve a harmful relationship, prove devotion, disclose
status, wear a thread, accept touch, use a swing, view the moon, buy gifts or
pray for another person's longevity. Men and people of any gender may use the
non-appropriative gratitude/reflection route. Normal food, water, medicine,
insulin, sleep, consent, allergen control and safety prevail. Savitri's source
story never instructs anyone to tolerate abuse or makes a spouse responsible
for another person's life or death.

Each pack contains English/Hindi meaning, two source-labelled narratives,
applicability, typical practice, three ordered procedure tiers, optional
materials and substitutions, closing, safety, major variants and unresolved
owner proposals. Authored coverage does not mean projected, hosted, released
or published and verifies **0 of 19,480** day-answer cells.
