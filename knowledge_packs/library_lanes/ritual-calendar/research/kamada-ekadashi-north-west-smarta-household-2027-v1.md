## Fixed source-labelled account

The reviewed N. A. Deshpande English expression of *Padma Purana*, Uttara
Khanda chapter 47, lines 54-83, places the account inside a
Yudhishthira-Krishna narration of Vasistha's answer to King Dilipa. In Nagapura,
the gandharva Lalita and the celestial nymph Lalita are described as a loving,
attached couple. While the husband sings and dances before King Pundarika
without his wife present, remembering her makes his words stumble. Karkotaka
reports the error. The angry king curses Lalita into a flesh-eating demon form.
The chapter gives an extended grotesque inventory of that form and says he
wanders in a forest while his wife follows in grief.

At a hermitage, Lalita asks a tranquil sage how her husband can be freed. The
sage names Kamada Ekadashi and instructs her to observe its vow and give the
resulting merit to her husband. The text says she fasts, dedicates the merit in
front of Vishnu and a brahmana, and her husband is immediately restored to
gandharva form. The couple rejoices together; the chapter attributes removal of
sin and religious merit to observance, recitation and listening.

Consumer treatment must not flatten these tensions:

- Remembering a loved one, attraction, marital affection and a performance
  lapse are not sin, pollution or justification for humiliation or punishment.
- Pundarika's curse and the story's dehumanising form are source facts and a
  disproportionate exercise of royal power, not a model for discipline.
- The grotesque body inventory and claims of eating human flesh are omitted
  from ordinary consumer copy. Illness, disability, appearance, distress or a
  mistake must never be described as demonic or less than human.
- Lalita's decision to follow, seek help, fast and dedicate merit is her action
  in the source. It is not a universal duty of wives, spouses, women or carers.
  No one must fast, surrender safety, remain in an unsafe relationship, assume
  another adult's responsibility or make contact against their wishes.
- Curse, demon form, merit transfer and immediate restoration are theological
  narrative claims, not diagnoses, mechanisms or guaranteed outcomes.
- The title's desire-fulfilling sense cannot become a promise of a particular
  person, marriage, pregnancy, cure, wealth, job, examination result,
  immigration result, legal outcome or any other desired result.

## Bounded present-day meaning

A safe ordinary meaning is **support without self-erasure, and proportionate
response to mistakes**. A participant may remember someone facing an ordinary
difficulty and choose one bounded form of help: ask what would actually help,
share a meal or practical task, pass on accurate information, accompany them to
qualified support if invited, or simply respect their space. A person reflecting
on their own mistake may acknowledge it and choose one proportionate correction
without accepting dehumanisation or abuse.

Help must be voluntary, wanted and within capacity. It does not require a
spouse, woman or carer to rescue another adult, repair an unsafe relationship,
hide misconduct, pay money, fast, disclose private facts, or promise an outcome.
Emergency, medical, mental-health, legal, safeguarding and workplace needs stay
with qualified routes.

## Safe ordinary household route

- Confirm the local date and authority.
- Choose normal food, water, medicine and sleep; a non-fasting form is complete.
- Remember Vishnu with a familiar name, prayer or silence.
- Hear a bounded account explicitly labelled Padma Purana chapter 47, omitting
  the grotesque body inventory and labelling curse, merit transfer, restoration
  and promised results as source claims.
- Choose either one proportionate correction for one's own mistake or one
  consent-safe offer of help that the other person may refuse.
- Close with gratitude and no demand for merit, rescue, reconciliation or a
  desired result.

No material is required. Optional: an existing Vishnu image/text, water or
fruit, and a private reminder. Silence, no image, no writing and no purchase are
complete. Do not record another person's private difficulty without consent.

## Safety and safeguarding

- A non-fasting form is complete. Children, pregnant or breastfeeding people,
  older adults, people with diabetes or another condition, people taking
  medicines with food, people with eating-disorder history and people doing
  heat/heavy labour must not begin or intensify fasting from Devam. Never change
  insulin, medicine, meals or hydration.
- No vigil, deprivation, private meeting, touch, payment, travel, relationship
  discussion or merit-dedication formula is required. Stop before fatigue.
- Support requires consent and can be refused or withdrawn. No spouse, woman,
  child or carer owes rescue, fasting, secrecy, money, access, forgiveness,
  reconciliation or continued contact. Never tell someone to stay with or
  return to an unsafe partner.
- A mistake does not justify humiliation, threats, curse language, workplace
  retaliation, violence or dehumanisation. Accountability and proportionate
  correction remain possible without abuse.
- A story cannot diagnose possession, curse, mental illness, disability, trauma
  or unusual behaviour. National mental-health training materials reject evil-
  spirit and curse explanations for mental disorders. Do not restrain, beat,
  isolate, fast, stop medication or perform exorcism; seek qualified health or
  emergency help for distress or danger.
- Do not use demon imagery for disability, illness, appearance, appetite,
  addiction, anger, trauma, a performance error or another human condition.
- Do not hide serious harm, abuse, coercion, exploitation, theft or professional
  misconduct as loyalty or spiritual support. Preserve safety, evidence,
  reporting options, restitution, treatment and legal duties.
- No wish, chosen partner, marriage, pregnancy, cure, freedom from a curse,
  restored body/status, forgiveness, relationship repair, wealth, success or
  other result is guaranteed.

## Major-variant decisions

- **Within lane:** language; image/no image; water/fruit/no offering; private
  reminder/no writing; familiar prayer/silence; individual/family; non-fast or
  already inherited clinically safe fast. These minor differences do not create
  state lanes.
- **Separate authority:** a formal sankalpa, merit-dedication formula, priest-led
  rite, mantra count, food rule or parana rule.
- **Separate safety/authority:** supernatural diagnosis, curse removal,
  possession or exorcism claim. Devam does not supply such a route.
- **Separate institution:** a named temple's live programme, priest-led liturgy,
  access, safeguarding, fees or offerings.
- **Separate professional route:** medical, mental-health, legal, workplace,
  safeguarding or survivor support. Ritual may accompany but never replace it.
- **Separate named observance:** Papamochani, Varuthini or another Ekadashi.

No major state-specific difference was evidenced for this ordinary household
lane across the reviewed North/West, Maharashtra and Andhra Pradesh calendar
fixtures. Other listed states should inherit this ordinary lane only when their
accepted authority resolves the same observance and no materially different
practice is evidenced; minor language, food and family expression are not
separate variants.
