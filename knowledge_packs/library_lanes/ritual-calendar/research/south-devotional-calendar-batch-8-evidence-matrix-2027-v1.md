## Shared sources and evidence roles

- Drik Panchang 2027 Chennai or named-location pages supply discovery fixtures
  and calendar identities only. Every date, nakshatra/tithi assignment and
  displayed interval must be recomputed for the user's city and tradition.
- Ministry of Tourism Utsav pages for Palani Thaipusam, Palani Panguni Uthiram
  and Tiruvannamalai Karthigai Deepam supply current official descriptions of
  living institutional practice. Their old event schedules are not copied.
- The Ramanuja practitioner page supplies the 1017-1137 identification and
  Chithirai/Thiruvathirai rule; a full biography needs a named historical or
  Sri Vaishnava source owner.
- Calendar descriptions of Murugan/Parvati/Vel, Murugan birth, Andal, and
  Soorapadman are labelled living narratives, not universal historical facts.
  Full scripture, hymn, theology and sampradaya claims are cross-linked rather
  than reconstructed from model memory.

## Major variants only

Separate calendar rules, temple programmes, kavadi or piercing disciplines,
milk abhisheka, water entry, long fasts, public combat drama, large procession,
Maha Deepam, lineage teaching and copyrighted/full hymn text are material and
remain with current owners. Language, spelling, ordinary food, flower choice,
image/no image, a familiar prayer or silence, and one supervised household lamp
do not create separate state lanes.

## Safe complete consumer route

Each answer asks who/where/calendar first; keeps normal food, water, medicine,
insulin, sleep and accessibility; tells a source-labelled bounded story; offers
a five-minute material-free remembrance; provides an optional safe household
form; and routes formal participation to the current temple, organiser, family
or lineage. No consumer must fast, pierce skin, carry kavadi or milk, enter
water, light a hill/fire structure, handle weapons, buy materials, join a
crowd, or disclose private information.

Completion is bilingual authored-library coverage only. It does not count a
lane as projected, hosted, independently culturally reviewed, released or
published, and it verifies **0 of 19,480** day-answer cells.
