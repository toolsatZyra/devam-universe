## Bounded action design

Minimum complete form: resolve local Ashadha Purnima; hear four labelled
knowledge-transmission/gratitude moments; name one freely chosen teacher or
mentor (including a parent, book/source tradition, or no named living person);
write one specific thing learned; offer private thanks, a message only when
welcome, or silence; practise the learning through one small action; close.
No living guru, image, feet-touching, bow, Sanskrit, money, gift, fast, temple,
public praise or reply is required.

Standard family/educational form may use an owned Vyasa or teacher image/book,
flower, water, card, safe shared food, a voluntary pre-budgeted gift or service,
and a familiar prayer. A recipient can decline contact, gift, touch, image,
public naming or participation. Children never meet a teacher privately or
send personal data/payment outside guardian and institution rules.

Ordinary institution participation confirms the named programme live and joins
only open visitor actions. It never copies diksha, mantra, paduka worship,
feet-washing/touching, private audience, money collection, residential stay or
teacher service from observation.

## Safety, consent and authority ceiling

- Gratitude is voluntary. It does not require obedience, secrecy, isolation,
  unpaid labour, political/religious allegiance, recruitment, touch, feet-
  touching, sexual access, marriage, reproduction, fasting, money, property,
  passwords, OTP, personal documents, medical decisions or withdrawal of a
  complaint.
- A title, robe, lineage, celebrity, age, scholarship or spiritual claim does
  not remove ordinary consent, law, safeguarding, financial diligence,
  institutional rules or the right to leave.
- No child privately contacts, travels with, meets, stays with, pays or sends
  images/data to a teacher or spiritual leader outside guardian and institution
  safeguards. Suspected abuse uses the relevant safeguarding/emergency route;
  gratitude never requires silence.
- A student may use institutional grievance/ICC/anti-discrimination processes.
  Reverence does not waive protection against harassment, retaliation or
  discrimination.
- Gifts and donations are optional, affordable and unpressured. Never borrow,
  transfer property, share PIN/OTP, use an unofficial payment demand or expect
  grades, initiation, access, healing, favour, merit or blessing in return.
- Fasting is optional and non-fasting is complete. Required food, water and
  medicine override custom.
- A teacher may be remembered without contact; a deceased, estranged, unsafe or
  harmful teacher need not be praised, forgiven, contacted or publicly named.
  Learning can be acknowledged while harm is named and boundaries retained.
