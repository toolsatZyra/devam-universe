# Annual alias and material-context audit 2027

Checked on 2026-08-13 against the ten source calendars in the v3 census.

This batch normalizes only high-confidence name clusters. Orthographic forms of
Akshaya Tritiya, Rama Navami, Narasimha Jayanti, Parashurama Jayanti, Vamana
Jayanti, Shankara Jayanti, Ratha Yatra, Ratha Saptami, Raksha Bandhan and Ugadi
remain searchable aliases rather than duplicate state lanes.

Material timing contexts remain distinct: South Rohini Janmashtami is not
collapsed into the North reference; Tamil Hanumath Jayanthi is not collapsed
into the North Hanuman date; Nagula Chavithi is not renamed Nag Panchami; and
Masik Krishna Janmashtami remains recurring rather than annual. Holi colour-day
aliases share a consent-led candidate, while Holika Dahan remains a separate
fire observance. The existing Mahashivaratri lane is reused for the annual
spelling labels.

Calendar co-occurrence does not supply stories or instructions. Direct review
found Parashurama Dwadashi on 2027-05-17 rather than the 2027-05-08 Jayanti,
and Vamana Dwadashi on 2027-04-17 rather than the 2027-09-12 Jayanti. Both are
therefore separate candidates. No fasting, parana, fire, colour, animal, food
or temple instruction is inferred here.
