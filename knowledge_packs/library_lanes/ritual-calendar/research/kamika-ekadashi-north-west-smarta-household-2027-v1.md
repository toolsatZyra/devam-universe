## Fixed source-labelled content and absence of a developed story

The reviewed N. A. Deshpande English expression of *Padma Purana*, Uttara
Khanda chapter 54, lines 54-76, is a nested mahatmya dialogue. Yudhishthira asks
Krishna about Shravana Krishna Ekadashi. Krishna says Brahma formerly answered
Narada's questions about its name, deity, manner and merit. Brahma names Kamika
and praises worship or meditation on Hari/Vishnu/Madhusudana/Krishna.

There is no developed origin story with a character sequence beyond this
dialogue. The chapter compares Kamika's attributed result with Vajapeya and
horse sacrifices; bathing at Ganga, Kashi, Naimisha, Pushkara, Godavari and
Dandaka; giving the earth, a milk cow, gold, silver, gems and other wealth; and
knowledge of the supreme spirit. It prescribes effortful observance, a night
vigil and Vishnu worship.

The chapter particularly praises Tulasi leaves and sprouts. It says seeing,
touching, saluting, sprinkling, planting and placing Tulasi at Vishnu's feet
destroy sin, purify, remove disease, frighten Yama and give salvation. It then
praises a lamp kept day and night, including ghee or sesame oil, and attributes
heavenly benefit to ancestors. It uses Yama fear, caste/birth-ranking language,
and says Kamika removes the sin of killing a Brahmana and “causing abortion.”
It promises Vishnu's abode, salvation, sin removal and great merit.
