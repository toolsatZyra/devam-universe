## Fixed source-labelled account

The reviewed N. A. Deshpande English expression of *Padma Purana*, Uttara
Khanda chapter 45, lines 56-98, is a nested Krishna-Yudhishthira,
Vasistha-Mandhatri and Vishnu-sages account. It calls the amalaki/dhatri tree
dear to Vishnu and narrates its emergence from a luminous drop associated with
Brahma after cosmic inundation. The heavenly voice identifies Vishnu and places
deities throughout the tree. These are source theological claims, not botany,
history or medical evidence.

The chapter then gives a formal and explicitly hard-to-practise rite: fasting,
avoiding categories of people whom the translation labels fallen, thieves,
heretics or immoral; bathing with clay; obtaining a small golden Parashurama
image; a flawless jar with five gems, umbrella, sandals, fragrance, garland,
incense, many lamps, parched grain and body-part invocations; all-night song,
music, stories and repeated circumambulation; morning light, gifts, feeding
Brahmanas and eating with one's fraternity. It promises Vishnu's world, removal
of sin, wealth, grain, heaven, salvation, merit and other results.

Consumer treatment:

- Do not convert a creation theology into a scientific origin claim.
- Do not use ritual to label, shun or deny ordinary safety, food, care, service
  or dignity to someone based on caste, sect, belief, occupation, alleged moral
  status or the chapter's social categories.
- Do not universalise the gold image, gems, many lamps, clay bath, fasting,
  vigil, repeated circumambulation, recipient hierarchy or fraternity meal.
- Do not add the popular hunter/King Vasuratha story unless a fixed reviewed
  expression is separately acquired and labelled. It is not present in the
  reviewed chapter 45 expression used here.
- Do not promise a cure, immunity, longevity, sin removal, wealth, salvation,
  heaven or any other result from amla, a tree visit, fasting or ritual.

## Bounded present-day meaning

A safe ordinary meaning is **reverence expressed as non-harming care**. Remember
Vishnu, hear the source-labelled amalaki account, notice one living system that
supports daily life, and choose one lawful, requested care action: water an
owned plant appropriately, remove litter safely with permission, avoid damaging
a tree, support a verified planting/care effort, or reduce one wasteful habit.
Do not claim that a symbolic act offsets environmental harm or replaces expert
horticultural, medical or civic guidance.

## Safe ordinary household route

- Confirm the local date and authority.
- Choose normal food, water, medicine and sleep; the non-fasting/no-vigil form
  is complete.
- Remember Vishnu with a familiar name, prayer or silence.
- Hear a bounded account explicitly labelled Padma Purana chapter 45. State
  that the tree theology, formal rite, social exclusions and results are source
  claims, not universal instructions.
- Choose one non-harming, permission-safe care action within your control.
- Close with gratitude and complete only that action.

No material is required. Optional: an existing amla fruit, a lawful image of an
amla tree, an existing Vishnu image/text, a clean reusable vessel, a flower or a
written action. No purchase, tree visit, fruit consumption, sapling, gold image,
gem, lamp, incense, grain, umbrella, sandals or gift is required.

## Tree, food, fire and health safety

- Do not enter private, protected, roadside or hazardous land without
  permission. Do not climb, cut, break, prune, scrape, nail, paint, bind tight
  thread/wire, hang weight from, dig around, light flame/incense near, pour
  unknown liquids on, leave food/plastic at, or crowd an amla or any tree.
- Circumambulation is optional. Do it only on a stable, accessible, permitted
  path without roots, traffic, crowd or fall risk; one respectful pause or a
  home image/no image is complete.
- Amla fruit is optional food, not medicine in this lane. Do not prescribe a
  dose, treatment, cure or immunity claim. Do not consume unknown, spoiled,
  contaminated, temple-handled or personally unsuitable fruit. Follow allergy,
  medical, swallowing and dietary guidance.
- No flame or incense is needed. Use daylight/electric light or none. A chosen
  flame stays on a stable non-combustible base, away from foliage and cloth,
  under continuous adult supervision, and is fully extinguished. No overnight
  lamp is permitted by this guide.
- No vigil is needed. Protect sleep and stop before fatigue.
- A non-fasting form is complete. Children, pregnant or breastfeeding people,
  older adults, people with diabetes or another condition, people taking
  medicines with food, people with eating-disorder history and people doing
  heat/heavy labour must not start or intensify fasting from Devam. Never change
  insulin, medicine, meals or hydration.
- Care and participation never depend on caste, sect, gender, purity, belief,
  reciprocal blessing, ritual payment or disclosure. Charity is voluntary,
  affordable, useful and requested; it must not create debt.
