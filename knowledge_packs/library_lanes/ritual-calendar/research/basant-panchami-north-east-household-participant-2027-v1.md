# Basant Panchami / Saraswati Puja North-East lane: research dossier v1

Checked: 2026-08-13

## Ownership and applicability

This dossier supports an actionable ritual-calendar lane for a North Indian household and ordinary school, college, temple or community-pandal participant, explicitly including the materially relevant West Bengal and Bihar contexts requested by the product owner. The generic Saraswati identity and story world remains with `devi-consumer/saraswati-puja-multi-context`; an existing typed proposal already requests that owner target. This lane may explain source-labelled spring, learning and living-practice associations, but it must not create a rival universal Saraswati theology, Navaratri Saraswati record or priestly liturgy.

Minor differences such as wearing yellow, the exact safe sweet, flower choice, book selection or a school programme do not create state copies. A public idol/pandal lifecycle with priest-led puja and visarjan is materially different from the home procedure and must remain institution-led. Child vidyarambha/akshara practice is parent/teacher/priest-led and cannot be improvised by the app.

## 2027 timing fixture

The Kolkata practitioner-calendar fixture is Thursday 11 February 2027. Its suggested morning interval is 06:11-11:51; Panchami begins 03:04 on 11 February and ends 03:18 on 12 February. The stated decision rule selects the day when Panchami prevails during Purvahna, and suggested worship remains within Panchami. Recompute for the user's city and accepted family/temple Panchang. A school, temple or pandal programme is a live institutional fact.

## Evidence map

- DrikPanchang Kolkata 2027: bounded calendar fixture, Panchami/Purvahna decision rule and descriptive evidence for student, school/college and vidyarambha associations. It is practitioner-calendar evidence, not sole ritual authority.
- Press Information Bureau and Akashvani 2026: nature/spring, new beginnings, learning, wisdom, arts and the special association with students. These sources support meaning, not detailed vidhi or guaranteed blessings.
- Bihar Information and Public Relations festival page: Bihar-specific evidence that Basant Panchami is widely identified as Saraswati Puja, with yellow linked to mustard fields and spring. Its manifestation and auspicious-result claims remain labelled living belief, not Devam fact or promise.
- West Bengal Council of Higher Secondary Education: current institutional recognition of Saraswati Puja/Sree Panchami and the preceding day. This proves salience in the education context, not one school procedure.
- A West Bengal government school page and current university records: examples of student/campus participation, decoration, music/arts and priest-led public puja. They remain institution-specific descriptions.
- Delhi Fire Service / Government fire guidance: temporary pandal exits, electrical clearance, extinguishers, separation of cooking and prohibition of open flame/fireworks near temporary structures.
- CPCB and West Bengal idol-immersion rules: natural clay and colours, no PoP/toxic paint/plastic, remove and segregate worship material, and use designated authority-scheduled immersion sites. Ordinary users should follow organisers rather than conduct immersion independently.
- FSSAI household guidance reused for offered/shared food: safe water, clean hands/utensils, allergen disclosure, prompt serving and safe storage.

## Consumer procedure boundary

The material-light form uses one book, instrument or creative tool already owned by the user, a source-labelled spring/learning reflection and one concrete learning action. The household form permits cleaning a learning space, respectfully placing familiar books/instruments, a safe optional flower/food/lamp, family-known prayer or silence, reading/study, sharing safe food and returning materials to use. It never fabricates mantra, repetition count, initiation, fast or exam-success promise.

The public-participation form tells the user to confirm programme and accessibility, keep exits clear, join only public actions, let the institution/priest define offerings and idol rites, safeguard minors and belongings, decline coerced food or ritual, and follow designated visarjan/waste arrangements. It does not teach image installation, consecration, homa, animal sacrifice, priestly worship, immersion or child-initiation rites.

## Major-difference routing

- West Bengal/Bihar school or public-pandal participation: same lane, distinct participant procedure.
- Yellow clothing/food/flowers: optional within-lane material expression.
- Household without an image or mantra: accessible substitution within lane.
- Institution-led idol installation and visarjan: separate authority context, not DIY.
- Child vidyarambha/akshara initiation: separate teacher/parent/priest-led authority context.
- Navaratri Saraswati Puja in Karnataka/Tamil/Gujarat contexts: separate owner-routed observance, never merged with Basant Panchami.
- Sikh Basant, Sufi Basant and other non-identical seasonal observances: outside this lane unless separately inventoried and sourced.
