## Story and claim ceiling

Lakshmi tying a rakhi to Bali, and Tagore's 1905 use of rakhi as a sign of unity.
Maharashtra Tourism additionally repeats Draupadi-Krishna, Shachi-Indra and the
Karnavati-Humayun association. These pages are current public retellings, not
fixed-text proof. Devam therefore:

1. labels the Krishna-Draupadi and Bali-Lakshmi accounts as Ministry of Tourism
   living retellings;
2. labels the Tagore episode as a current official historical retelling;
3. does not claim the retained Mahabharata contains a Raksha Bandhan origin;
4. omits Karnavati-Humayun because its historicity is contested and no fixed
   evidence was established for this pack; and
5. makes no victory, protection, prosperity, health or supernatural guarantee.

## Bounded action design

Minimum complete form: confirm date locally; ask before contact or tying; name
one mutual-care wish; use a loose plain thread/card/message or no material;
receive the other person's wish; close with one realistic reciprocal action.
Gender, touch, tilak, flame, Sanskrit, sweet, money, gift, fast, temple and a
promise of lifelong protection are not required.

The traditional household form may use a clean rakhi, optional tilak and rice,
an optional guarded lamp, safe food and a pre-budgeted gift. Every action is
offered, not imposed. A remote or estranged form is complete through a welcome
message, private remembrance or no contact. A person may decline participation
without proving affection or explaining trauma.

## Safety and consent boundary

- Ask before touching a forehead or wrist, tying anything, taking an image,
  posting, visiting, sending food or naming a relationship publicly.
- Tie loosely; remove immediately for pain, numbness, swelling, colour change,
  itching or distress. Use paper/card/no thread for infants, unsafe materials,
  sensory discomfort or skin concerns. Keep beads, pins and small decorations
  away from young children and anyone likely to mouth them.
- An aarti flame is optional. Use an LED lamp or no lamp around children,
  oxygen, loose clothing, hair, pets, crowds or fire restrictions.
- Check food allergens, hygiene, dietary restrictions and diabetes needs.
- Gifts are optional and affordable. No loan, dowry-like expectation, coercive
  transfer, PIN/OTP, password or promise of obedience is part of Rakhi.
- Mutual care does not authorize surveillance, control of clothing, movement,
  friends, education, work, marriage, sexuality, money or medical decisions.
- A child uses guardian-approved contact and does not privately meet, pay or
  send personal data to an adult in the name of the festival.
- Abuse, estrangement, bereavement, adoption, infertility, gender identity or
  family structure never creates a duty to contact or perform.
