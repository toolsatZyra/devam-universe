# Mahashivaratri North/West India Smarta household lane — source dossier

Retrieved and reviewed: 2026-08-13. This dossier supports one bounded consumer
lane. It is not a source edition, universal ritual manual, or live temple
schedule.

## Evidence map

1. **Śiva Purāṇa, Vidyeśvara-saṃhitā 9** — J. L. Shastri translation,
   citation-only via Wisdomlib. Verses 10-15 connect the named Śivarātri day
   with Shiva's proclamation after Brahma and Vishnu worship him and separately
   date the fire-column manifestation to Mārgaśīrṣa under Ārdrā. This is used to
   prevent the common collapse of two textual moments into one universal
   festival origin.
   https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/shiva-purana-english/d/doc225554.html
2. **Garuda Purāṇa, Dutt translation, chapter CXXIV, “The Siva Ratra
   Vratam”** — fixed 1908 public-domain English expression. It supplies the
   Sundarasena/fowler narrative, waning-fourteenth-night identity, vigil,
   water/bilva worship, four night divisions, and a formal closing. Its long
   fast, homa, pañcagavya, twelve-year, reward, and priest-led prescriptions
   are historical textual evidence, not copied into a universal modern home
   procedure.
   https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/the-garuda-purana-dutt/d/doc122604.html
3. **Drik Panchang, Delhi, 2027** — current practitioner calendar evidence.
   It resolves Saturday 6 March 2027, local Nishita 00:07-00:57 on 7 March,
   four local night quarters, and a 7 March parana window. It also explains
   the Purnimanta Phalguna / Amanta Magha naming difference. These exact times
   are a Delhi fixture, not reusable clock times for another location.
   https://www.drikpanchang.com/festivals/maha-shivaratri/maha-shivaratri-date-time.html?geoname-id=1273294
4. **Stanford Office for Religious & Spiritual Life, 2026-27 observance
   calendar** — independent civil-date corroboration for 6 March 2027. It does
   not establish ritual method or Hindu authority.
   https://orsl.stanford.edu/upcoming-observances/hindu-religious-services
5. **Incredible India, Ministry of Tourism** — official current descriptive
   evidence for widespread temple visiting, abhisheka, water/milk/honey and
   bilva offerings, prayer, vigil, major-temple crowds, and plural popular
   legends (Shiva-Parvati union, halahala, tandava). It is tourism copy, not a
   śāstric adjudication or household manual; energy and guaranteed-result
   claims are not adopted.
   https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/festivals-and-events/maha-shivratri
6. **Sringeri Sharada Peetham, Mahashivaratri 2026** — official
   institution-specific evidence for abhisheka, Rudra worship, four-yāma puja,
   scripture recitation, and an all-night programme. These are descriptive of
   Sringeri and are not converted into lay household instructions.
   https://www.sringeri.net/events/mahashivaratri-2026
7. **Directorate General of Health Services, Ministry of Health and Family
   Welfare, Diabetes Mellitus FAQ 43** — medical boundary for prolonged
   fasting: people using insulin should not fast; people with diabetes should
   preferably refrain, and treated type-2 patients should consult their
   physician about drug timing and dose. This supports a non-fasting default
   and a prohibition on changing medicines from ritual guidance.
   https://dghs.mohfw.gov.in/uploads/assets/FTQHV3zrwD8qHFnFiXRXIrjAo6BFUdILJFfyAcqk.pdf

## Adjudicated claims

- The selected 2027 Delhi date is 6 March; exact night windows are local and
  must be recalculated or obtained from the user's family/temple Panchang
  outside Delhi.
- Magha and Phalguna labels can denote the same civil observance under Amanta
  and Purnimanta month naming. They are not evidence of two national dates.
- A non-fasting, flame-optional remembrance path is the default accessible
  instruction. Formal vrata, fasting, parana, homa, consecration, and initiated
  mantra remain outside it.
- The Garuda Purana fowler story and the Shiva Purana proclamation/fire-column
  sequence are separately labelled textual narratives. Marriage, poison, and
  tandava are presented as plural popular festival associations documented by
  the Ministry of Tourism, never as one proven universal origin.
- Four-yāma worship is documented textually and institutionally. The elaborate
  consumer tier is participation under an established temple or family
  authority, not a generated priestly script.
