# Holi / Dhulandi North India consent-led household lane: research dossier v1

Checked: 2026-08-13

## Frozen applicability denominator

This dossier supports one lane: a North Indian household or invitation-only small community gathering in which every application of dry colour, tilak, flower colour or water is separately consented to. It must also give a useful no-colour form for people who want only greetings, story, music, food or quiet company. Consent is active, specific and reversible; family relationship, prior years, entering a venue, clothing or silence never substitutes for asking.

The lane does not authorize street colouring of passers-by, water balloons, public procession management, forced touching, intoxication, unsafe food, animal colouring or high-pressure crowd play. Holika Dahan is the previous-day lane. Dol Jatra/Dol Purnima or Shantiniketan Basant Utsav, Punjab Hola Mohalla, Barsana-area Lathmar Holi, Goa Shigmo, Manipur Yaoshang, Madhya/West Indian Rang Panchami and materially distinct temple or sampradaya observances require separate sourced lanes. Dhuleti, Dhulandi and Rangwali Holi remain aliases here only where they mean the ordinary next-day North Indian colour observance.

## 2027 timing fixture

The Delhi practitioner-calendar fixture places Rangwali Holi / Dhulandi on Monday 22 March 2027, the day after Holika Dahan on Sunday 21 March. The consumer answer is a civil-day observance rather than a portable clock-time ritual. The product must still recompute the date for the user's location and accepted calendar and verify every live venue or neighbourhood schedule.

## Meaning and story boundary

- Incredible India's current Holi page describes spring, colour, togetherness and playful colour practice, and gives a contemporary living Radha-Krishna colour account. Its rhetoric that people may unexpectedly be showered or that everyone is fair game is descriptive tourism copy and is explicitly rejected as consumer instruction.
- The previous-evening Prahlada/Holika account remains source-labelled in the Holika Dahan lane. This lane may briefly explain that continuity but does not duplicate the fire procedure.
- Goa's official 2026 greeting describes spring, renewal, hope, truth, courage, unity in diversity, natural colour, water conservation and avoiding harm to others or nature. These are public meanings, not guaranteed spiritual outcomes.
- Ministry of Tourism's Utsav index demonstrates materially different forms—Shantiniketan floral Basant Utsav, Punjab Hola Mohalla, Goa Shigmo and other regional expressions. It is routing evidence, not proof of a universal practice.

## Consent, health, environment and animal evidence

- Assam and Mumbai Police public messaging, contemporaneously reported by Indian Express, says consent is not implied and must be sought; colour without consent is harassment, not Holi participation. Older Delhi Police warnings likewise reject forced colour and balloons. The app adopts the stronger, person-neutral rule for every participant.
- Haryana Health Department advises hydration, greetings/home-made sweets, tilak Holi, organic colours or flowers, eye/skin/nose protection, qualified medical help or 112 in emergency, and no drink-driving, drugs, animals, water balloons, loud music, synthetic colour, mud or cow dung.
- The NHM eye-care training manual says children require adult supervision, protective eyewear should be used, and Holi chemical colour should be avoided in favour of herbal colour. The lane will not improvise medical treatment; it tells users to stop, rinse exposed eyes with clean water without rubbing, and seek qualified care for persistent pain or vision change only if the fixed manual supports those particulars after final verification. Until then, emergency language remains conservative.
- The Ministry of Environment's 2026 campaign supports biodegradable nature-based colours. It is an educational example, not a blanket allergy-safety guarantee: turmeric, beetroot, leafy material and flowers can still irritate or stain, so patch/ingredient awareness and consent remain necessary.
- Animal Welfare Board of India's 2026 advisory says no dry or wet colour, water balloon or coloured spray on pets, strays, cattle or birds; keep pets safe from noise/crowds, remove residue/plastic/balloons and obtain veterinary help after exposure or injury.
- FSSAI's Holi festive drive identifies heightened adulteration risk in sweets, savouries, milk products, oils and cereals. Packaged or purchased food must be from a reliable licensed source where practical, ingredients/allergens disclosed, and intoxication never hidden in food or drink.

## Consumer procedure boundary

The minimum no-colour form is greeting, a source-labelled spring/togetherness reflection, one repaired relationship or kind action, and an optional safe food or music close. No colour is necessary.

The standard household form creates explicit stations: no-colour, dry tilak/hand colour, and optionally limited water. Each person chooses before play and can change choice. Colour is offered on the hand first; face, hair, mouth, eyes, clothing, mobility/medical devices and personal belongings are off-limits unless separately and explicitly agreed. No surprise contact, chasing, restraint, balloons or colour from heights/vehicles.

The invitation-only participant form requires a named organiser, bounded venue, consent briefing, clean water/handwashing, labelled colours, first-aid/emergency access, dry safe floor, child supervision, quiet/no-colour area, animal protection, food/allergen labels, sober transport and planned cleanup. A user leaves immediately if the organiser treats attendance as blanket consent.

## Major-difference routing

- Greeting-only, dry tilak/hand colour and limited-water play: same consent-led lane; participants select individually.
- Natural powder versus flower-derived colour, exact sweet, song or clothing: minor materials, not state variants.
- Delhi/UP/Haryana/Rajasthan ordinary next-day colour-day aliases: same lane when practice and authority match.
- Rang Panchami on a later civil day: separate calendar lane.
- Dol/Basant Utsav, Hola Mohalla, Lathmar Holi, Shigmo and Yaoshang: materially different story, calendar, institution or public form; separate lanes.
- Temple-specific deity colour service, procession or priest-led rite: separate institutional/ritual authority.
- Bhang, alcohol or other intoxicant preparation/service: outside this lane; never treated as required Holi practice.

## Review requirements

- Tourism descriptions never override affirmative consent.
- The pack must contain a complete no-colour answer, not merely safer colour play.
- English/Hindi parity must cover consent withdrawal, children, eyes/skin, slippery floors, balloons, animals, food/allergens/intoxicants, driving, cleanup and emergency routing.
- No natural colour is called universally hypoallergenic or safe for every person.
- No universal Krishna theology, national liturgy, promised reconciliation or social-barrier erasure is claimed.
- Only major regional/calendar/authority differences create separate lanes.
