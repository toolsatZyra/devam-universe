## Meaning and story evidence

The practitioner Dwadashi collection explicitly associates the month sequence
with named Vishnu forms or avatar identities. It is useful living-calendar
evidence, not a universal national ritual manual:

<https://www.drikpanchang.com/vrat-katha/dwadashi/dwadashi-vrat-katha-collection.html>

For the ten Vishnu-form labels, the fixed source-labelled narrative spine is
Bhagavad Gita 4.4-8. Arjuna questions how Krishna could have taught the ancient
Vivasvan; Krishna says both have had many births, declares himself unborn yet
manifest through his own power, and says he comes forth when dharma declines,
to protect the good, restrain wrongdoing and re-establish dharma. This is a
source theological account, not proof that a particular Dwadashi originated in
the Gita, not permission to identify or harm a supposed evildoer, and not a
promise of rescue or social victory.

Fixed textual portal:

<https://www.gitasupersite.iitk.ac.in/srimad?language=dv&field_chapter_value=4&field_nsutra_value=4>

Ramalakshmana may additionally cross-link to Valmiki Ramayana Ayodhya Kanda
2.45-46 and 2.53: Rama, Sita and Lakshmana begin exile, Lakshmana accompanies
and guards them, and Rama avoids prolonging the citizens' hardship. This is
brotherly/source context, not a command to obey abuse, lose sleep or accept
exile.

Bachha Baras is not given the Vishnu-form template. The existing reviewed Vasu
Baras material preserves a current practitioner Nandini/cow-and-calf narrative
and an animal-welfare boundary. Gujarat Bachha Baras retains its own date and
regional owner. A complete home route requires no live animal; feeding,
touching, washing, decorating, tethering, transport or farm/gaushala visit
belongs to the animal's responsible keeper and current institution.

## Safe complete consumer form

For every item, the generic complete route is: resolve applicability; keep
normal food, water, medicine and sleep; hear the calendar identity and labelled
source story; use a familiar Vishnu name, plain-language reflection or silence;
choose one voluntary care/accountability action or none; and close without a
result claim. No fast, vigil, parana, bath, lamp, incense, mantra count,
donation, animal contact, temple attendance or purchase is required.

Formal fasting, deity-specific mantra/nyasa, priest-led worship, initiation,
offerings, vrata-katha recitation, parana, temple schedule and named family
close require their own current authority. Health, medicine and safeguarding
override ritual instructions.

## Major variants only

Telugu/Hindi/English language, spelling, dress, familiar Vishnu name,
image/no-image, prayer/silence and ordinary food do not create state variants.
Separate routing is required only for changed tithi/day authority, named-form
theology or story ownership, formal fast/parana, temple/family procedure,
animal handling, food/allergen exposure, fire/crowd risk or a different close.

## Acceptance boundary

Reject copied clock times; one national Dwadashi puja; mandatory fasting,
vigil, mantra, donation or temple attendance; avatar claims as history;
real-person demon/evildoer labels; promised cure, protection, victory or
absolution; unsafe cattle contact; maternal/child-longevity pressure; and a
translated state variant without a material difference.

This matrix does not count a lane as authored. Authored does not mean projected,
hosted, independently culturally reviewed or released; all 19,480 day cells
remain unprojected.
