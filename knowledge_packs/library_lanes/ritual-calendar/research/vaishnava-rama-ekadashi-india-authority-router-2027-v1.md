# Vaishnava Rama Ekadashi India authority-router research (2027)

Checked 2026-08-13. This evidence freeze covers only the separately accepted
`ekadashi-rama-vaishnava` denominator. It does not convert every Vaishnava,
ISKCON visitor, Indian state or household to one date, fast, parana, mantra,
food rule, vigil or institutional programme. It does not replace the completed

## Frozen applicability denominator

Count this lane only when the user follows, visits or explicitly asks about a
**named current Vaishnava authority** whose calendar materially selects a
different observance day or procedure. The authority may be a sampradaya,
temple, institution or household's accepted Vaishnava calendar. “Vaishnava”
alone is not sufficiently deterministic, and a state is not a ritual authority.

Reviewed 2027 Indian discovery fixtures show the material date split clearly:

- a general Vijayawada fixture places ordinary Rama Ekadashi on Monday,
  25 October and Vaishnava Rama on Tuesday, 26 October;
- an independently produced ISKCON/Vaishnava fixture for Korba,
  Chhattisgarh places Rama Ekadashi on Tuesday, 26 October;
- an ISKCON festival fixture for Jalali, Uttar Pradesh also displays Tuesday,
  26 October;
- a Kolkata, West Bengal daily fixture displays ordinary Rama parana together
  with Gauna Rama and Vaishnava Rama on Tuesday, 26 October;
- reviewed Maharashtra and Andhra Pradesh fixtures likewise distinguish the
  ordinary 25 October date from a 26 October Vaishnava display.

These samples establish that an authority/date variation is real and
consumer-material. They do **not** prove a universal 26 October date across
India, all Vaishnavas or all institutions. The Korba page expressly describes
itself as an independently created GCal-rule implementation and warns that a
mismatch may be a software error. It is useful current practitioner-calendar
evidence, not official GBC, temple or sampradaya authority.

Before answering “what do I do today?”, resolve:

2. the named sampradaya, temple, institution or accepted household calendar;

5. whether the user wants a safe devotional remembrance or the authority's

Never copy the 2027 date or any displayed parana time to another city, year,

## Fixed story and source relationship

The complete source-labelled Rama story remains the Mucukunda–Candrabhaga–
Shobhana account in *Padma Purana*, Uttara Khanda 6.60, reviewed in N. A.
Deshpande's English expression together with the displayed Sanskrit:

- <https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/the-padma-purana/d/doc365637.html>
- <https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/padma-purana-sanskrit/d/doc428115.html>

The authority-specific date split does not create a new Purana story. The
consumer pack must remain understandable by summarising the whole sequence,
while cross-linking the completed ordinary Rama lane and its source context
instead of inventing a second canonical story owner.

The complete sequence that must survive bilingual compression is:

1. Yudhishthira asks Janardana the name of Kartika Krishna Ekadashi; Krishna
   names Rama and attributes great merit to it.
2. King Mucukunda is described as a truthful Vishnu devotee and powerful ruler.
3. His daughter Candrabhaga is “given” in marriage to weak Shobhana; her
   consent is not narrated.
4. Shobhana visits when Ekadashi approaches. Candrabhaga knows he cannot bear
   hunger and fears her father's severe rule.
5. Mucukunda publicly orders that nobody eat; even elephants, horses, young
   animals and other animals are denied grass, food and water.
6. Shobhana asks what to do. Under royal, household and social pressure he
   accepts the fast although he is weak.
7. Hunger causes extreme distress. At sunrise Shobhana dies.
8. Mucukunda arranges cremation. Candrabhaga does not die with her husband;
   after the rites she remains at her father's house.
9. The source attributes an ornate but unstable divine city to Shobhana's
   observance.
10. The pilgrim Somasharman sees Shobhana, hears that the city is unstable,
    and carries a message to Candrabhaga.
11. Candrabhaga asks to go there and says her accumulated vow merit can make
    the city stable.
12. Somasharman takes her to Vamadeva, who sprinkles her with water and Vedic
    mantras; the source attributes a divine body to this act.
13. Candrabhaga reaches Shobhana and says she has observed Ekadashi faithfully
    since after age eight. Her merit is said to stabilise the city.
14. The source describes their attributed reunion and promises enjoyment,
    salvation, sin freedom and Vishnu's world.

This is a source story containing coercive restriction, animal deprivation,
death, survival, funeral, bereavement, messenger contact, childhood observance,
merit and attributed reunion. It is not an institutional fasting manual,
medical recommendation, funeral procedure or promise about the afterlife.

## Meaning and safe Devam synthesis

A bounded consumer meaning is: **a calendar difference can be religiously
meaningful without giving anyone authority to endanger life, food, water,
medicine, sleep or free consent**. The named tradition owns its calendar rule;
the individual retains bodily autonomy and may choose a complete fed,
hydrated, rested form.

Shobhana's known weakness, severe hunger and death must remain a warning, not
a model of successful devotion. Candrabhaga's continued life after his death
must remain explicit; survival is not disloyalty. These are Devam safety
interpretations, not claims that the source teaches a modern consent ethic.

## Who commonly observes this lane

This route is for a user who already names or knowingly follows a Vaishnava
sampradaya, temple, institution or household calendar that distinguishes this
day. It may also help a visitor understand why a temple observes Rama one day
after the ordinary household listing.

Do not say that all Vaishnavas, all ISKCON communities, all residents of a
reviewed state, all temple visitors or all Indians observe it. A visitor does
not acquire the institution's fasting duty merely by attending. If the user
does not name an authority, show the ordinary and authority-specific choices
and ask which calendar they follow; do not silently choose.

## Complete safe “what do I do today?” route

### Minimum private authority-aware form

1. Confirm city, civil date and the named current authority. If these are not
   known, present the date difference without declaring today the correct day.
2. Keep normal food, water, medicine, insulin, hygiene, child feeding, animal
   care and sleep. Begin no fast, vigil or parana from this router.
3. Remember Vishnu/Janardana with a familiar name, prayer or silence. No image,
   mantra, offering or material is required.
4. Hear the complete Mucukunda–Candrabhaga–Shobhana sequence, including the
   decree, animal deprivation, known weakness, pressure, distress, death,
   Candrabhaga's survival, funeral, unstable city, messenger, Vamadeva rite,
   childhood observance, merit and attributed reunion.
5. State the boundary: pressure is not consent; death is not ritual success;
   no animal or child loses food or water; no survivor owes merit, self-harm or
   reunion; source afterlife claims are not established facts.
6. Optionally protect one food, water, medicine, rest or easy-exit safeguard
   already within one's lawful care. No purchase, disclosure, confrontation,
   contact or action is required.
7. Close fed, hydrated and rested, without blame, miracle claim or guessed
   parana. If seeking the formal vrata, move to the named authority's current
   instructions and retain medical safety.

### Consent-led family form

Use the same material-free, fed, hydrated and rested core. Explain that the
calendar difference comes from authority rules, not from one state being more
or less devout. Give every participant an easy no and private exit. Do not
role-play the decree, starvation, death, cremation, dead-person message,
sprinkling or reunion; assign king, enforcer, weak person, widow, messenger,
teacher or rescuer roles; or demand fasting, vigil, merit, belief, forgiveness,
disclosure, donation or contact.

### Named temple, sampradaya or institution form

Verify the official current calendar and programme directly with the named
authority. Ask about visitor eligibility, ordinary/Gauna/Vaishnava/
Mahadvadashi label, fast and food rules, parana, mantra or initiation limits,
child and animal care, medicine and accessibility, crowd control, emergency
plan, photography, donation and exit. The authority owns its formal procedure;
this router does not reconstruct it from general memory or a story chapter.

Keep normal medical care and leave any unsafe restriction. A remote, home,
non-fasting or cancelled form is complete. Displayed parana times are never
portable.

## Materials and substitutions

No material is required for the complete safe form. Optional existing supports

Do not require fasting food, water vessel, tulasi, flower, lamp, flame,

## Safety and dignity boundaries

- Never withhold or reduce food, water, medicine, insulin, child feeding,
  hygiene or sleep because of this router. Never withhold food or water from
  an animal for a human observance.
- A king, guru, institution, employer, school, elder, spouse, household or
  social group cannot turn pressured fasting into free consent.
- Shobhana's hunger, extreme distress and death are warnings, not ritual
  success. Danger signs require qualified urgent care, not perseverance.
- Children keep normal food, water, medicine, school and sleep. The source's
  childhood statement is not permission for a child fast or vigil.
- Candrabhaga is not to blame for a coercive royal system. A spouse is not
  property, enforcement labour, merit supply or rescue duty.
- Candrabhaga survives her husband. Never romanticise self-harm, sati, death
  with a spouse, compulsory widowhood conduct or reunion.
- The story's cremation is not permission to approach, touch, move, wash,
  dress, photograph, cremate or handle a body, pyre, ash or remains.
- Do not claim contact with the dead, relay messages, locate an afterlife,
  interpret dreams or signs, or promise reunion, divine body/city, salvation,
  pleasure, wealth, sin removal or Vishnu's world.
- Vamadeva's sprinkling is a source event, not permission to sprinkle a person,
  improvise a restricted mantra or promise transformation.
- Illness, death, grief, poverty, instability or an unmet hope is not proof of
  weak faith, sin, impurity, wrong calendar or failed observance.
- Do not rank eligibility, dignity, truth, prayer, food, water or safety by
  caste, birth, gender, marital status, health, disability, age or wealth.
- Immediate danger in India calls for emergency help such as 112 or qualified
  care; do not wait for ritual advice.

## Major variants only

The **material** variation completed here is named-authority timing and formal
practice: a Vaishnava authority can select a different civil day and parana
from the ordinary Rama lane. This justifies a separate router.

Keep the selected states together while the authority question, safe sequence,
story, materials, safety and close remain the same. Language, spelling, month
vocabulary, dress, food preference, familiar Vishnu name, image/no-image and
silence are minor differences and do not justify state packs.

Require separate handling only for:

- the already completed ordinary Rama household lane;
- a named sampradaya, temple or institution whose current date, Mahadvadashi,
  fasting, worship, mantra or parana procedure materially differs;
- Gauna Rama where the named authority treats it as a distinct calendar route;
- child, animal, medical, disability, nutrition or sleep needs;
- bereavement, self-harm, funeral, remains, widowhood, coercion, abuse, legal
  or crisis needs; and
- Govatsa/Vasu Baras, Padmanabha Dwadashi, Dhanteras, Yama Deepam or another
  nearby observance with a different identity, timing, practice or close.

Do not create a West Bengal, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu,
Kerala, Maharashtra, Odisha, Andhra Pradesh, North or West variant merely
because a discovery fixture was sampled there.
