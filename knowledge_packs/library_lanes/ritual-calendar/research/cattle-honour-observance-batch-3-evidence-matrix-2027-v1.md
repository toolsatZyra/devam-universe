# Cattle-honour observance batch: frozen evidence matrix (2027)

Reviewed 2026-08-13. Exactly three accepted denominator items are frozen:
`bol-choth`, `pola`, and `vrishabhotsava`. Coverage before the batch is 183 of
208. These are related by cattle honour but are not one national ritual.

## Reconciled identities

| Item | Timing | Common route and material difference |
|---|---|---|
| Bol Choth | Gujarati Shravana Krishna Chaturthi; 21 August 2027 in the reviewed India fixture, before Gujarati Nag Pancham. | Mainly Gujarati family observance for the welfare of cows and calves. Current sources describe an evening Godhuli worship, fast and avoidance of milk products. All remain optional; offspring/wealth claims are attributed, not promised. Bahula Chaturthi is a related regional name requiring its own owner confirmation. |
| Pola / Bail Pola | Maharashtra Amanta Shravana Amavasya, corresponding to the reviewed 31 August 2027 Amavasya fixture. | Maharashtra and Chhattisgarh farming-community thanksgiving for bulls/oxen. Maharashtra Tourism records rest from work, washing/oil massage, decoration, food, procession, household rangoli and children's wooden bulls. A non-animal household form can use a wooden/drawn bull and gratitude to farmers. |
| Vrishabhotsava | Current calendars co-list it with Pola on Shravana/Bhadrapada Amavasya; recompute the 2027 city/calendar assignment rather than assuming identity. | Bull/Nandi-facing observance label. Current evidence is too thin to universalize a separate live-animal vidhi. It receives a bounded image/gratitude lane and routes formal Shaiva, temple or cattle practice to a current owner. It is cross-identified with Pola when a user's calendar treats them as the same event, preventing duplicate action. |

## Story and meaning boundary

Maharashtra Tourism supplies the living meaning of gratitude to cattle that
support farm work and also reports a young-Krishna/Polasur story. The latter is
kept as a source-labelled tourism narrative, not claimed as the universal
origin of Pola. For Vrishabhotsava, Nandi's well-known Shiva association may be
used only when supported by a fixed source or current named temple; no story is
invented from the name. Bol Choth's Krishna/Gau Mata identity and prosperity or
offspring beliefs remain attributed current practice, not guaranteed results.

## Action and animal-welfare boundary

The Prevention of Cruelty to Animals framework and current Animal Welfare Board
materials govern any live-animal route. No generic pack directs ownership,
transport, restraint, racing, forced procession, paint/dye, piercing, horn
alteration, heavy ornament, smoke/flame near an animal, forced bathing, forced
feeding, milk deprivation, overwork or withholding veterinary care. A handler
must already have lawful responsibility and veterinary knowledge; the animal's
health, rest, shade, water, feed, footing and ability to withdraw take priority.
Users without such responsibility use a drawing, toy/wooden bull, existing
image, story, farmer gratitude or donation to a verified welfare provider.
