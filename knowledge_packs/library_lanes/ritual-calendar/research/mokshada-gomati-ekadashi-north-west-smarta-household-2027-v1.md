# Mokshada / Gomati Ekadashi ordinary North/West Smarta household research (2027)

Checked 2026-08-13. This evidence freeze covers the accepted ordinary
`ekadashi-mokshada` work item and the census-dispositioned Gomati same-date
alias. It does not merge Gita Jayanti reading, Guruvayur Ekadashi, a
Gauna/Vaishnava date, fasting, vigil, Tulasi handling, dream interpretation,

parana or temple liturgy.

## Applicability and deterministic timing

The ordinary lane applies only after the user's Indian city and accepted
ordinary Smarta Panchang resolve Margashirsha Shukla Ekadashi. Reviewed 2027
Indian discovery fixtures for Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Punjab,
Arunachal Pradesh and Chhattisgarh place **ordinary Mokshada Ekadashi on
Thursday, 9 December 2027**. Reviewed 2027 ISKCON discovery fixtures also show
9 December for the sampled Indian locations. That observed agreement is not a
portable national rule or parana window.

Older calendar evidence shows separately labelled Gauna/Vaishnava Mokshada on
a following civil day in some years. Every city, sunrise, arunodaya,
tithi/day-boundary, Mahadwadashi, ordinary/Gauna/Vaishnava authority and parana
must therefore be recomputed. The 2027 candidate disposition does not add a
separate Gauna or Vaishnava Mokshada denominator item; if a user's current
authority resolves a distinct date or close, route it as a major authority
variant rather than guessing or silently merging it.

Gomati is dispositioned as a same-date regional name for Mokshada. Keep the
alias within one ordinary lane unless fixed story, deity, eligibility, ordered
practice or close proves materially different. Language, spelling, month
vocabulary and state alone do not create variants.

## Fixed Padma Purana expression

Reviewed N. A. Deshpande's English expression of *Padma Purana*, Uttara
Khanda, chapter 39, together with the displayed Sanskrit:

- <https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/the-padma-purana/d/doc365616.html>
- <https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/padma-purana-sanskrit/d/doc428094.html>

The complete 47-verse sequence is:

1. Yudhishthira praises Vishnu and asks Krishna for the name, manner and deity
   of Margashirsha Shukla Ekadashi.
2. Krishna distinguishes the preceding Utpatti/Utpanna from the bright-half
   Moksha/Mokshada day and attributes Vajapeya merit and destruction of sins
   to hearing its account.
3. The formal opening directs careful Damodara worship with Tulasi sprouts,
   incense and lamps, observance joined to the earlier method, a night vigil,
   dance, song and praise. It attributes sin removal and ancestor salvation to
   the story and merit.
4. In Champaka city King Vaikhanasa rules prosperous subjects. In a dream he
   sees his forefathers in a degraded state/hell, weeping and asking him, as
   one born from their bodies, to rescue them.
5. Distressed, he tells Brahmanas that kingdom, horses, elephants, wife and
   sons no longer please him. He asks what vow or penance could save the
   ancestors and questions the worth of a wealthy powerful son whose father is
   in hell.
6. The Brahmanas direct him to sage Parvata's hermitage. He travels with
   Brahmanas and kings, prostrates and touches Parvata's feet. Parvata asks
   about the seven constituents of the kingdom.
7. Vaikhanasa repeats the dream and asks for a remedy. Parvata meditates and
   claims knowledge of the ancestors' former acts.
8. Parvata says the king's father, proud of royal power, left his wife during
   her conception-favourable period for royal business and did not have sex
   with her; the text calls this a sin causing him and the ancestors to fall
   into terrible hell.
9. Parvata directs everyone to perform Mokshada and give its merit to the dead
   father. Vaikhanasa returns, observes it with all, and transfers the merit.
10. The source attributes an immediate flower shower, the father and ancestors'
    salvation, the father's voice from the sky blessing his son, heaven,
    destruction of the observer's sins, salvation after death and Vajapeya
    merit from reading/hearing.

These are source events and claims. The sexual/reproductive premise, filial
rescue duty, caste hierarchy, dream/hell message, merit transfer, voice from
the sky, miracle and afterlife outcome must remain visible as source facts but
must never become ordinary consumer instructions or verified assertions.

## Secondary formal-practice corroboration

*Narada Purana* chapter 120, verses 59b-62a, independently names Moksha or
Mokshada on Margashirsha Shukla Ekadashi. It directs fasting, Dwadashi Ananta
worship with upacaras, Brahmana feeding and dakshina, followed by eating with
kin, and attributes pleasures, uplift of ten ancestor and ten successor
generations, and attainment of Hari.

- <https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/narada-purana-english/d/doc1857985.html>

Use this as identity/formal-practice corroboration only, not a consumer script
or proof of lineage and afterlife results.

## Sensitive source facts that must not become Devam's voice

- **Sexual and reproductive coercion:** the story labels a husband's failure
  to have sex during a conception-favourable period as sin. Consent is required
  every time. Marriage, fertility timing, lineage hope, royal/family duty,
  ritual or fear of hell never creates sexual consent or reproductive duty.
  Do not ask users to schedule sex, conceive, disclose fertility, resume
  contact, forgive coercion or change contraception/medical care.
- **Son and descendant duty:** the father demands rescue and the king questions
  the value of a son who cannot save him. No child, son, daughter, descendant,
  spouse or mourner owes ritual labour, merit, money, conception, lineage,
  forgiveness or afterlife rescue.
- **Dreams and dead-person communication:** a distressing dream starts the
  story and a voice from the sky ends it. Do not claim dreams diagnose an
  ancestor's condition, prove guilt, request action, transmit messages or
  establish contact with the dead. Do not tell a grieving person to obey a
  dream or seek signs.
- **Hell, degraded birth and caste hierarchy:** hell/degraded-stock language
  and Brahmana-centred authority are source facts, not rankings of human worth.
  Never rank dignity, safety, food, prayer, grief or access by caste, birth,
  occupation, gender, fertility, marital status or lineage.
- **Merit transfer and automatic salvation:** the source attributes immediate
  ancestor release and salvation to the vow. Do not promise merit transfer,
  ancestor rescue, flower/voice signs, heaven, no rebirth, Moksha, sin removal
  or changed circumstances of the dead.
- **Automatic absolution:** ritual never erases abuse, sexual coercion,
  violence, neglect, evidence, reporting, accountability, restitution, repair
  or legal duty. Regret and worship do not substitute for consent and safety.
- **Fasting and vigil:** no one owes food/water restriction, altered medicine
  or sleep deprivation. A complete ordinary form remains fed, hydrated and
  rested.
- **Plant, flame, smoke and performance:** Tulasi sprouts, incense, lamps,
  dance, song and vigil are formal source elements. Do not require plant
  touching/plucking, flame, smoke, fragrance, movement, sound or public
  performance. Each needs authority, safety, accessibility and consent.
- **Donation, feeding and hierarchy:** Brahmana feeding/dakshina are formal
  source practices, not ordinary materials or payments. Do not solicit money,
  food, property, gifts or apology, or rank recipients by birth.
- **Royal and expert authority:** king, Brahmanas and meditating sage are story
  roles, not permission to diagnose guilt, past lives, sexual conduct, dreams,
  ancestors or afterlife. A priest, guide or app cannot verify those claims.

## Consumer meaning

A safe Devam synthesis is: **care for the living responsibility that is
actually yours today without inheriting an unverifiable debt from a dream,
ancestor, family line or fear**.

An optional no-purchase action is to check one current consent, care or record
already within the user's responsibility, or to decline one guilt-based demand.
Do not contact an estranged person, confront family, interpret a dream, change
sexual/reproductive/medical decisions, handle an estate, report wrongdoing,
make payment or take another person's task from this guide. No action is
required.

## Complete ordinary household routes

### Minimum private form

1. Confirm local ordinary Mokshada/Gomati and distinguish Gita Jayanti,
   Guruvayur Ekadashi and any current Gauna/Vaishnava authority.
2. Keep normal food, water, medicine, insulin, hygiene and sleep; begin no
   fast, vigil or parana.
3. Remember Vishnu, Krishna or Damodara with a familiar name, prayer or
   silence; no image, Tulasi, lamp, incense, mantra, food or gift is required.
4. Hear the complete Vaikhanasa sequence, including the dream, ancestor demand,
   son-duty rhetoric, journey to Parvata, sexual/reproductive premise, vow,
   merit transfer, flower shower, sky voice and attributed salvation.
5. State that consent is required every time; no descendant owes rescue; dreams
   are not instructions or verified contact; ritual does not erase harm.
6. Hear the same-day separation: Gita Jayanti has an attributable reading lane;
   Guruvayur requires its named temple/tradition authority.
7. Optionally protect one living consent/care boundary already yours, or choose
   no action; no disclosure, contact, confrontation or interpretation is due.
8. Close fed, hydrated and rested, without parana, merit transfer, ancestor
   message, miracle test or later-event interpretation.

### Consent-led family form

Use the same fed, rested, material-free core. Give an easy no and private exit.
Do not assign father-rescuer, dutiful son/daughter, spouse, conceiver, faster,
vigil-keeper, dream interpreter, priest, donor or recipient roles. Do not ask
about sex, fertility, contraception, pregnancy, grief, dreams, ancestors,
estrangement or family wrongdoing. No family member owes reading, disclosure,
forgiveness, contact, action or belief.

### Named temple, Gita or institution participation

First identify the route. For Gita Jayanti, use the existing attributable
edition/commentary reading lane and verify the current organiser. For
Guruvayur or another temple, verify current temple authority, date, schedule,
entry, crowd, accessibility, children, fast/food, plant, flame/smoke,
photography, donation and exit. Do not handle text, image, plant, lamp, incense,
food or public materials without authorisation. A cancelled, remote or
material-free home form is complete.

## Materials and substitutions

No material is required. Optional existing supports are a familiar Vishnu,
Krishna or Damodara image/text and a private consent/care reminder, each with
no-image, no-writing and silent substitutions.

Do not require Tulasi sprouts/leaves, incense, lamps, flame, flowers, food,
dakshina, gifts, ancestor photograph, dream journal, Gita purchase or any paid
item. The material-free ordinary form is complete. An attributable Gita already
owned or lawfully available belongs only to the separate Gita Jayanti route.

## Major variants only

Keep Mokshada and census-dispositioned Gomati in one ordinary lane across the
selected states while identity, source story, safe sequence, authority and
close remain materially the same. Language, spelling, month vocabulary, dress,
food preference, familiar Vishnu name, image/no-image and silence are minor.

Require separate routes for:

- a current location/authority-specific Gauna, Vaishnava, ISKCON or
  Mahadwadashi date and parana difference;

  named institutional programme;
- Guruvayur Ekadashi or another named temple/tradition identity;
- a formal family/guru/temple fast, vigil, Tulasi, lamp, incense, dance, song,
  feeding, donation, merit-transfer or parana practice;
- medical, nutrition, sleep, sexual/reproductive consent, trauma, grief,
  mental-health, estate, accountability, legal or crisis needs; and
- another nearby observance such as Vivaha Panchami, Matsya Dwadashi,
  Dattatreya Jayanti, Karthigai Deepam or local Margashirsha observance.
