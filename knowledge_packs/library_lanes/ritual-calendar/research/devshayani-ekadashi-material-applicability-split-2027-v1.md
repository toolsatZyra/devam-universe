## Fixed source-labelled chapter and internal naming tension

The reviewed N. A. Deshpande English expression of *Padma Purana*, Uttara
Khanda chapter 53, lines 54-73, begins with Yudhishthira asking Krishna for the
name and manner of Ashadha Shukla Ekadashi. Krishna names Shyani, attributes
heaven, salvation, sin removal and sacrificial merit, and speaks of worshipping
Vamana with lotuses.

Yudhishthira asks how Vishnu sleeps in the ocean, how he resorted to Bali and
why people sleep on the ground. Krishna tells a Vamana-Bali account. Bali is a
devoted ruler who performs sacrifices and vows. Krishna says that, after
deliberation with Indra, Brihaspati and other deities, he takes the Vamana form,
uses what this translation calls prevarication, asks Bali for three and a half
steps of land, expands cosmically, seizes Bali, places a half-step on his head
and sends him to the lower world. Bali remains truthful and Vishnu is pleased.

The chapter then says one Vishnu form stays with Bali while another lies on
Shesha in the ocean until Kartika Ekadashi. It prescribes worship, a night
vigil, lights, eating on a palasha leaf, sleeping on the ground, celibacy and
month-specific food avoidance: vegetables in Shravana, curd in Bhadrapada, milk
in Ashvina and two-leafed grains in Kartika. It uses caste-denigrating language
and attributes salvation, pleasure, heaven, sin removal and sacrifice merit.

Lines 59 and 69 in the reviewed expression call the bright-half Ashadha day
“Kamika,” although the chapter and question identify Shyani. Devam does not
silently repair this possible textual/translation naming inconsistency and does
not derive the date from it. The accepted Panchang and source-expression labels
remain distinct evidence layers.

## Consumer treatment of source tensions

- Vishnu's four-month sleep is source theology, not biological information or
  a claim that the divine is unavailable. Do not use it to delay care,
  responsibility, worship, travel safety or emergency action.
- The Vamana-Bali account contains devotion, truthfulness, deception,
  conquest, seizure and subordination. Preserve those events without turning
  them into permission for fraud, coerced consent, land-taking, humiliation,
  caste hierarchy or force by a powerful person. Do not erase Bali's devotion
  or reduce the story to “evil demon defeated.”
- This chapter's Vamana-Bali account is source context for Devshayani. The
  Kerala Onam/Mahabali reception and its consumer story owner remain separate;
  shared characters do not merge regional meanings.
- Night vigil, ground sleeping, celibacy, lights, palasha-leaf eating and
  four-month food restrictions are formal source practices, not generic home
  instructions. Never prescribe sleep deprivation, floor sleeping, sexual
  control of another person, flame use or elimination of vegetables, curd,
  milk, pulses/legumes or other food groups from this ordinary pack.
- ICMR-NIN's current dietary guidance emphasises adequate variety across food
  groups, with needs varying by age, sex, physiological status and activity.
  A religious restriction cannot override infant/child feeding, pregnancy or
  lactation nutrition, allergy-safe food, diabetes care, eating-disorder care,
  disability needs, prescribed diet or medicine.
- Celibacy is an individual voluntary formal vow, not abstinence owed by a
  spouse and never permission to shame sexuality, control contraception,
  prevent care or override consent.
- Lotus and palasha materials are source details, not instructions to pick
  plants, enter protected/restricted land, purchase ritual goods or use a leaf
  as a food-safety surface.
- The source's caste term, heaven, salvation, sin destruction and sacrificial
  equivalence are historical/theological claims, not dignity rankings or
  guarantees.

## Bounded ordinary household meaning

A safe ordinary meaning is **begin a modest, reviewable season of steadiness**.
After remembering Vishnu and hearing the source-labelled chapter, choose one
small intention that does not alter food, water, medicine, sleep, sexuality or
another person's conduct: for example, a weekly check-in on an unfinished
responsibility, a short recurring period of quiet, or one reversible reduction
in avoidable waste. Set a review date rather than claiming a four-month vow.

The intention is optional, can be changed or stopped, and creates no merit
competition. It is not a promise to abstain, diet, spend, donate, travel,
withdraw from relationships or delay necessary work.

## Safe ordinary household route

- Confirm the local date and accepted authority; retain Harishayan only as a
  reviewed alias and note the chapter's “Kamika” naming tension.
- Keep normal food variety, water, medicine, hygiene, sleep, bed, consensual
  relationships, work and care. The non-fasting, rested form is complete.
- Remember Vishnu with a familiar name, prayer or silence.
- Hear a bounded chapter-53 account: Shyani, Vishnu's sleep, Vamana and Bali,

- Optionally choose one small, reversible, non-dietary and non-coercive
  intention with a safe review point.
- Close with gratitude and normal care, with no claim of salvation, purity,

No material is required. Optional: an existing Vishnu image/text or a private
reminder. Silence, no image, no writing, no purchase, no flame, no flower, no
leaf and no contact are complete.

## Safety and safeguarding

- Do not begin or intensify fasting. Never change insulin, medicine, meals,
  hydration, infant/child feeding, pregnancy/lactation nutrition, prescribed
  diet, hygiene or sleep.
- Do not stay awake, sleep on the ground, stop using a bed, use fire, collect
  lotus/palasha, eat from an unverified leaf or remove vegetables, curd, milk,
  pulses/legumes or another food group from this ordinary guide.
- Do not impose celibacy, diet, fasting, touch, prayer, silence or a four-month
  vow on a spouse, child, worker, student, patient or dependent. Consent can be
  refused or withdrawn; safeguarding and care continue.
- Do not use Vamana-Bali to justify deceit, pressured consent, land/property
  taking, caste hierarchy, humiliation, restraint or force. Serious disputes
  require present-day legal, safeguarding and accountability routes.
- Do not call a person demon, lower, impure or spiritually unavailable. Do not
  repeat the chapter's caste slur in ordinary consumer copy.
- No heaven, salvation, sin removal, purity, sacrificial merit, success,
  marriage restriction, health benefit or other result is guaranteed.

## Maharashtra Varkari/Ashadhi evidence boundary

- Maharashtra Tourism identifies Ashadhi Ekadashi with Devshayani and describes
  Vitthal devotion and the Pandharpur procession. Some general tourism copy on
  the same page includes unsourced household instructions and guarantees; do
  not import those claims.
- Maharashtra's Directorate of Municipal Administration describes the Dehu and
  Alandi palkhis, Warkari walking, abhang/kirtan and convergence on Pandharpur.
  Its route is year-specific and cannot be copied to 2027 without a live check.
- The Shri Vitthal Rukmini Temple Committee describes Ashadhi as its largest
  Varkari gathering and gives temple-specific pilgrimage context.
- Maharashtra SDMA documents very large crowds and drowning risk around the
  Chandrabhaga/Bhima river. A participation pack must never instruct bathing,
  barefoot long-distance walking or crowd entry; it needs current official
  route, weather, crowd, river, medical and access guidance.
