# Dwadashi Shraddha North/West authority-router research (2027)

## Frozen denominator

This dossier selects exactly the calendar-resolved `dwadashi-shraddha` item.
It is the Pitru Paksha Krishna Dwadashi death-tithi authority route, also called
Baras Shraddha in current practitioner material. It is not:

- Magha Shraddha merely because both occur on 27 September in the reviewed
  2027 New Delhi fixture;
- the Ekadashi, Trayodashi, Purnima or Sarva Pitru route;
- generic Ekadashi-parana or a Vaishnava Dwadashi vrata;
- one national Sannyasi, Vaishnava, Gaya, ghat, temple, priest, sampradaya or
  family procedure.

The lane is shared across the selected North/West and major-state audience only
at the safe authority-router level. Translation, spelling, dress, ordinary food
preference, photograph/no-photograph and familiar prayer/silence do not create
state variants.

## Existing material audit

alongside Magha Shraddha, and correctly refuses to decide ancestor or performer.

selection, Sannyasi/Vaishnava applicability router, complete narrative,
Magha-separation route, formal materials boundary and rejection of large
current benefit claims.

The completed Magha and Sarva Pitru packs remain separate owner lanes. Their
procedures and applicability are not copied into Dwadashi.

## Deterministic timing dependency

The reviewed New Delhi list places **Dwadashi Shraddha on Monday, 27 September
2027**, identified as Ashwina Krishna Dwadashi. It also places Magha Shraddha on
that civil date. The co-occurrence is a routing fact, not a merger.

The deterministic formal resolver requires:

1. the user's city, Pitru Paksha convention and local Krishna Dwadashi interval;
2. the locally calculated aparahna interval for each candidate civil day;
3. Dwadashi's duration within each candidate aparahna, selecting the day with
   the greater Dwadashi-aparahna overlap under the accepted Shraddha authority;
4. explicit authority resolution for a tie, kshaya/vriddhi complication or a
   family/sampradaya exception;
5. current Kutup, Rohina, aparahna, venue and priest/institution schedule only
   for a named formal route.

Never copy 27 September, the Washington 2026 Dwadashi interval or formal
windows, a Dwadashi/ Magha pairing, performer, ancestor, materials or closing to
another city, year or authority. If the deterministic resolver is absent, do
not declare a formal day; private remembrance remains available without timing
pretence.

## Meaning, applicability and attributed claims

The current practitioner page states that Dwadashi Shraddha applies to deceased
family members whose death tithi was Dwadashi in either Shukla or Krishna
Paksha. It separately states that Dwadashi is suitable for someone who had
taken Sannyasa before death, calls the observance Sannyasi Shraddha, associates
it with Vaishnava ancestors, and directs an unknown death date to Sarva Pitru
Amavasya instead.

These are living applicability claims, not automatic Devam classification.
Whether a deceased person was a renunciant, belonged to a Vaishnava lineage, or
falls under a Dwadashi exception requires family knowledge and a named current
authority. Devam never assigns Sannyasa, sect, death tithi, lineage or performer
from a calendar label or name.

The same page names Shraddha, tarpan, pinda, Kutup/Rohina/aparahna, Brahmin
meals, clothes and utensil donations, Vishnu remembrance and Vishnu
Sahasranama. It promises Vishnu-loka, eternal peace, ancestor satisfaction,
peace, prosperity and spiritual progress. These are attributed claims and
owner-specific practices, not generic instructions, obligations, diagnoses or
guarantees.

The consumer lane applies to:

- an individual seeking private remembrance while death-tithi or authority is
  unresolved;
- an opt-in family whose named elder/priest/text says Dwadashi applies to a
  particular ancestor;
- a participant in a current named Dwadashi, Sannyasi or Vaishnava formal rite
  after applicability, role, timing, materials and closing are resolved.

No son, daughter, spouse, widow, widower, adopted person, unmarried person,
childless person, child, disciple or mourner automatically owes performance,
fasting, recitation, feeding, donation, priest payment or ancestor rescue.

## Source-labelled stories

### 1. Dwadashi death-tithi identity

Current material identifies Dwadashi Shraddha through an ancestor's Dwadashi
death tithi during Pitru Paksha and gives Sannyasi/Vaishnava as additional
claimed applicability contexts. The reviewed 2027 New Delhi calendar places it
beside Magha, but Dwadashi remains a tithi-aparahna selection while Magha is a
Nakshatra-aparahna selection. Unknown death date is not silently absorbed into
Dwadashi. This identity story proves no universal performer, formal procedure,
Vishnu-loka, eternal peace or consequence for omission.

### 2. Nachiketa and Yama in the Katha Upanishad

The fixed lawful narrative expression is Friedrich Max Muller's 1884 public-
domain translation of the Katha Upanishad in *Sacred Books of the East*, volume
15.

Vajasravasa gives possessions in a sacrifice. His young son Nachiketa sees that
the cows being given are exhausted and repeatedly asks to whom he will be
given. His angry father says that he gives him to Death. Nachiketa reaches
Yama's abode and waits three nights while Yama is absent. Yama returns and
offers three boons in recompense.

Nachiketa first asks that his father become calm, recognise him and welcome him
without anger. Yama grants this. For the second boon Nachiketa asks about the
fire teaching associated in the text with heaven; Yama teaches it and names it
for him. For the third boon Nachiketa asks what happens after death and observes
that people disagree. Yama offers long life, descendants, wealth, power, music
and pleasures instead. Nachiketa refuses what is temporary. Yama distinguishes
the good from the merely pleasant and teaches about Self, knowledge, discipline
and death. The text closes by saying Nachiketa received the knowledge and
attained its goal.

This is reflection on death and discernment, not Dwadashi Shraddha's origin,
Sannyasi classification or formal vidhi. No child leaves home, approaches
danger, fasts, waits without food/water, obeys abuse, reconciles with an unsafe
parent, handles fire or seeks contact with Yama or a dead person. The story
proves no afterlife fact, Vishnu-loka, ancestor message or guaranteed relief.

## Complete generic routes

1. **private minimum:** resolve the city and Dwadashi-aparahna if possible;
   ask whether a named authority says the death-tithi or Sannyasi/Vaishnava
   context applies; keep normal care; hear both labelled stories; remember
   through a name, value, safe memory or silence; choose one optional living-
   care act or none; close without contact, sect, merit or result claims;
2. **opt-in family remembrance:** separate Dwadashi from same-date Magha and
   unknown-date Sarva Pitru; invite with an easy no; protect grief and privacy;
   retain food, water, medicine and sleep; tell both stories with child and
   afterlife boundaries; allow different or absent memories; use only a
   familiar prayer if wanted; close without performer or Sannyasi assignment;
3. **named formal participant:** identify the current family/priest/text/
   sampradaya/institution; verify death-tithi, renunciant/Vaishnava
   applicability, performer, local Dwadashi-aparahna selection, Kutup/Rohina,
   sequence, materials and venue; bring only requested safe affordable items;
   remain participant-only; retain health, consent, privacy and cost limits;
   use the owner's safe close and copy nothing home.

## Materials and substitutions

Nothing is required for private or family remembrance. Optional supports are a
labelled story, written name, consented photograph, flower, ordinary drinking
water, familiar safe food, private note or supervised electric/light source.
Each can be replaced with a spoken name, safe memory, silence, ordinary care,
electric light or nothing.

Pinda, sesame, kusa, ritual water, tarpan vessels, sacred fire, lamps,
Sahasranama or other recitation texts, Brahmin meals, animal feeding, clothes,
utensils, offerings and dakshina belong only to a named formal authority. The
generic lane supplies no quantity, mantra, preparation, recipient, sequence or
disposal.

## Major variants only

Minor language, spelling, dress, ordinary food preference, photograph/no-
photograph, familiar prayer and silence remain unified.

Separate routes are required only when evidence materially changes:

- ordinary Dwadashi death-tithi versus a named Sannyasi/renunciant or
  Vaishnava applicability rule;
- death-tithi uncertainty, which the current source directs toward a separate
  Sarva Pitru authority route;
- same-date Magha Shraddha and its independent Nakshatra-aparahna rule;
- performer, sequence, recitation, materials, donation and closing under a
  named family, priest, text or sampradaya;
- named Gaya, ghat, river, temple or institution timing, access and safety;
- grief, abuse, estrangement, adoption, lineage, child/dependent, food/
  medicine, water/fire/crowd, privacy, accessibility or cost needs.

A future state route needs evidence of changed timing, applicability,
performer, ordered action, materials, safety or closing—not translation or
popularity alone.

## Safety and non-coercion

- No fast, dehydration, meal delay, medicine change, ritual bath, water entry,
  fire, smoke, crowd, travel, animal feeding, food disposal, recitation,
  donation, purchase or priest payment is required.
- No child or dependent copies Nachiketa's departure, danger, waiting, abusive
  command, unsafe reconciliation or fire. Guardian safety overrides completion.
- No one owes positive memory, forgiveness, family disclosure, belief,
  photograph use, touch, public grief, gotra or unsafe-relative contact.
- Do not classify a deceased person as Sannyasi/Vaishnava, infer death tithi,
  diagnose Pitru Dosha or assign a performer without evidence and authority.
- Dreams, birds, animals, food, water, flame, coincidences and feelings are not
  ancestor messages.
- Do not promise satisfaction, Vishnu-loka, eternal peace, merit, prosperity,
  spiritual progress, health, wealth, progeny or reunion.
- Do not shame or assign duty by gender, kinship, marriage, fertility,
  adoption, sect, discipleship, lineage or participation status.
- Immediate danger uses 112. Medical, water, fire, animal, food,
  safeguarding, venue and legal rules override ritual completion.
