# Holika Dahan North India household and ordinary-participant lane: research dossier v1

Checked: 2026-08-13

## Frozen applicability denominator

This dossier supports exactly one actionable lane: a North Indian household that wants a fire-free remembrance, or an ordinary participant attending an organiser-controlled community or temple Holika Dahan. It does not teach bonfire siting, construction, fuelling, ignition, priest-led Holika puja, homa, circumambulation close to flame, or crowd management. Those require a responsible organiser, local authority and, where applicable, a family or temple ritual authority.

The next-day Holi/Dhulandi colour observance is a separate selected item. West Bengal Dol Jatra/Dol Purnima, Punjab Hola Mohalla, Barsana-area Lathmar Holi and materially different regional or sampradaya fire observances are not state variants of this lane. Exact grains, flowers, prayer words, dress and the presence or absence of a household image are minor differences and do not justify state copies.

## 2027 timing fixture

The Delhi practitioner-calendar fixture is Sunday 21 March 2027. The cited Pradosh window after Bhadra is 18:33-20:55. Purnima begins 18:21 on 21 March and ends 16:13 on 22 March. The same source records a materially different Banaras/North-after-midnight convention at 05:14-06:23 on 22 March. The product must recompute the civil date and permitted interval for the user's location and accepted family/temple Panchang; no displayed clock time is portable. A public event time remains a live organiser fact.

## Story-source separation

- H. H. Wilson's public-domain translation of *Vishnu Purana*, Book I, chapter XVII, supports Prahlada's steadfast devotion, Hiranyakashipu's anger and persecution, repeated attempts to kill the child, and Prahlada's deliverance. The selected chapter does not contain the familiar Holika-in-the-fire episode.
- Incredible India's current festival page records the familiar living Holika account: Hiranyakashipu seeks Prahlada's death with Holika's help; Holika burns and Prahlada survives. Devam labels this as a contemporary festival account and does not retrofit it into the cited Purana chapter.
- The same current page describes families gathering at a symbolic bonfire, sharing stories and prayer, and interpreting it as good overcoming evil or the burning away of negativity. This is descriptive living-practice evidence, not authority for a consumer to throw material into a fire.
- Radha-Krishna colour play on the same page belongs to the next-day Holi/Dhulandi lane and is excluded here.

## Safety and environmental evidence

- Uttar Pradesh Police fire guidance says children must never be left near open fire, matches/lighters must be inaccessible, loose clothing is unsafe near fire, exits must remain known and clear, and people should leave before calling for help rather than re-entering a burning place.
- Delhi Fire Service temporary-structure guidance requires adequate exits and width, clearance from electrical lines and hazards, extinguishers/water, no combustible storage, and segregated cooking. These are organiser duties; the ordinary participant checks visible conditions and leaves if unsafe.
- NCDC/NPCCHH Government of India air-pollution guidance identifies children under five, older adults, pregnant people, and people with respiratory or cardiovascular illness as higher-risk. It advises avoiding open burning of wood, leaves, crop residue and waste, checking AQI, reducing outdoor activity when air is poor, and seeking medical care for breathing difficulty or worsening symptoms.
- Delhi environment policy treats biomass, plastic and garbage burning as air-pollution concerns. Devam therefore never recommends plastic, rubber, painted/treated wood, garbage, aerosols, batteries, fuel or fireworks as ritual material.

## Consumer procedure boundary

The minimum and standard home forms are deliberately fire-free. They ask the user to resolve the day, read one source-labelled account, offer prayer or silence in their own/family-known words, identify one harmful habit or conflict to address, and close with a concrete repair or service action. An optional electric light may mark the moment; a diya is not needed.

The community-participant form asks the user to verify the organiser and time, check AQI and personal risk, locate exits and a safe barrier, supervise children, join only the public story/prayer directed by organisers, and leave without touching the fire or adding material. Any offering is handed only to an authorised organiser if the event explicitly accepts it. Devam supplies no fuel list.

## Major-difference routing

- Fire-free home remembrance versus ordinary attendance: same lane, separate procedures.
- Delhi-style Pradosh timing versus the cited Banaras after-midnight convention: material calendar override; local Panchang authority is required.
- Family-known prayer, grain, flower or symbolic material: minor within-lane choice; no state lane.
- Priest-led Holika puja, homa, bonfire construction/ignition or organiser role: separate authority context, not DIY.
- Holi/Dhulandi colour day: separate selected lane.
- Dol Jatra/Dol Purnima, Hola Mohalla, Lathmar Holi and distinct southern fire traditions: separate observances or applicability lanes only when inventoried and sourced.
