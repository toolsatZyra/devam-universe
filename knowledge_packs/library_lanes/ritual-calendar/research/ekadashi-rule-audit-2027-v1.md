# Ekadashi candidate rule audit 2027

Checked on 2026-08-13 against North-reference, Gujarati, Kannada, Marathi,
Odia and Telugu regional calendars recorded in the v3 census.

## Result

All 41 raw Ekadashi labels are dispositioned. Twenty-four ordinary monthly
instances are retained. Eleven regional labels share the exact 2027 civil date
and paksha/tithi with a base instance:

- Sattila -> Shattila;
- Baruthini -> Varuthini;
- Harishayan -> Devshayani;
- Deva Utthapan -> Devutthana;
- Pankoddhar -> Vijaya;
- Jala Krida -> Apara;
- Kaliyadalan -> Aja;
- Gomati -> Mokshada;
- Pavitra -> Shravana Putrada; and
- Parivartani and Parswa Paribartan -> Parsva.

They remain searchable regional aliases. If later fixed narrative or living-
practice evidence shows a changed deity/story, eligibility, sequence or
closing, the affected alias must be promoted to a separate applicability lane.

Gauna Kamika, Gauna Pausha Putrada, Vaishnava Kamika, Vaishnava Papanasini,
Vaishnava Pausha Putrada and Vaishnava Rama are retained as distinct contexts.
The qualifier and, in several cases, next-day date change what a user needs to
know; a generic Ekadashi answer must not override it.

## Limits

The calendar evidence supports identity, date comparison and tithi/paksha only.
It does not establish fasting eligibility, grain restrictions, medication
advice, parana windows, mantras, stories, temple programmes or promised
outcomes. Those require scoped sources and deterministic local timing.
