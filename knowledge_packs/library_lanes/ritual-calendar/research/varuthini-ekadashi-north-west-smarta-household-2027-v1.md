## What the fixed chapter does and does not contain

The reviewed N. A. Deshpande English expression of *Padma Purana*, Uttara
Khanda chapter 48, lines 54-66, is Yudhishthira's question and Krishna's answer.
It identifies Varuthini and attributes worldly and other-worldly happiness,
good fortune, removal of sin, desired objects, salvation and heaven to the vow.
It names Mandhatri, Dhundhumara and Shiva only as brief examples of attributed
results; it does not narrate their episodes.

The chapter then ranks gifts: horses, elephants, land, sesame, gold, food,
daughter/cow and knowledge. It says food satisfies ancestors, gods and people,
and calls knowledge the best gift. It uses a historical patriarchal formulation
in which a father "gives" an adorned daughter in marriage. It condemns taking
money for giving a daughter, attaching severe karmic consequences. Finally it
lists ten restrictions on Dashami, a longer Ekadashi list, another Dwadashi
list, fasting, vigil and Vishnu worship, followed by promised results.

Important negative finding: the reviewed chapter contains **no bear attack,
lost leg or bodily restoration narrative about King Mandhata**. That popular
account must not be silently imported into this source expression. A future
story owner may research its actual source separately.

Consumer treatment must preserve the tensions:

- The chapter's promised protection, desired objects, prosperity, sin removal,
  heaven and salvation are theological source claims, not guarantees.
- A woman, daughter, spouse or any person is not property, a gift, a transfer of
  guardianship, a source of family merit or a means to obtain ritual results.
  Marriage requires the free consent and equal dignity of the adults involved.
- The text's rejection of taking money in connection with giving a daughter in
  marriage can be retained as an anti-transaction warning, but not as approval
  of treating her as a gift or controlling her marriage.
- India Code states that the Dowry Prohibition Act, 1961 prohibits giving or
  taking dowry and separately penalises demanding dowry. Devam does not give
  case-specific legal advice; pressure, demands, threats or violence require
  qualified legal/safety support.
- Food and knowledge are useful themes only when consent-led, safe and wanted.
  Do not donate allergens, unsuitable food, misinformation, religious pressure
  or unwanted advice. A recipient never owes conversion, gratitude, access,
  labour, a vote, secrecy or relationship consideration.
- The detailed food, sex, sleep, tooth-cleaning, travel, exercise, social-contact
  and vigil restrictions are source facts, not a universal home checklist.
  Devam supplies a complete non-fasting, normally fed, hydrated and rested form.
- Do not turn the source's labels for people into social exclusion, caste
  policing, shaming, isolation or denial of ordinary care.

## Bounded present-day meaning

A safe ordinary meaning is **protect dignity without making care transactional**.
Choose one bounded action that reduces a preventable harm or shares something
useful without controlling the recipient: check an emergency contact, correct
unsafe information, offer suitable food after asking, share a reliable public
resource, remove a small household hazard, respect a relationship boundary, or
refuse participation in a coercive marriage demand. The other person may say
no, and no spiritual or social debt is created.

Do not ask a participant to investigate a marriage, confront an unsafe family,
provide legal advice, disclose abuse, donate money, fast, travel or contact
someone. Where there is dowry pressure, forced marriage, violence, threat,
medical danger or safeguarding risk, prioritise immediate safety and qualified
support; a devotional action cannot replace them.

## Safe ordinary household route

- Confirm the local date and authority.
- Choose normal food, water, medicine, hygiene, movement and sleep; the
  non-fasting form is complete.
- Remember Vishnu with a familiar name, prayer or silence.
- Hear a bounded account explicitly labelled Padma Purana chapter 48. Label the
  benefits and restriction lists as source claims, name its patriarchal
  daughter-as-gift language, and say the reviewed chapter has no bear/leg story.
- Choose one consent-safe dignity-protecting action with no created debt.
- Close with gratitude, normal food/medicine/rest and no claim of protection,
  merit, prosperity, desired objects, bodily restoration or legal resolution.

No material is required. Optional: an existing Vishnu image/text, ordinary safe
food for oneself, or a verified public resource. Silence, no image, no donation,
no writing and no purchase are complete.

## Safety and safeguarding

- A non-fasting form is complete. Never change insulin, medicine, meals,
  hydration, hygiene, sleep, exercise or safe travel from this guide.
- No vigil, sexual restriction, tooth-cleaning restriction, isolation, donation,
  marriage discussion, private meeting, contact or travel is required.
- No woman or child is a gift. Do not arrange, pressure, advertise or negotiate
  a marriage through this route. Adult marriage consent must be free; a child
  or vulnerable person requires safeguarding, not ritual negotiation.
- Do not give, demand, negotiate, conceal or facilitate dowry. Do not tell a
  person facing threats or violence to confront others alone; seek qualified
  safety and legal support. Devam does not decide whether a specific item is
  legally dowry or provide case advice.
- Ask before food or information sharing. Check allergies, dietary needs,
  storage, hygiene, expiry and recipient preference. Give no medical diet,
  supplement, cure claim or misinformation.
- Do not shame poverty or pressure donation. A gift creates no access, labour,
  gratitude, secrecy, religious participation, marriage or relationship debt.
- Do not exclude, insult or deny care to anyone based on the source's social or
  purity labels.
- No protection, armour, good fortune, desired object, sin removal, heaven,
  salvation, fertility, marriage, cure, restored limb/body, wealth or legal
  outcome is guaranteed.

## Major-variant decisions

- **Within lane:** language; image/no image; safe food/no offering; verified
  resource/no resource; familiar prayer/silence; individual/family; non-fast or
  already inherited clinically safe fast. These minor differences do not create
  state lanes.
- **Separate authority:** the chapter's full Dashami-Ekadashi-Dwadashi food,
  hygiene, sleep, movement, sex, vigil, mantra and parana regimen.
- **Separate story expression:** the bear attack/lost-leg/restoration account;
  it is absent from the reviewed chapter and needs its own source review.
- **Separate legal/safety route:** dowry demand, forced marriage, child marriage,
  domestic violence, threat or coercion. Ritual may accompany but never replace
  qualified help.
- **Separate institution:** a named temple's current programme, priest-led rite,
  access, safeguarding, fees or offerings.
- **Separate named observance:** Kamada, Mohini or another Ekadashi.

No major state-specific difference was evidenced for this ordinary household
lane across the reviewed North/West, Maharashtra, Odisha, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil
and Kannada/Telugu fixtures. Month labels differ under purnimanta/amanta systems
but the reviewed civil date agrees; minor language, food and family expression
are not separate variants.
