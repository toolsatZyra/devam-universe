# Vaishnava Kamika: India authority-router research (2027)

Checked 2026-08-13. This evidence freeze scopes the separately accepted
`ekadashi-kamika-vaishnava` denominator item. It does not create one ritual for
all Vaishnavas and does not reopen or count ordinary or Gauna Kamika.

## Applicability decision

“Vaishnava” is not a sufficient practice authority. The consumer lane must
first identify the user's named sampradaya, inherited family calendar or
institution. ISKCON/Gaudiya, Sri Vaishnava, Madhva, Ramanandi, Vallabha,
Swaminarayan and other Vaishnava communities cannot be treated as one
nationwide fast, food, mantra, parana or temple-liturgy lane.

The complete router applies when the user's accepted named Vaishnava authority
explicitly returns **Vaishnava Kamika Ekadashi**. It provides:

- a deterministic authority-selection check;
- the reviewed 2027 New Delhi/Indian discovery fixture as non-portable evidence;
- the shared, source-labelled Kamika chapter context;
- a complete fed, rested, material-free Vishnu/Krishna remembrance; and
- separate routes for each formal sampradaya, family or institution rule.

It never says that a user becomes eligible for the Vaishnava date by choosing
it, visiting an ISKCON page, living in a particular state or preferring the
later day.

## Current calendar and authority evidence

### Current 2027 discovery fixture

DrikPanchang's ISKCON-profile page for New Delhi places ISKCON Kamika on
**Friday, 30 July 2027**, with a location-specific 31 July parana window and
Ekadashi tithi coordinates:
<https://www.drikpanchang.com/iskcon/ekadashi/kamika/kamika-ekadashi-date-parana-time.html?geoname-id=1261481&year=2027>

The reviewed general Indian regional pages place ordinary Kamika on 29 July
and label both Gauna and Vaishnava Kamika on 30 July. This establishes a
material 2027 date/authority distinction but does not prove that every
Vaishnava authority in India selects 30 July.

The final pack may state 30 July as a reviewed New Delhi/Indian discovery
fixture. It must not copy the published parana window, tithi clock times, food
rules or date to another city, institution, sampradaya or year.

### Official ISKCON/GBC authority evidence

- ISKCON's official festivals page states that Vaishnava festival dates use a
  lunar calendar and therefore vary year to year:
  <https://iskcon.org/festival/>
- the official 2001 GBC resolution records that ISKCON used a multi-calendar
  system, that differences with Gaudiya Math calculations caused confusion,
  and that a Vaishnava Calendar Committee was assigned to clarify and improve
  VCAL:
  <https://gbc.iskcon.org/2001/>
- the official 2013 GBC resolution records that GCal was written in
  consultation with the GBC Vaishnava Calendar Committee, had been used as a
  dependable calculation system and was owned by the GBC West Bengal society:
  <https://gbc.iskcon.org/gbc_res/GBCRES13.html>
- ISKCON Bangalore's official 2026–2027 calendar demonstrates that its named
  institutional calendar can select “Dvadasi (Fasting for Ekadashi vrata)” and
  provides institution-specific closing labels. Its published range ends in
  March 2027 and therefore does not establish July 2027 Kamika for Bangalore:
  <https://www.iskconbangalore.org/vaishnava-calendar/>

These official sources support a named-authority router and warn against a
generic national rule. They do not establish every Vaishnava sampradaya's
current calendar, food rules, mantra, fasting eligibility, parana or temple
programme.

## Fixed Kamika source context

The reviewed N. A. Deshpande English expression of *Padma Purana*, Uttara
Khanda, chapter 54 remains the fixed named-Kamika mahatmya source:
<https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/the-padma-purana/d/doc365631.html>

It is a nested Yudhishthira–Krishna–Brahma–Narada dialogue, not a developed
origin story. Brahma names Kamika and praises Hari, Vishnu, Madhusudana and
Krishna. The chapter compares its attributed result with sacrifice,
pilgrimage bathing, land, cow, gold, silver and gems; praises overnight vigil,
Tulasi seeing, touching, saluting, sprinkling, planting and offering; praises a
continuous ghee or sesame-oil lamp; and includes Yama, ancestor, caste/birth,
killing-a-Brahmana and abortion language. It attributes cure, purity,
absolution, heaven, salvation and Vishnu's world.

The chapter does not state an ISKCON, Gaudiya, Sri Vaishnava, Madhva,
Ramanandi, Vallabha, Swaminarayan or generic Vaishnava calendar algorithm. It
cannot derive the 30 July date, parana, food rule, mantra or institutional
liturgy. Chapter 53's two bright-half “Kamika” labels remain source-expression
tension only.

## Consumer meaning

The safe bounded reflection is: **follow a trustworthy named authority without
turning devotion into expensive display or coercion**. This is a Devam
synthesis of the calendar evidence and chapter comparisons, not a universal
Vaishnava doctrine.

The lane may name Vishnu/Krishna remembrance because both the fixed Kamika
chapter and reviewed Vaishnava/ISKCON evidence support that focus. It may not
select one mantra, diet, fast, Tulasi procedure, lamp, vigil or parana for all
Vaishnavas.

## Complete actionable routes

### Minimum authority-routed home form

1. Name the accepted sampradaya, inherited family calendar or institution and
   confirm that it explicitly returns Vaishnava Kamika for the user's city.
2. If authority is unknown or sources conflict, stop the formal route and use
   the complete safe fallback; do not choose between 29 and 30 July by
   convenience.
3. Keep normal food, water, prescribed medicine, insulin, child feeding,
   hygiene and sleep. Begin no fast, food restriction or parana.
4. Remember Vishnu/Krishna with a familiar family expression, silence or no
   image.
5. Hear the bounded chapter-54 mahatmya and state that it contains no developed
   origin story or generic Vaishnava calculation rule.
6. Optionally keep one modest, no-purchase care commitment within the person's
   responsibility; no Tulasi or plant action is required.
7. Close with normal care and no cure, absolution, ancestor or salvation claim.

### Consent-led family form

The family identifies the same named authority and uses only rules it can
actually verify. Anyone may decline, eat, drink, take medicine, sleep, avoid
source language, remain silent or leave without explanation. No one discloses
health, pregnancy, abortion, miscarriage, fertility, caste or family history.
The generic pack does not calculate parana or judge whose observance is more
authentic.

### Named institution participation

Verify the institution's official current calendar and live public programme,
including access, food, plant, flame, photography, donation, crowd and
safeguarding rules. Attend only as a voluntary public participant. Do not copy
its liturgy, mantra, food restrictions, Tulasi handling, lamp, vigil or parana
into household instructions. A remote, cancelled or home fallback is complete.

## Materials and substitutions

No material is required. Optional existing supports are a familiar
Vishnu/Krishna image or text and a private reminder. Each has a no-image,
no-writing and silent substitution. Do not require or source Tulasi, leaves,
plants, ghee, sesame oil, lamps, flames, food, pilgrimage water, gifts,
donations or paid items.

## Safety, dignity and accountability

- A complete authority-router fallback is non-fasting, fed, hydrated, rested,
  no-vigil, no-touch, no-flame, no-purchase, no-Tulasi, no-plant, no-leaf,
  no-disclosure, image-free, writing-free and material-free.
- Do not select the Vaishnava date from a generic label, convenience, state,
  ethnicity, dress, surname or temple visit.
- Do not publish or calculate parana from this pack.
- Do not pluck, touch, ingest, apply, sprinkle, plant, buy, water or distribute
  Tulasi from this lane. Ritual does not diagnose or cure disease.
- Do not stay awake overnight or light a day-and-night lamp.
- Never ask about pregnancy, abortion, miscarriage, fertility, sexual history
  or medical records; do not shame or obstruct lawful confidential care.
- Do not rank dignity or authenticity by caste, birth, family, sampradaya,
  initiation, occupation, wealth, observance, disability, health or
  reproductive history.
- Serious killing, violence, abuse or other harm is not erased by ritual,
  hearing, Tulasi, lamp, vigil, fasting, initiation or regret. Preserve
  accountability, qualified care and law.
- Do not promise disease removal, bodily purification, calamity prevention,
  good birth, ancestor benefit, sin removal, absolution, heaven, salvation or
  Vishnu's world.

The ICMR *Guidelines for Management of Type 1 Diabetes Mellitus* ground the
no-fasting-without-clinical-guidance boundary. The Medical Termination of
Pregnancy Act, 1971, including section 5A confidentiality, grounds the
reproductive-privacy boundary. Neither is individual medical or legal advice.

dress and voluntary participation inside the router.

- another source story or observance.

State copies are forbidden unless state evidence also proves a changed
authority, eligibility, ordered practice, story/deity focus, close or material
safety boundary.
