# Monthly Purnima batch: eleven-lane evidence matrix (2027)

Checked 2026-08-13. This evidence-first batch covers the eleven accepted
`monthly-purnima-recurring` items still incomplete after Bhadrapada Purnima:
Pausha, Magha, Phalguna, Chaitra, Vaishakha, Jyeshtha, Ashadha, Shravana,
Ashwina, Kartika and Margashirsha Purnima.

This matrix does not count a lane as authored, projected, hosted, independently
reviewed, released or published. Named festival, vrata, deity, Buddhist/Jain/
Sikh, Vedic-fire, Shraddha and institutional overlays remain separate even
when they share the civil date or tithi.

## Shared meaning and source-labelled stories

### 1. A named monthly full-moon identity

Each lane is the Shukla Purnima instance carrying that lunar-month name under
the user's accepted calendar convention. It marks the completion of the waxing
fortnight in that convention. Amanta/purnimanta vocabulary, South-Indian
Pournami terminology, fasting selection and same-day named observances can
change the user-facing answer. The tithi label does not establish one national
deity, fast, altar, bath, mantra, donation, Satyanarayana procedure, temple
programme or promised result.

### 2. Soma, Rohini, Daksha and Prabhasa

The fixed lawful narrative expression is K. M. Ganguli's public-domain English
Mahabharata, Shalya Parva section 35, checked against the Kumbhaghonam Sanskrit
mirror. Inside Baladeva's pilgrimage, Janamejaya asks why Soma lost and regained
splendour. Vaishampayana says Daksha gave twenty-seven daughters, associated
with constellations and time-reckoning, to Soma. Soma favoured Rohini and
neglected the others. They complained; Daksha twice told Soma to treat them
equally, but Soma continued. Daksha cursed Soma with wasting illness; herbs and
creatures declined as Soma waned. The gods appealed. Daksha prescribed a
partial resolution at Prabhasa, where Soma honoured Mahadeva and recovered his
cool rays; the account connects this with waxing and waning.

This is a lunar-change story in an epic-tirtha context—not the origin of any
monthly Purnima or a consumer vidhi. Divine polygamy, unequal affection,
command, curse, illness, sacred-water action and cosmic consequences are story
events. They do not require marriage, equal intimacy, reconciliation, cure,
travel, water entry, obedience or staying in an unsafe relationship. Illness is
never punishment, and a neglected partner is never pressured to remain.

## Shared living-practice evidence and safe answer

The current practitioner list associates full-moon days with Purnima fasting,
Pournami Vratam and Satyanarayana Puja, while explicitly warning that the
Satyanarayana or fasting day can fall one day before the listed actual Purnima.
That proves distinct living routes; it does not authorise one universal fast or
puja. Existing current material also shows holy-dip, moon-viewing, charity,
named deity and temple forms. Each needs its own source, eligibility, timing,
materials and safety when the user actually names it.

The complete generic monthly answer is:

1. resolve the local month-named Shukla Purnima and disambiguate actual Purnima
   from vrat/Satyanarayana and same-day named owners;
2. keep ordinary food, water, medicine, insulin, sleep and hygiene; start no
   fast, vigil, water entry, flame, donation or travel from this lane;
3. hear the source-labelled calendar identity and complete bounded Soma story;
4. state that relationship command, curse, illness and sacred-water action are
   narrative events, not instructions or diagnoses;
5. optionally use silence, a familiar family prayer, safe ground-level/window
   moon notice, or no moon viewing at all;
6. optionally choose one affordable care/service act or none;
7. close with ordinary food, hydration, medicine and rest and no result claim.

An opt-in family can use the same sequence with an easy no, no-photo option,
no compulsory belief/disclosure/touch and a safe shared-food or care close. A
named temple or community participant first verifies the current owner,
programme identity, schedule, access, offering, food/fire/water/crowd rules and
closing, remains participant-only and copies nothing home.

## Materials and substitutions

Nothing is required for the generic lane. Optional supports are the labelled
story, a note, familiar prayer, flower, ordinary drinking water, safe food or
electric light. Each may be replaced with a spoken thought, silence, safe
memory, ordinary care or nothing.

Fasting items, kalasha, image, panchamrita, prasada, mantra text, sacred fire,
ritual bath, river/sea offering, moon offering, formal donation, priest fee and
temple materials belong to named owners. No quantity, mantra, preparation,
recipient or disposal sequence is inferred here.

## Major variants only

Every lane keeps language, Purnima/Pournami spelling, dress, ordinary food,
familiar deity name, image/no-image, prayer/silence and state popularity inside
one route. A separate variant is justified only by a material change in:

- actual Purnima versus Purnima-vrat/Satyanarayana timing;
- named festival, vrata, religious or deity-owner applicability;
- performer/participant eligibility or initiation;
- formal ordered actions, materials, fire/food/water rules or closing;
- live temple, ghat, mela, river, shore or community access/schedule;
- medical, fasting, pregnancy, child/dependent, disability, grief, water,
  height, flame, crowd, travel or financial safety.

No reviewed evidence establishes a generic state-by-state monthly-Purnima vidhi
for West Bengal, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala,
Maharashtra, Odisha or Andhra Pradesh. Their materially named observances are
routed to separate owners rather than converted into translated copies.

## Safety and non-coercion

- No fasting, dehydration, meal delay, medicine change, vigil, ritual bath,
  river/pond/sea entry, roof/ledge, isolated viewing, flame, telescope, travel,
  crowd exposure, donation, purchase or priest payment is required.
- Children/dependents retain normal care and guardian safety; anyone may stop.
- Never frame illness, infertility, poverty, grief, relationship conflict or a
  lunar phase as punishment.
- Never promise cure, children, marital fortune, prosperity, ancestor
  liberation, sin removal, multiplied merit or wish fulfilment.
- The Soma story does not authorise coercion, forced equality of intimacy,
  stalking, forced reconciliation, parent control, caste hierarchy or ritual
  exclusion.
- Moon visibility is not a spiritual test; astronomy equipment is unnecessary.
- Immediate danger uses 112. Medical, water, fire, food, crowd, weather,
  safeguarding and institutional rules override ritual completion.
