# Kanni Sankramam Kerala Malayalam-solar household research (2027)

## Frozen denominator and non-invention boundary

This dossier selects exactly `Kanni Sankramam` in the accepted
`solar-ingress-kanya` family and `malayalam_solar` applicability context. The
frozen solar disposition records a material **Malayalam solar-month identity
and date rule**, while explicitly saying that no separate household action is
inferred. This lane therefore does not invent a Kerala-wide puja.

The already reviewed North/shared Kanya pack is reusable for a safe,
material-free solar-transition remembrance and its optional source-labelled
Aditya Hridaya story. It is not reusable as Kanni timing authority. Bengal
Vishwakarma Puja, Shraddha, named temples and formal Surya rites remain separate.

## Meaning and source-labelled stories

### Malayalam solar-month transition

Kanni Sankramam names the transition from Chingam to Kanni in the Malayalam
sidereal solar calendar used in Kerala. The calendrical story is the opening of
a new solar month and a visible way of organising seasonal/civic time. The
label does not establish one Kerala household altar, deity, fast, temple,
offering or promised result.

Government/academic calendar material describes the Malayalam/Kollam calendar
as a Kerala solar-calendar context, while historical calendar material notes
the importance of ascertaining the exact Sankranti day. This supports regional
calendar identity, not a universal puja.

### Optional Aditya Hridaya source story

The reusable fixed story is Valmiki Ramayana, Yuddha Kanda 6.107, from the IIT
Kanpur Sanskrit and translation portal. Rama is exhausted and thoughtful on
the battlefield while Ravana stands ready. Agastya approaches and teaches the
Aditya Hridaya hymn, praising Surya through cosmic functions and source-level
claims about victory, enemy-removal, sorrow, sin and longevity. Rama listens,
recovers composure, recites and returns to battle; Surya encourages him toward
Ravana's defeat.

This is a battle-context Surya story, not Kanni Sankramam's origin and not a
generic mantra instruction. No real person or group is Ravana, an enemy, demon,
darkness or obstacle. Devam promises no victory, cure, longevity,
sorrow-removal or enemy-removal and never asks a user to identify or defeat a
real person.

## Safe complete route

The private and family route reuses only the generic solar-transition core:

1. resolve the Kerala city, Chingam-to-Kanni ingress and accepted calendar;
2. distinguish Kanni from Kanya, Vishwakarma, Shraddha and named formal rites;
3. keep normal food, water, medicine, sleep, shade and child care;
4. hear the Malayalam calendar identity and optional labelled Aditya story;
5. state that the battle results are source claims, not promises or real-person
   metaphors;
6. optionally use silence, a familiar Surya name/prayer, daylight indoors or a
   safe shaded place—never direct Sun viewing;
7. notice one transition and choose one small affordable care action or none;
8. close with ordinary care and no required material or result.

The family form adds explicit opt-in, bilingual telling, easy exit and no
compelled belief/disclosure/touch. The temple route is visitor-only: identify
the institution, verify its current official schedule and eligibility, remain
in authorised areas, obey heat/fire/water/crowd rules and copy no institutional
procedure home.

## Materials and substitutions

No material is required. Optional familiar items may include a story summary,
chair, daylight, flower, water or supervised lamp. Every object can be replaced
with silence, an electric light, ordinary drinking water, a spoken sentence or
nothing. No new image, Malayalam mantra, puja set, donation or food is inferred.

## Major variants only

Minor language, spelling, dress, food preference, familiar Surya name,
image/no-image and silence stay inside this lane.

Separate routes are required for:

- North/shared Kanya Sankranti because its accepted calendar/date authority is
  different even when 2027 civil dates happen to match;
- Bengal Vishwakarma Puja because workplace, tools, machinery, organiser and
  shutdown/restart safety materially change the procedure;
- Shraddha because death-tithi, performer and family/priest authority change
  applicability;
- a named temple or formal Surya rite because live schedule, mantra, punya-kala,
  fire/water/food rules and closing belong to that authority;
- medical, disability, child, heat, sun, water, fall, fire, crowd, travel or
  financial needs that modify or stop participation.

## Safety

- Never look directly at the Sun or use binoculars, telescopes, cameras,
  magnifiers, smoked glass or improvised filters.
- No fasting, dehydration, meal delay, medicine change, vigil, roof, water
  entry, flame, crowd, travel or donation is required.
- No battle enactment, real-person enemy label, coercive prayer, Sanskrit,
  Malayalam recitation, touch, disclosure, forgiveness or belief is required.
- No ritual guarantees victory, cure, longevity, sorrow-removal, prosperity,
  safety, reconciliation or merit.
- Immediate emergencies use 112; current medical and institutional rules
  override the pack.
