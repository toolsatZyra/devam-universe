## Consumer and safety boundary

Normal food, water, medicine, insulin, sleep and clinical care outrank fasting.
No gold, conch, gourd, fruit, root, priest payment, special leaf, flame, smoke,
moon avoidance, vow, mantra, donation or promised result is required. Stories
do not blame illness, infertility, menstruation, bereavement, pests, caste,
sexual violence or misfortune on omitted worship. Animal control must be lawful
and humane. Immediate danger in India routes to 112.
