## Fixed source-labelled account

The reviewed N. A. Deshpande English expression of *Padma Purana*, Uttara
Khanda chapter 44, lines 54-77, frames a nested narration: Yudhishthira asks
Krishna; Krishna reports Narada asking Brahma; Brahma tells a Rama account.
After Sita's abduction, Jatayu's death, alliance with Sugriva and Hanuman's
return from Lanka, Rama asks how the army can cross the ocean. Lakshmana directs
him to the sage Bakadalbhya. The sage prescribes Vijaya Ekadashi and the chapter
attributes Rama's crossing and victory to its observance.

The chapter's ordered formal rite begins on Dashami. It specifies a decorated
gold, silver, copper or clay water-filled pitcher with sprouts, seven grains,
barley and a golden Narayana image; worship with betel nut, coconut, sandal,
incense, lamps, food and stories; an all-night vigil; then taking the jar to a
river or stream and giving it with great gifts to a Veda-learned Brahmana on
Dvadashi. It promises victory, success, heaven, sin destruction and sacrificial
merit.

Consumer treatment must preserve rather than silently modernise those facts:

- Sita's abduction is violence against her; it is never her fault, a romance,
  or a model for relationships.
- The war, killing and conquest are narrative events, not consumer advice to
  defeat an enemy, retaliate, dominate or bypass law.
- The detailed pot/grain/gold-image/vigil/river/gift sequence is a formal
  textual rite, not a universal household minimum and not inferred from model
  memory.
- The text's recipient hierarchy and great gifts are source facts, not a rule
  that useful giving must go to one caste or that a household should incur
  expense or debt.
- No ritual guarantees victory in litigation, exams, elections, business,
  medicine, relationships, war, travel, immigration or any other outcome.

## Bounded present-day meaning

A safe ordinary household reflection is **courage with preparation rather than
guaranteed conquest**: name one legitimate obstacle, seek qualified advice,
choose one lawful next step and protect people affected by the plan. Vishnu or
Rama remembrance may accompany the action, but prayer does not replace consent,
evidence, professional advice, emergency response or law.

Do not ask the user to identify a human "enemy." Do not promise that an outcome
will be won. Where conflict exists, use de-escalation, documentation, lawful
process, safeguarding and qualified help. No one must forgive, reconcile with,
contact or return to an unsafe person or authority.

## Safe ordinary household route

- Confirm the local date and the ordinary Smarta/Vijaya authority.
- Choose a safe, non-fasting remembrance of Vishnu or Rama; silence and no
  image are complete.
- Hear a short account explicitly labelled Padma Purana chapter 44. State that
  the formal rite and attributed victory are textual claims, not today's
  universal instructions or guarantees.
- Name one legitimate obstacle without naming another person an enemy.
- Write or mentally choose one lawful, proportionate next step and one safety
  check: ask an expert, gather a document, make a plan, request support, pause an
  unsafe action or use an authorised grievance/emergency route.
- Close with gratitude, food/water/medicine/rest as normally needed, and carry
  out only the safe action within the user's control.

Materials can be none. Optional familiar items are an existing Vishnu/Rama
image or text, a small clean vessel kept at home, water, a flower/fruit, and
paper. Do not require a new metal vessel, gold image, seven grains, barley,
sprouts, betel, coconut, incense, flame, overnight vigil, river trip or gift.

## Safety and environmental boundaries

- A non-fasting form is complete. Children, pregnant or breastfeeding people,
  older adults, people with diabetes or another condition, people taking
  medicines with food, people with eating-disorder history, and people doing
  heat/heavy labour must not start or intensify fasting from Devam. Never alter
  insulin, medicine, meals or hydration for this guide.
- No vigil is required. Stop before fatigue; sleep deprivation is not
  preparation, courage or devotion.
- No river, stream, sea, pond, ghat or sanctum visit is required. Do not enter
  water, lean over an edge, leave a child unsupervised, or place/abandon a pot,
  grain, image, metal, plastic, food, oil, ash or other material in water.
  Government of India drowning-prevention guidance requires caregiver
  supervision for children's water activity and warns that drowning can be
  quick and silent.
- Keep an optional vessel at home, reuse it normally, or give a genuinely useful
  item directly with consent. Do not call littering or unsafe immersion a
  textual requirement.
- No flame is needed. Use daylight/electric light or none; supervise and fully
  extinguish any chosen flame. Do not leave a lamp burning overnight.
- Charity must be voluntary, affordable, useful and requested; it must not cause
  debt. Caste, gender, purity, reciprocal blessing or ritual access must not be
  conditions of basic care.
- Sita's abduction and the story's violence may be unsuitable for a child or a
  survivor today. Ask consent, offer a short non-graphic version and do not
  demand disclosure.
- For legal, medical, financial, workplace, relationship or safety problems,
  use qualified advice and emergency/grievance routes. Ritual never certifies a
  strategy or predicted result.

## Major-variant decisions

- **Within lane:** language; ordinary flower/fruit; safe vessel/no vessel;
  image/no image; Vishnu/Rama familiar prayer or silence; individual/family;
  non-fast or an already inherited clinically safe fast.
- **Separate authority:** a formal Dashami-to-Dvadashi pot, seven-grain, golden-
  image, vigil, river and recipient-specific gift rite.
- **Separate authority:** any Gauna/Vaishnava date or parana rule that differs
  materially from the user's ordinary Smarta authority.
- **Separate institution:** a named temple's live schedule, priest-led liturgy,
  crowd controls, offerings, access or fees.
- **Separate Odisha institution lane:** Pankoddhar Ekadashi at Sri Lokanath
  Temple, Puri. It is a Shaiva sanctum-water-removal and annual darshan event,
  not an ordinary Vijaya household alias. Do not copy its institutional action
  into a home or advise touching/consuming sanctum water or decomposed offerings.
- **Separate named observance:** Amalaki, Papamochani or another Ekadashi.
