## Fixed lawful narrative spines

### Full-moon lanes: Soma, Rohini, Daksha and Prabhasa

The reviewed public-domain Ganguli expression of Mahabharata, Shalya Parva 35
supplies the complete Soma waxing/waning account. Soma favours Rohini over
Daksha's other daughters; they complain, Daksha orders equal conduct, Soma
ignores the warning and is cursed with wasting. As creatures and herbs decline,
the gods appeal. Daksha offers a partial resolution at Prabhasa, where Soma
recovers splendour and thereafter waxes and wanes. It is a story of lunar change
and unequal treatment, not the origin of Dol, Chitra, Madana, Gamha or Sharad,
and not a command to marry, offer equal intimacy, reconcile, bathe or seek a
cure.

<https://www.ibiblio.org/sripedia/ebooks/mb/m09/m09035.htm>

### New-moon/ancestor lanes: Nachiketa and Yama

Max Muller's public-domain Katha Upanishad supplies the reviewed death-question
story. Vajasravasa gives worn cows; Nachiketa repeatedly questions him and is
angrily assigned to Death. After waiting at Yama's home, he receives three
boons: reconciliation with his father, fire teaching and an answer to the
disputed question of death. He rejects wealth, longevity and pleasure; Yama
distinguishes the good from the merely pleasant and teaches the Self. The child
danger, waiting, fire and reconciliation are not actions to copy. The story
proves no afterlife fact, ancestor message, regional Amavasya origin or Shraddha
procedure.

<https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Sacred_Books_of_the_East/Volume_15/Katha-upanishad>

## Material living-practice differences

- **Dol:** use the existing Holi/Dol owner only for colour, procession, deity,
  food, crowd and consent practice. A material-free story/song/listening route
  is complete; no colour application or temple attendance is inferred.
- **Chitra:** current practitioner material associates Chitragupta, records of
  deeds, Shiva/Madurai and Murugan contexts. Use these as attributable living
  traditions, not proof of divine surveillance, sin scoring or punishment.
- **Savitri:** reuse the Mahabharata Savitri-Satyavan source owner. No wife,
  woman or spouse must fast, save a partner, remain married, accept abuse or
  guarantee longevity.
- **Pithori:** current practitioner material associates mothers, child welfare,
  sixty-four Yoginis, Sapta Matrikas and flour/cow-dung figures. These require a
  formal current owner. The complete route requires no pregnancy, motherhood,
  child disclosure, fast, flour image, cow dung or feeding of ranked recipients.
- **Sharad:** existing official/current material supports attributable Lakshmi,
  Krishna-Raas, harvest, music, milk and community contexts. Milk requires
  allergy, refrigeration and time/temperature safety; moonlight is not a cure;
  vigil is never required; moon visibility does not determine success.
- **Aadi/Thai/Mahalaya:** ancestor remembrance is a scoped living context, but
  formal tarpana, pinda, sesame/kusha, water entry, performer eligibility and
  priest/family close remain named-authority routes.
- **Gamha/Madana:** the accepted names are retained, but reviewed evidence does
  not justify importing Balabhadra/Raksha/cattle or Madana vrata instructions.
  These are explicit owner routes, not missing text filled from memory.
- **Mauni/Somavati:** silence is voluntary and interruptible; no speech
  deprivation, isolation, fasting, holy dip, tree-circling, marital or fertility
  promise is generic.

## Major variants only

Language, spelling, dress, ordinary food, image/no-image and prayer/silence do
not create state variants. Timing authority, ancestor performer, named deity or
story owner, formal fast, water/animal/fire/food/crowd exposure, eligibility and
closing do.

## Acceptance boundary

Reject one national full-/new-moon puja; copied dates or clocks; compulsory
fasting, vigil, silence, dip, tarpana, mantra, donation, milk, moon viewing,
colour or animal contact; wife/mother/child duty; divine surveillance; ancestor
or afterlife certainty; cure, longevity, fertility, protection, absolution or
prosperity guarantees; and cosmetic state variants.

This matrix does not count a lane as authored. Authored does not mean projected,
hosted, independently culturally reviewed or released. All 19,480 day cells
remain unprojected.
