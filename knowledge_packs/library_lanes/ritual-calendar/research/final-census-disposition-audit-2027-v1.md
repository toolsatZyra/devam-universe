# Final raw-census disposition audit 2027

Reviewed on 2026-08-13. This batch dispositions the final 133 labels from the
425-label regional calendar census.

## Rules

A named observance is conservatively accepted for research unless evidence
supports a narrower outcome. Acceptance is not a claim that a puja exists, that
all users observe it, or that actionable guidance is complete. Fourteen labels
were matched to actual files in knowledge_packs/rituals before reuse; a date
fixture or name alone was not treated as content.

Regional spellings and sequence-end markers route to accepted candidates only
when no material action difference is established. Banada Ashtami and the
Dasara/Dasahara/Dussehra labels route to the Devi owner. Agni Nakshatram begin/
end and the generic Solar New Year marker remain calendar explanation, not
standalone vidhi.

Avani Avittam and its Rigveda, Samaveda and Yajurveda labels, plus Gayathri
Japam, remain blocked for generic instructions. Vedic shakha, sampradaya,
eligibility, family transmission and qualified guidance materially determine
what may be done. The block preserves the user's day question but requires a
safe answer that asks context and does not simulate initiation- or priest-led
practice.

Rabindranath Tagore Jayanti remains visible for wider Indian-culture routing but
is outside this selected Sanatana puja denominator. Eclipses, pilgrimages,
temple festivals and ancestor periods are accepted candidates because they can
require participation or safety guidance even when they are not household
pujas.

## Completion caveat

Raw-label disposition is now complete at 425/425. Normalized candidate
acceptance is only the inventory boundary. Most accepted candidates still lack
bilingual stories, applicability, deterministic local rules, typical practice,
materials, substitutions, ordered steps, closing, safety and reviewed variants.
The 19,480 day-answer cells are not complete merely because every label has a
disposition.
