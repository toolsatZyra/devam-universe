# Sarthi Understanding Phase 1 evidence admission

Status: **partial admission; no baseline run**.

This checkpoint admits evidence packets, not answers. Phase 0
`evidence_readiness: ready` means candidate material exists; it does not mean a
scenario is admitted. A packet is admitted only when every declared evidence
role and scope field is present, the exact product artifact is hash-bound, and
its use stays within its rights and quotation boundary.

Six of the thirty bilingual held-out pairs are admitted. Twenty-four remain
unadmitted: twelve ready candidates still need packet compilation, seven are
blocked by partial evidence, and five require research. English and Hindi share
the same packet and scope boundary; admission does not certify answer quality in
either language.

Underlying `reference_only`, `private_evidence`, `internal_only`, and
`citation_only` sources may support Devam's reviewed synthesis. Their text may
not be exposed or quoted by this evaluation. Product-cleared or
derivative-allowed records remain labelled as Devam review or product content
and never become source originals by admission.

No model has been called, no answer has been generated or scored, and no
quality, learning, understanding, production-readiness, or comparative claim is
made. A Phase 1 baseline may begin only after its selected scenarios have frozen
packets, runtime authorization, and reviewer calibration.
