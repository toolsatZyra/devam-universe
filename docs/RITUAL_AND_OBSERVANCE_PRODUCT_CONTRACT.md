# Ritual and observance product contract

## Purpose

A calendar card or a generic remembrance exercise is not a complete ritual
experience. For a user asking about a festival, vrata, weekday, tithi, temple
practice, or family observance, Devam must answer four practical questions:

1. Does this apply to me, here, today, in my tradition or family context?
2. What does it mean, and what source-labelled origin narratives explain it?
3. What is typically done, by whom, and at what time?
4. What can I actually do, step by step, at the level of observance I want?

The answer must be concise and locally relevant by default. Other regional or
sampradaya variants remain available as background and are surfaced only when
the user asks, when their context is unknown, or when the difference changes
the recommended action.

## User-complete observance record

An observance is product-complete only for a named applicability lane. A lane
binds the observance, date/rule, geography, tradition or institution, household
or temple setting, language, and version. It must contain:

- **Identity and applicability:** names and aliases; who commonly observes it;
  geography, sampradaya, institution, family-practice, life-stage, and setting
  qualifiers; and explicit questions Sarthi should ask when the answer changes.
- **Calendar and timing:** deterministic local date and relevant window;
  precedence rule; timezone and location; whether a time is astronomical,
  textual, institutional, customary, or a specific temple programme; and a
  freshness boundary for live schedules.
- **Meaning and significance:** a short user-facing explanation plus sourced
  theological, historical, social, and seasonal meanings where applicable.
- **Origin narratives:** one or more attributable stories with their source,
  textual layer, tradition, place, and known variants. A later regional story
  must not be presented as the single universal origin.
- **Typical practice:** what households, temples, communities, or particular
  institutions commonly do, labelled by the population for which evidence
  exists. Description is distinct from instruction.
- **Actionable vidhi:** preparation, materials, ordered steps, relevant words or
  readings, timing dependencies, closing, and after-practice actions. A
  traditional household form is the core when evidence supports one. A short
  accessible form and a fuller family- or teacher-led form are alternatives,
  not substitutes for documenting the actual practice.
- **Materials and substitutions:** required versus optional items, safe and
  respectful substitutions, accessibility options, and what should not be
  improvised without family, temple, or qualified guidance.
- **Variants and conflicts:** materially different regional, linguistic,
  sampradaya, temple, and family forms remain separate. Devam records conflicts
  and the rule used for the current answer rather than silently flattening them.
- **Evidence:** passage- or page-level support where available, source role,
  provenance, rights lane, and confidence for each material claim or step.
- **Boundaries:** health, fire, crowd, food, fasting, pregnancy, medication,
  environmental, legal, and specialist-practice cautions where actually
  relevant. Safety is not a reason to erase the ordinary practice; it is a
  reason to contextualize it.

If the evidence does not support a practical sequence, the lane is a calendar,
story, or participation record—not a completed vidhi. Devam must preserve the
gap and continue research.

## Product presentation

Today and Sarthi should lead with a compact answer tailored to the user:

- what today is and why it is relevant;
- the most important local time or decision window;
- a short summary of significance;
- the next actions the user can take; and
- one natural prompt to expand the story, full vidhi, materials, timing,
  alternatives, or sources.

The full record may be rich, but the conversational answer should not read like
an academic paper. Citations and alternatives stay expandable unless they are
needed to prevent a materially wrong or unsafe answer.

## Completion and evaluation

A lane cannot be marked product-complete merely because it has a deterministic
date, bilingual text, three duration tiers, a source list, or many explicit
denials. Automated and human checks must prove:

- applicability is correctly selected for representative user profiles;
- meaning, story, typical practice, timing, and vidhi are all present or the
  lane is honestly classified as incomplete;
- the action sequence is coherent from preparation through closing;
- every material instruction is attributable to the correct scope;
- a regional or institutional example is not universalized;
- a simplified path does not masquerade as the complete traditional practice;
- unsupported outcomes, invented timings, and synthetic ritual steps are absent;
- Hindi and English render the same scoped meaning without erasing source terms;
  and
- Sarthi gives the relevant answer first and does not volunteer unrelated
  variants.

This contract is the completion boundary for the September-December layer and
all later festival and ritual acquisition.

The first conforming implementation is
`knowledge_packs/rituals/bengal-mahashtami-community-participant-2026-v1.json`.
Its `user_complete_lane` status applies only to the exact named participant
scope. It is deliberately not evidence that priest-led, household-consecration,
all-Bengal, all-Durga, or September-December coverage is complete.
