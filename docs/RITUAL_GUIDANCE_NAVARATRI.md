# Shardiya Navaratri household guidance

## Implemented boundary

The MVP now contains one complete-in-scope internal-beta procedure for the
supported North India and West India Smarta household contexts at the beginning
of Shardiya Navaratri. It is available in English and Hindi. Region and
tradition must be a valid pair; Devam does not cross-wire a North India region
with the West India practice code or vice versa.

The frozen research pack is
`knowledge_packs/rituals/shardiya-navaratri-north-west-india-v1.json`:

- bytes: `36,235`
- SHA-256: `d25a2d4d1d16fe56e119ff78ad03d6d825ef09c5a40611c03e411ddb995fcbb9`
- contract: `DEVAM_RITUAL_PROCEDURE_PACK_V1`

The loader verifies the exact hash, two-guide/six-source universe, tier and
ten-day order, step ordinals, every source binding, and the complete set of
fail-closed practice denials before returning the guide.

## What the guide provides

- a sustainable ten-minute daily Devi prayer without requiring installation;
- a bounded Ghatasthapana form with timing hand-off to Devam's deterministic
  location-aware Panchang rule;
- daily care and a safe conclusion for the household setup;
- a family-text or priest-led boundary for formal recitation, homa, Kumari
  worship, and lineage-specific mantras;
- materials and substitutions in English and Hindi;
- an explicit warning against an unsupervised continuous flame; and
- a ten-day Shailaputri-to-Vijayadashami reflection path whose entries are
  editorial prompts, not mandatory ritual requirements.

## Evidence mix

The pack binds six separately typed sources: Maharashtra Tourism's official
Ghatasthapana page, the Government of India tourism Navratri overview, the
current Drik Panchang kalasha sequence, one Art of Living Durga Saptashati
practice description, the retained Vijnanananda English Devī Bhāgavatam
compilation, and the retained Pargiter Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa/Devīmāhātmya edition.
Current practice, living-tradition instruction, and historical scriptural
attestation remain distinguishable.

## Claims deliberately left open

This pack does not claim:

- one universal Navaratri procedure;
- Bengali Durga Puja, South Indian Golu, Gujarati Garba, or Nepal Dashain;
- all regional or family variants;
- formal priest mantras or initiation-bound practice;
- an unsupervised continuous flame;
- a fasting or medical regimen;
- that historical prescriptions are mandatory modern practice;
- that the editorial daily reflections are rites; or
- that every Navaratri civil date after the opening has been independently
  resolved for every location and tradition.
