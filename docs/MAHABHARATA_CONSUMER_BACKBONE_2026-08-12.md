# Mahabharata consumer backbone checkpoint

Date: 2026-08-12
Contract: `DEVAM_MAHABHARATA_CONSUMER_BACKBONE_V1`

## Outcome

The fixed Kisari Mohan Ganguli English Mahabharata expression is mapped into
one complete beginning-to-end consumer story plan for ordinary Hindi- and
English-speaking users. The plan has:

- 12 broad consumer arcs;
- 77 ordered bilingual story turns;
- exact, non-overlapping coverage of all 2,107 qualified source-relative units;
- an English and Hindi title and story promise for every turn; and
- initial character, place, kingdom and thematic traversal facets for every
  turn.

This is the narrative map that detailed scenes will expand. It is not yet a
consumer-complete story, a playable world or a database projection.

## Consumer architecture

The default journey is organized around recognisable human events rather than
Sanskrit book labels: a family becomes a world, the crown becomes a trap,
thirteen years away, peace is attempted, war opens, war consumes its heirs,
night survives the battlefield, the survivors ask how anyone can rule,
Bhishma's final counsel, a kingdom tries to heal, the elders leave the palace,
and an age ends.

Each canonical turn will later power several reversible routes without copying
the story:

- continue in story order;
- follow a character's changing point of view;
- enter through a location or kingdom;
- follow a consequence or teaching into another turn; and
- open a separately governed modern place, festival, temple or living-practice
  world without rewriting the epic as current history.

Source section coordinates remain internal grounding. Normal UI copy will lead
with events, people, choices, places and consequences in Hindi or English.

## Coverage and compression rule

Every qualified source unit belongs to exactly one contiguous turn. A turn may
compress a source interval, but its detailed scene authoring must preserve all
material actions, motivations, consequences, character continuity and place
changes within that interval. Source headings or unit counts alone do not make
a turn complete.

The current fixed boundaries deliberately exclude Harivamsha. They do not claim
that one Ganguli expression exhausts every Mahabharata tradition or the broader
civilizational story universe.

## Adversarial findings carried into authoring

- Draupadi's public humiliation, coercion and appeal to law must not become a
  decorative dice-game beat.
- Abhimanyu's isolation and many-against-one killing must remain legible rather
  than being aestheticised as spectacle.
- Ashvatthama's attack must plainly identify the sleeping survivors and the
  consequences for the next generation.
- Post-war grief must retain the perspectives of Gandhari, Kunti and bereaved
  women, not only the victors' remorse.
- Caste-ranked, gendered, royal, hospitality, gift and cow-related prescriptions
  must be situated inside their historical narrative world. They are not
  universal modern instructions.
- Dense paired ranges such as the Durvasa/Jayadratha/Rama and Savitri/Karna
  intervals require multiple detailed scenes; the backbone grouping is not
  permission to collapse their independent stories.
- The Bhagavad Gita, Yaksha questions, Vidura's counsel, Bhishma's teachings and
  other reflective passages need approachable narrative framing and cannot be
  reduced to decontextualized quote cards.

## First detailed authoring batch

The first fifteen packs under `knowledge_packs/mahabharata/` begin that next
checkpoint with 93 scenes and 424 substantial English/Hindi beats. They cover
Adi source ordinals 1–185 exactly once: the first ten backbone turns are fully
scene-authored, including the complete first Shakuntala/Bharata, Yayati,
Ganga-Bhishma-to-Pandu, Kuru-children, education-to-rivalry, and
fire-to-hidden-life, and road-to-Panchala journeys. The batches
preserve exact span hashes, characters, places and visual direction while
keeping source apparatus out of normal story copy.

The scenes are authored but not projected. They do not change the honest
`consumer_story_available: false` or `product_projection_available: false`
state. The remaining 67 turns still require the same depth before the selected
expression can be called consumer-complete.
