# Diwali/Deepavali hero universe

## Product boundary

Diwali is the fourth launch-critical Devam hero universe, alongside Ganesha,
Durga, and Ramayana. It is not a synonym for one Lakshmi Puja evening and it is
not complete because the Panchang can label a Diwali date.

The hero must let a user explore why, when, where, and how materially distinct
communities observe the festival family. It connects source works, stories,
deities, historical and living traditions, calendar decisions, practices,
places, art, family memories, and present-day public celebration without
flattening disagreements into one universal account.

## Required separately attributable lanes

- Govatsa Dwadashi/Vasu Baras where locally applicable;
- Dhanteras/Dhantrayodashi, Dhanvantari traditions, and Yama Deepam;
- Naraka Chaturdashi, Kali Chaudas, and South Indian Deepavali;
- Lakshmi Puja, business/account-book traditions, and regional household forms;
- Bengali Kali Puja as its own Shakta calendar and practice lane;
- Bali Pratipada/Padwa and distinct narratives of Bali;
- Govardhan Puja and Annakut, with Vaishnava/sampradaya distinctions;
- Bhai Dooj, Bhau Beej, and Yama Dvitiya regional forms;
- Jain Diwali and Mahavira nirvana traditions;
- Bandi Chhor Divas and its Sikh historical setting; and
- materially distinct traditions such as Nepal Tihar when included in product
  geography, without treating them as interchangeable Indian household rites.

Each lane needs its own source basis, names, language and region tags,
calendar rule, stories/claims, procedures where appropriate, applicability,
uncertainties, and rights lane. Coincident civil dates do not merge identities.

## MVP completion definition

The Diwali hero is ready only when it includes:

1. evidence-backed Hindi and English orientation for every required lane;
2. deterministic location/tradition-aware calendar decisions for launch scope;
3. actionable procedures only where their precise applicability is supported;
4. source-grounded stories and clearly labelled variants rather than a single
   invented origin narrative;
5. an interconnected Atlas region with places, deities, stories, practices,
   and a multi-day temporal path;
6. one curated journey and one challenge/mission that do not award spiritual
   merit;
7. exact retrieval and Sarthi evaluations across factual, practical, variant,
   and personal-guidance questions; and
8. explicit coverage metrics showing what is complete, partial, or absent.
