# Sarthi artificial-wisdom research integration review

**Review date:** 2026-08-07  
**Decision:** accept the research as a falsifiable architecture and evaluation
programme; do not treat it as proof that artificial wisdom has been solved.

**Final-package status:** the user confirmed that the broadened research track
is complete and authorized its applicability review. Both frozen research
manifests, all three executable mechanism probes, and the current targeted
Sarthi architecture tests were revalidated before this decision was finalized.

## Executive verdict

The research is useful and applicable to Sarthi, but its value is mainly in
governance, separation of concerns, and evaluation discipline—not in adding a
large new technical stack.

The central recommendation is accepted:

> Build Sarthi as a governed, source-grounded human-AI system whose observable
> behaviour can support better judgment. Do not market a model, prompt, graph,
> or database as inherently wise.

The broader General Artificial Wisdom Architecture (GAWA) is accepted as an
experimental audit framework. Its governance, epistemic, experiential,
abstraction, cognitive, metacognitive, and interaction planes are questions
that the system must answer. They are not seven services, seven databases, or
seven MVP workstreams. Sarthi is one domain instantiation of that framework,
with its own sources, traditions, role limits, procedures, living-practice
evidence, values, authorities, and evaluation population.

This conclusion strengthens the MVP without slowing the library programme. The
source library and user-complete ritual acquisition remain the critical path.
The intelligence layer should grow only when a held-out comparison proves that
it improves Sarthi over the same-model, same-evidence grounded baseline.

## Evidence and integrity review

The completed research directory contains two related programmes:

1. the original Sarthi-specific practical architecture; and
2. the later independent-foundations study, which separates wisdom,
   intelligence, expertise, insight, rationality, morality, prudence, common
   sense, judgment, charisma, and related constructs before applying them to
   Sarthi.

The review independently rehashed both frozen manifests:

- 19/19 records in `ARTIFACT_MANIFEST.sha256` matched;
- 16/16 records in
  `independent-foundations/FOUNDATIONAL_ARTIFACT_MANIFEST.sha256` matched.

The three local probes reproduced their recorded mechanism-level results:

- typed scope filtering improved the six authored retrieval fixtures from
  5/6 to 6/6;
- typed evidence routing improved mean required-type recall from 0.875 to 1.0
  on two authored fixtures;
- the toy Governor selected the authored acceptable option in 8/8 scenarios,
  versus 2/8 for a single-objective policy and 6/8 for a fixed weighted sum;
- relational analogy selected 3/3 authored structural matches, while the
  surface baseline selected 0/3.

These results prove that the proposed representations and policies are
executable. They do **not** prove user benefit, cultural legitimacy,
generalisation, real-world outcome improvement, or wisdom. The fixtures and
gold answers were authored to expose the intended distinctions. The research
correctly says this, and that limitation is mandatory in every downstream use.

The evidence registries are substantial architectural literature reviews. This
integration review did not independently re-browse and re-adjudicate every
external citation, so it accepts no empirical claim merely from citation
volume. Product promotion still requires task-specific evidence and testing.

## What is accepted into Devam now

### 1. Capability lattice, not a DIKW ladder

Data, information, evidence, knowledge, understanding, intelligence,
expertise, insight, rationality, metacognition, common sense, morality,
prudence, judgment, charisma, compassion, and wisdom remain distinct. More
sources, embeddings, graph edges, model parameters, or fluent prose do not
automatically produce wisdom.

This matters directly to Devam:

- the library stores sources and evidence;
- the middle layer stores scoped claims, procedures, relationships, conflicts,
  and reviewed syntheses;
- runtime intelligence retrieves, compares, computes, and plans;
- wisdom-support performance is evaluated in the quality of the resulting
  situated judgment and action—not stored as another content tier.

### 2. Sarthi as a domain instantiation

The Sarthi application must explicitly supply:

- a role and authority charter;
- an epistemic constitution distinguishing scripture, commentary,
  translation, scholarship, living practice, Devam synthesis, and model output;
- domain terminology and non-equivalence mappings;
- task, decision-impact, and authority routes;
- tradition, regional, institutional, family, and practitioner applicability;
- values and legitimacy boundaries, including affected-party and consent
  considerations;
- a capability registry describing what each model, tool, and data lane has
  actually demonstrated; and
- an evaluation constitution with claim expiry when model, evidence, policy,
  or population changes materially.

For the MVP, these may be compact versioned contracts in code and Postgres.
They do not require a general-purpose ontology platform.

### 3. Thin, inspectable Governor

The current request planner is the correct MVP shape for the research's
Governor: a small control function that decides whether to answer directly,
retrieve, compute, clarify, compare, offer conditional guidance, defer, or
escalate. It must record operational facts, not hidden chain-of-thought.

The present `DEVAM_SARTHI_RUNTIME_PLAN_V0_1` is therefore retained. It is a
first route-planning implementation, not a validated wisdom capability. Its
regex classification and forty authored routing scenarios are suitable for
deterministic regression, but they are not yet an answer-quality baseline or a
robust natural-language classifier.

### 4. Typed retrieval and smallest sufficient evidence

Retrieval should declare the evidence types required by the task before answer
generation. Rights, publication state, applicability, conflicts, and evidence
coverage filter the route. Long undifferentiated context is not wisdom and is
often worse for cost, latency, and source fidelity.

### 5. Generation, validation, selection, and rendering are distinct

One model call may physically implement more than one phase for low-impact
requests, but the contracts remain separate. A generated interpretation cannot
validate itself merely by sounding coherent. Consequential guidance requires
independent deterministic checks, evidence checks, or a separately evaluated
critic route appropriate to the decision impact.

### 6. Charisma and rhetoric firewall

Sarthi's warmth is a product strength, but warmth, confidence, fluency,
devotional vocabulary, and perceived profundity cannot count as evidence of
wisdom. Evaluation must compare content under style controls and penalise
unearned authority, sycophancy, dependency cues, fate/karma blame, coercive
certainty, and unnecessary verbosity.

### 7. Vector evaluation with hard failures

Do not publish one “wisdom score.” Evaluate source fidelity, applicability,
context sensitivity, procedural completeness, judgment, alternatives,
consequences, uncertainty, proportionality, agency, tradition fidelity,
charisma susceptibility, latency, and cost separately. Severe failures cannot
be averaged away.

## What remains experimental

The following are promising but must earn their cost in same-model,
same-evidence, held-out ablations:

- relational case retrieval with explicit similarities and disanalogies;
- observed outcome records with consent and confounder limits;
- reviewed pattern and meta-pattern hypotheses with counter-cases, scope,
  predictions, and retirement criteria;
- argument representations for real interpretive conflict;
- candidate-and-critic guidance for consequential routes;
- consequence or causal tools where assumptions and validation are explicit;
- longitudinal learning from outcomes; and
- a portable second-domain instantiation of the general framework.

The user’s core insight about deep wisdom through patterns and meta-patterns is
preserved here, but not converted into an unreviewed “wisdom database.” A
meta-pattern is a hypothesis until independent evidence, counterexamples,
scope, and prospective transfer tests support it. This protects the distinctive
vision from being diluted into eloquent pattern matching.

## What is deferred or rejected

Do not add these to the MVP:

- a native graph database merely because the domain is interconnected;
- a comprehensive cognitive architecture implemented as infrastructure;
- autonomous multi-agent debate as the default reasoning path;
- an argument or causal engine without a named task-level gain;
- automatic synthesis promotion from model output or private conversations;
- a universal Hindu or Indic moral constitution;
- automated dharma resolution or commentary harmonisation;
- prestige rankings treated as truth probability;
- a scalar wisdom score;
- engagement, deference, or emotional dependence as an optimisation target; or
- marketing claims that Sarthi, an LLM, or GAWA possesses general human wisdom.

## Sarthi implementation decision

### Keep now

- current Supabase/Postgres claim-and-evidence design;
- deterministic Panchang ownership;
- current thin planner and typed coverage contract;
- direct, conditional, plural, clarify, unable-to-ground, and escalation modes;
- concise companion rendering with expandable evidence;
- explicit source, synthesis, interpretation, and applicability boundaries.

### Add before claiming improved judgment

1. Freeze a representative baseline of complete Sarthi answers, not only route
   labels.
2. Create a 60-100 scenario consequential pilot with paired context changes,
   counterexamples, Hindi/English coverage, direct/clarify/options/defer cases,
   and source-grounded evidence bundles.
3. Add a compact situation card: affected parties, stakes, authority,
   reversibility, uncertainty, and only materially missing context.
4. Compare grounded RAG, prompt-only guidance, context routing, and the thin
   Governor under blinded, style-controlled review.
5. Promote no case, pattern, critic, or outcome layer unless it closes a
   measured residual gap without increasing hard failures, latency,
   preachiness, or unnecessary abstention.

This pilot should begin after the current hero and September-December content
has enough complete evidence lanes to make the evaluation meaningful. It must
not pause library acquisition or ritual completion.

## Final implementation-applicability audit

The completed broadened research package was rechecked after the independent
foundations work finished. Both frozen manifests still match exactly: 19/19
main records and 16/16 independent-foundations records. The three executable
mechanism probes reproduce their recorded results, and the frozen answer-level
pilot passes all 15 structural and evidence-fixity checks. The pilot still says
`not_run_no_authorized_same_model_runtime_or_blinded_review_panel`; therefore no
claim of better Sarthi judgment is currently allowed.

The present code is directionally aligned but only partially implements the
accepted architecture:

| Research requirement | Current implementation | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| strong grounded factual baseline | published claim/evidence retrieval plus deterministic product lanes | keep |
| deterministic ownership of calendar facts | Panchang route is separate from model inference | keep |
| task, impact, authority, material context and typed evidence plan | `DEVAM_SARTHI_RUNTIME_PLAN_V0_1` exists and its authored routing tests pass | keep as a scaffold, not a validated Governor |
| coverage gate before generic synthesis | generic claim fallback checks typed coverage and fails closed | keep |
| one governing route across complete answer production | generic evidence-sufficient retrieval now passes through the planner and optional bounded generation contract; current deterministic and bespoke routes still retain precedence and can return before that contract | partial; unify only after preserving the working deterministic lanes |
| situation model for consequential guidance | affected parties, alternatives and uncertainty appear as required evidence types, but there is no complete runtime situation card | add in the controlled pilot, not as a database service |
| generation, validation, selection and rendering separation | generic claim composition is now a separately testable optional Responses API stage, while citations and rendering remain application-owned; independent consequential-answer validation is not implemented | partial; pilot only |
| answer-level evidence that the architecture helps | 32 pairs / 64 scenarios are frozen, but no same-model four-arm answer run or blinded review exists | required before any capability claim |
| cases, outcomes, pattern hypotheses, arguments or causal models | intentionally absent | remain absent until a residual measured failure justifies one |

This yields a concrete implementation order:

1. finish enough complete source and ritual evidence bundles for the frozen
   scenarios, especially personal guidance and Ramayana exact-plus-interpretive
   coverage;
2. freeze the current complete-answer grounded baseline;
3. make the planner's operational plan available to every answer route without
   replacing reliable deterministic resolvers;
4. add a short-lived situation card and deterministic evidence/applicability
   validators for the consequential pilot only;
5. run the same model and evidence through the four frozen comparison arms and
   obtain blinded review; and
6. delete or defer every added component that does not improve held-out answers
   without increasing severe failures, latency, verbosity or unnecessary
   abstention.

The result is intentionally asymmetric: library, claim, procedure, variant and
applicability work proceeds now because every product surface needs it. Richer
"wisdom" machinery proceeds only after evidence that it helps a named Sarthi
task family.

## Final applicability decision

The research does not replace Devam's lean architecture; it validates its
direction and supplies a stronger scientific and product boundary. The correct
architecture is:

```text
content-addressed source library
    -> scoped claims, procedures, conflicts and relationships in Postgres
    -> deterministic tools and typed hybrid retrieval
    -> thin Sarthi domain Governor
    -> bounded validation and selection when decision impact requires it
    -> concise companion response with expandable evidence
```

Cases, outcome memory, patterns, meta-patterns, arguments, and causal tools are
an evidence-driven research expansion path around this spine. They are not the
spine itself.
