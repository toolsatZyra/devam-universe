# Ramayana district: Panchavati breaks open

## Product purpose

This is the seventh finished visual district in the bounded Ramayana story world. It tests the hardest part of the current interaction contract: whether one place can change from home, to social encounter, to war, to deception, to an emptied threshold, and finally to a moving aerial route without reducing the sequence to one-line chapter cards.

The player can traverse the same material as:

- a linear eight-scene story with 43 bilingual beats;
- a place route across Panchavati, Janasthana, Maricha's retreat, the golden-deer path, the cottage, and the southward sky-road;
- character constellations for Jatayu, Surpanakha, Khara, Dushana, Akampana, Ravana, Maricha, Rama, Sita, and Lakshmana;
- four playable entrances from the whole-epic compass: `panchavati-surpanakha`, `war-at-janasthana`, `golden-deer-plot`, and `sita-is-taken`;
- reversible encounter routes that return to the exact scenes in which a character or place participates.

The district ends after Jatayu's resistance and Sita's dropped cloth and ornaments. Rama and Lakshmana's return, the search, grief, and the dying Jatayu begin with source-ordered passage 54 and remain outside this checkpoint.

## Eight scene worlds

1. **Jatayu welcomes them to Panchavati** — an alarming silhouette becomes an old family friend, guardian, and neighbour to a newly built home.
2. **Surpanakha breaks Panchavati's quiet** — desire, rivalry, rejection, and violent humiliation turn a household encounter into a wider conflict.
3. **Janasthana falls** — appeals and successive forces expand the conflict until Akampana carries news toward Ravana.
4. **Ravana chooses deception** — competing reports, Surpanakha's provocation, Maricha's warning, and coercion form the separation plan.
5. **The golden deer separates the house** — wonder, suspicion, pursuit, and an imitated voice pull the three inhabitants apart.
6. **A mendicant reaches the empty cottage** — fear wounds Sita and Lakshmana's conversation, and hospitality becomes the final unguarded threshold.
7. **Sita calls the forest to witness** — Sita refuses Ravana and actively turns trees, river, mountain, birds, and animals into a witness network.
8. **Jatayu rises against Ravana** — unequal resistance interrupts the route, and cloth and ornaments make the world below a recoverable trail.

## Art and interaction direction

Each scene has original 16:9 painterly environment art with distinct foreground, middle, and background planes. The visual grammar progresses from warm shelter through storm, deceptive radiance, moonlit suspense, and aerial scale. Surpanakha's scene avoids mutilation and caricature; the Janasthana and Jatayu scenes communicate consequence without gore; the abduction scenes keep Sita's refusal, speech, witness-making, and evidence trail visually active.

The district uses the existing bounded 2.5D camera and motion system. Scene switching changes the environment, each beat changes focal crop, depth, palette, and movement, and direct scene buttons permit skipping without losing back/forward continuity. Characters and places open as in-world encounter constellations rather than ejecting the visitor into a text page.
