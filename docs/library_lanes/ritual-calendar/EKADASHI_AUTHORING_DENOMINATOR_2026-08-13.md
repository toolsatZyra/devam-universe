# Ekadashi named-lane authoring denominator

Frozen on 2026-08-13 for the ritual-calendar lane. This document resolves the
selected-scope shorthand `ekadashi-north-west-smarta-all-year-v1`; it does not
create a generic national Ekadashi ritual.

## Exact denominator

The reviewed 41-label census resolves to **30 named applicability work items**:

- **24 ordinary named Ekadashi lanes**: Aja, Amalaki, Apara, Devshayani,
  Devutthana, Indira, Jaya, Kamada, Kamika, Mokshada, Nirjala, Papamochani,
  Papankusha, Parivartani/Parsva, Pausha Putrada, Rama, Saphala, Shattila,
  Shravana Putrada, Utpanna, Varuthini, Vijaya and Yogini;
- **six material date/tradition lanes**: Gauna Kamika, Gauna Pausha Putrada,
  Vaishnava Kamika, Vaishnava Papanasini, Vaishnava Pausha Putrada and
  Vaishnava Rama; and
- **eleven regional-name aliases**, which do not add work items unless evidence
  later proves a changed deity/story, eligibility, ordered sequence or close:
  Sattila, Baruthini, Harishayan, Deva Utthapan, Pankoddhar, Jala Krida,
  Kaliyadalan, Gomati, Pavitra, Parivartani and Parswa Paribartan.

The 24 ordinary names are named-family work, not a promise of exactly 24 civil
dates in every year. For example, Saphala occurs twice in the New Delhi 2027
fixture. A completed Saphala lane must answer both resolved occurrences without
being counted twice in the 208-item authoring denominator.

## Major-difference rule

A separate pack or route is required only when at least one of these changes:

1. the deterministic civil date or parana applicability;
2. the responsible sampradaya, temple or family authority;
3. who is eligible or expected to perform the action;
4. the named source story or deity focus in a way that changes the answer;
5. the ordered actionable practice or close; or
6. a material health or safety boundary.

Language, spelling, ordinary flowers or fruit, dress, household image/no-image,
short prayer choice, and voluntary fast/non-fast choice remain within a lane
when they do not change those six things. State copies are forbidden on those
minor grounds.

## Reuse audit

The existing recurring content and practice packs provide a bilingual,
non-fasting Vishnu/Krishna remembrance scaffold and an explicit warning that
place, Panchang profile, sampradaya and parana must be resolved. They are useful
starting material but do **not** complete any of the 30 named items because they
do not provide each item's source-labelled story, named timing fixtures and
material variants.

The 2027 census evidence establishes label identity and alias/date comparison
only. It does not establish fasting eligibility, food rules, medication advice,
parana windows, mantra, story, temple programme or promised result.

## Authoring order and first lane

Author in 2027 civil-date order, while counting named work items only once.
Start with `ekadashi-saphala-north-west-smarta-household-2027-v1`, whose New
Delhi discovery fixtures are 3 January and 23 December 2027. Then proceed
through Pausha Putrada, Shattila, Jaya, Vijaya, Amalaki, Papamochani, Kamada,
Varuthini, Mohini, Apara, Nirjala, Yogini, Devshayani, Kamika, Shravana Putrada,
Aja, Parivartani/Parsva, Indira, Papankusha, Rama, Devutthana, Utpanna and
Mokshada, interleaving the six material Gauna/Vaishnava lanes on their resolved
dates.

Each named lane remains incomplete until it has English and Hindi meaning,
source-labelled narrative, ordinary-observer applicability, deterministic
timing dependency, typical practice, ordered steps, materials, substitutions,
safety, closing and major variants. Named temple programmes remain live-check
routes; Devam does not copy temple liturgy or prescribe fasting/parana from a
generic card.
