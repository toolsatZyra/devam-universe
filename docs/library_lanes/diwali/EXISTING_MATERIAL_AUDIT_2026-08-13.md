# Existing Diwali material audit

The repository already contains strong bounded ritual work, but it does not yet
contain the selected Diwali consumer library defined by this lane.

## Reused without duplication

Fifteen current-contract ritual packs are valid practice inputs for fifteen of
the twenty frozen worlds. They remain in the shared `knowledge_packs/rituals`
directory, are referenced by exact SHA-256, and will not be copied or edited.
They cover Maharashtra Vasu Baras; North/West Dhantrayodashi and Yama Deepam;
Maharashtra Naraka Chaturdashi; Tamil dawn Deepavali; BAPS Kali Chaudash; West
India Lakshmi Puja; Bengal Kali Puja; Maharashtra Bali Pratipada; Karnataka
Balipadyami; BAPS Gujarati New Year; ISKCON Govardhana/Annakut; North India Bhai
Dooj; Jain lay remembrance; and SGPC-context Bandi Chhor participation.

## Missing at the freeze

Five selected worlds still need lane-local scoped practice or participation
records: the North Indian Rama-homecoming association, a non-BAPS generic
Gujarati New Year household orientation, Nathdwara Shrinathji Annakut
institutional orientation, Maharashtra Bhau Beej, and Bengal Bhai Phota.

All thirteen selected story families need substantial bilingual authoring.
Existing ritual-pack story fields are valuable source-labelled summaries, but
none is counted as a beginning-to-end consumer story.

## Material that is deliberately not counted twice

- Panchang records prove bounded calendar decisions, not story or full vidhi.
- Legacy ritual packs are superseded provenance inputs, not additional coverage.
- The six-stop journey is a useful orientation slice, not the twenty-world
  denominator.
- Atlas nodes and edges prove navigation presence, not content depth.
- A generic five-day page is misleading if treated as the order, meaning, or
  origin followed by all communities.

The machine-readable audit is
`knowledge_packs/library_lanes/diwali/existing-material-audit-v1.json`.
