## Completion claim boundary

This denominator defines the consumer-MVP Diwali/Deepavali world that this
isolated lane may call complete. It is not every Indian, South Asian, household,
temple, sectarian, or diaspora observance. It does not make one festival origin,
calendar, theology, or procedure universal.

The lane is complete only when all twenty selected worlds have:

1. approachable English and Hindi orientation;
2. a separately attributed meaning and present-practice account;
3. a complete named practice or participation lane, either by exact reference
   to an already reviewed shared ritual pack or by a lane-local scoped record;
4. every associated selected story linked to a substantial beginning-to-end
   bilingual story sequence;
5. characters and places that resolve locally or through a typed cross-lane
   proposal;
6. source and rights metadata sufficient to distinguish fixed narrative
   evidence, current official evidence, living-practice evidence, and Devam
   synthesis; and
7. zero unclassified selected-scope gaps.

Authored lane files are not shared-registry integration, database projection,
Atlas hosting, review acceptance, or release.

## Selected places and institutions

The lane will provide consumer place records for Ayodhya, the mythic ocean of
milk, Pragjyotisha, Vraja/Govardhan, Pavapuri, Gwalior Fort, and Amritsar. It
will provide current, separately scoped institution or temple records for
Dakshineswar Kali Temple, Kalighat Kali Temple, Shrinathji Temple at Nathdwara,
ISKCON Bangalore, Jal Mandir/Pavapuri, Gurdwara Data Bandi Chhor at Gwalior,
and Sri Harmandir Sahib. Current hours and programmes are freshness-bound and
are never inferred from story evidence.
