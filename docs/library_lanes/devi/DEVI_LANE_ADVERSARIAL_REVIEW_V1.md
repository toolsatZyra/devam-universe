# Devi lane adversarial review v1

## Claim ceiling

The selected denominator is authored, not projected, hosted, released or
independently culturally reviewed. Its complete counters apply only to the
frozen lane denominator. They do not establish complete Devi literature,
theology, worship, geography or living tradition.

## Adversarial findings addressed

- All 588 source units must appear exactly once. The lane validator reconstructs
  every chapter/verse coordinate from the existing translation input and compares
  it in order with the 26 story ranges.
- The frame story cannot disappear behind battle headings. Five opening moments
  preserve Suratha, Samadhi, Medhas and their question; the final moment returns
  to their different boons.
- Durga, Mahamaya, Ambika, Chandika, Kaushiki, Kalika, Kali and Chamunda cannot
  be flattened. Six dossiers preserve source-scoped naming and living-identity
  boundaries.
- The story's coercion and violence cannot become present instruction or labels
  for human groups. The refusal, seizure attempts and battle remain explicit,
  while consumer copy rejects human demonisation.
- Source benefit claims cannot become empirical guarantees. Chapter 92 is
  presented as the sacred text's own claim and not as app-generated promise or
  ritual procedure.
- Navadurga days cannot be projected onto the thirteen chapters without a
  separately sourced living tradition.
- Bengal Durga Puja, Gujarat Garba, Karnataka Navami and North/West household
  Navaratri cannot be merged into one national practice.
- Kali Puja and Diwali Lakshmi Puja are cross-lane connections. Their actionable
  records are reused by reference and not duplicated in the Devi lane.
- Temple belief, current institution, datable history and visitor operations
  remain separate fields. Current operational facts expire on 2026-11-13 unless
  revalidated sooner by a new notice.
- No large source carrier, application projection, shared registry, migration or
  other lane file is written by this task.

## Still requiring independent review

The bilingual consumer prose needs independent Sanskrit-aware and Hindi
editorial review. The living-practice and place dossiers need reviewers familiar
with the named regions and institutions. Integration must independently resolve
canonical identities and proposal targets. Those review states are not relabelled
as authoring gaps inside the selected denominator.
