# Ramayana district: Deeper into Dandaka

## Product purpose

This is the sixth finished visual district in the Ramayana world. It tests whether Devam can turn a bounded source sequence into multidimensional storytelling rather than a chapter list or a stack of text cards.

The player can move through the same material as:

- a linear eight-scene story;
- a place route from Chitrakoot toward Panchavati;
- character doorways for Sita, Rama, Lakshmana, Atri, Anasuya, Viradha, Sarabhanga, Sutikshna, Mandakarni, and Agastya;
- relationship constellations linking events, people, places, and the next story threshold;
- a 49-turn whole-epic compass in which `deeper-into-forest` and `dandaka-vows` now open playable scenes.

The district ends before Jatayu appears. That is a deliberate product and evidence boundary for the next expansion.

## Eight scene worlds

1. **Chitrakoot can no longer hold them** — the first forest home empties under threat and memory.
2. **Sita tells her own beginning** — a story-within-story in which Sita is the narrator.
3. **Dandaka receives three travellers** — the forest appears as a network of lived-in hermitages.
4. **Viradha breaks the road** — danger interrupts navigation and forces a change of method.
5. **The forest makes its case** — vulnerable communities turn a private exile into a public obligation.
6. **Sita questions the bow** — an unresolved ethical dialogue remains explorable rather than becoming a slogan.
7. **Ten years become a living map** — Panchapsara and many hermitage stays express time through traversable geography.
8. **Agastya points toward Panchavati** — hospitality, storied gifts, concern for Sita, and a route to the next home converge.

Together these scenes contain 46 bilingual beats. Detailed scripts are loaded only for the active district; the six-district initial story manifest remains below 300 KB raw and 48 KB gzip.

## Art direction

Every scene uses original 16:9 cinematic painterly art with foreground, midground, and background planes suitable for 2.5D camera movement. The common visual language is grounded Indian epic game concept art: natural faces and anatomy, tactile forest materials, restrained mythic light, no text embedded in art, and no calendar-poster or generic Western-fantasy styling.

Each scene has a distinct spatial purpose:

- departure vista;
- intimate memory courtyard;
- open settlement constellation;
- interrupted ravine path;
- community gathering hub;
- branching ethical threshold;
- time-place lake and long route;
- hospitality foreground opening toward the next destination.

## Exact source boundary

The selected expression is Manmatha Nath Dutt's product-allowed English prose translation retained in the one-copy source vault.

- Ayodhya volume object: `sha256:7d3b9e1613d60dfacea39f2564243e943cf38703eadb7245d92337b238082034`
  - source ordinals 191-193;
  - Ayodhya Kanda-relative units 116-118;
  - byte span 984977-1003002;
  - line span 15565-15841.
- Aranya volume object: `sha256:c3ef74a07ef0cf016eb0428deb76d6036d13be343c65225946471113a2da475b`
  - first twelve source-ordered passages;
  - byte span 17794-79231;
  - line span 299-1264.

The Aranya source prints `SECTI0N VI.` with a zero. Devam does not silently repair the source marker: byte spans and source order are authoritative, and the defect is retained in citation metadata.

This district is a retelling of one selected English expression. It is not every Ramayana recension, historical GIS, a modern travel route, a universal ethical settlement, or a complete account of any character, place, theology, or present institution.

## Replication contract

A future district is not complete merely because it has nodes or summaries. It must provide:

1. an explicit source and story boundary;
2. scene-level source reconstruction and rights metadata;
3. a complete detailed story sequence appropriate to the bounded material;
4. original visual worlds with distinct spatial grammar;
5. linear forward/back movement and direct scene hopping;
6. map, character, place, and relationship entry points that return to the story;
7. stable pan, zoom, reset, keyboard, wheel, and pinch behavior on desktop and mobile;
8. bilingual story delivery without requiring Sanskrit or scholarly apparatus;
9. lazy payload limits and image prefetching proportionate to the active district;
10. unit, API, desktop-browser, mobile-browser, source-boundary, and visual inspection evidence.
