# Devam product brief

## Vision

Devam brings the significant knowledge of Sanatana Dharma, Hindu traditions, Indian culture, and Indian wisdom under one roof. “All” means exhaustive coverage of significant works, traditions, practices, stories, people, places, events, and materially distinct versions—not every redundant scan or printing.

The source library is the underlying data layer. A claim-centered intelligence layer connects sources, entities, relationships, places, times, traditions, variants, and evidence. Users consume it through the Living Atlas, Sarthi, and exact search/retrieval.

## Core experiences

### Living Atlas

A browser-first, responsive 2.5D open world with the immediacy of a premium
exploration game: a full-viewport cosmic knowledge universe, master entities as
luminous stars, connected discoveries at different apparent depths, spatial
camera movement, parallax, atmosphere, and continuous cinematic transitions.
The graph is felt as a place before it is presented as an interface. It supports:

- free exploration;
- curated narrative or practice journeys; and
- challenges/missions, including optional privacy-conscious real-world challenges.

The primary consumer path is story-first in Hindi or English. A user entering
Ramayana, Ganesha, Durga, or Diwali continues travelling through a spatial story
world; the product must not collapse into a conventional article page. Source
identity, editions, variants, Sanskrit originals, and uncertainty remain
available as a progressive-disclosure trust layer rather than dominating the
first encounter. Panchang and ritual utilities are contextual destinations, not
persistent Atlas chrome. Sarthi is a floating companion control that opens a
chat over the current world; it does not displace exploration.

It is not a children’s game and does not award “spiritual merit.” Game feel means
movement, discovery, wonder, continuity, and optional missions—not gamifying
sacred authority. Sacred figures use consistent approved iconography. Native
apps, full 3D/AAA scope, and optional generated 3D portals come later.

### Sarthi

Sarthi is a warm, wise, culturally fluent companion—not a guru or divine authority. It understands scripture, philosophy, tradition, ritual, Panchang, stories, temples, and the user’s conversational context. It asks naturally about location, language, family practice, and sampradaya when useful.

Answers are concise and human by default. Evidence, exact passages, variants, and “why Sarthi says this” remain available beneath the conversation but do not clutter every reply. Alternatives appear when requested or materially necessary.

Sarthi can offer practical personal guidance through the lens of Indian wisdom without simplistic fate/karma blame. It remembers only with user control; sensitive matters remain session-specific unless the user asks otherwise. Imminent danger or crisis receives compassionate escalation.

Sarthi's intelligence is not only retrieval. It links facts, procedures,
stories, cases, relationships, recurring patterns, counterexamples, values in
tension, and short- and long-term consequences. Its guidance should demonstrate
contextual judgment, humility, compassion, and practical relevance. Reviewed
pattern and meta-pattern hypotheses remain evidence-linked, scoped, versioned
Devam syntheses—not scripture, settled wisdom, or uninspectable model intuition. See
`SARTHI_WISDOM_ARCHITECTURE.md`.

### Search and APIs

Exact retrieval returns relevant passages/articles plus provenance. Ask mode synthesizes a grounded, natural multilingual answer. Internal exact-retrieval and ask-Sarthi endpoints exist from the start; a public developer API comes later.

## MVP

The MVP is a broad civilizational library and Atlas with four deep hero universes:

1. Ganesha and Ganesh Chaturthi;
2. Durga, Navaratri, and Durga Puja;
3. Ramayana; and
4. Diwali/Deepavali as a multi-day, multi-tradition festival universe.

It also includes the complete September-December Panchang and ritual layer, covering every relevant tithi, festival, weekday observance, timing, regional variation, procedure, materials, substitutions, minimum/standard/elaborate forms, and source basis.

For each completed festival or ritual lane, the user must be able to learn what
applies to them, why it matters and which source-labelled stories explain it,
what people in that context typically do and when, and what they can do step by
step. A deterministic date card or a remembrance-only companion is valuable but
does not complete a vidhi. `RITUAL_AND_OBSERVANCE_PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md` is the
completion boundary.

Ramayana coverage is grounded chiefly in Valmiki while making major variants visible. Product-usable Valmiki Ramayana and Ramcharitmanas, strong Hindi/English translations, selected commentary, and comparison dossiers for Kamba, Krittivasi, Adhyatma, Jain/Buddhist, and Southeast Asian tellings are required.

Durga coverage includes the complete Devi Mahatmya/Chandi/Saptashati identity, Sanskrit plus Hindi/English, relevant Devi Bhagavata and Kalika Purana material, regional ritual sources, and Shakti Peethas.

Ganesha coverage includes Ganapati Atharvashirsha, Ganesha Purana, Mudgala Purana, relevant Purana narratives, hymns and ritual sources, Ashtavinayak, regional forms, and public Ganeshotsav history.

Diwali coverage includes the multi-day calendrical and practice sequence rather than a single generic festival page: Govatsa Dwadashi/Vasu Baras where applicable, Dhanteras/Dhantrayodashi and Yama Deepam, Naraka Chaturdashi/Kali Chaudas and South Indian Deepavali, Lakshmi Puja, Bengali Kali Puja, Bali Pratipada/Padwa, Govardhan Puja/Annakut, Bhai Dooj/Bhau Beej/Yama Dvitiya, Jain Diwali, Bandi Chhor Divas, and materially distinct regional traditions. Their dates, meanings, stories, procedures, and applicability remain separate when sources or communities differ.

The announced Diwali 2026 worldwide theatrical release of Part 1 of Namit Malhotra's two-part *Ramayana* makes Diwali and Ramayana a coordinated launch-discovery opportunity. The film is an editorial/campaign gateway, never evidence for scripture, festival dates, history, or ritual instructions. Because release timing is mutable, the campaign must revalidate the official film channel before publication.

MVP languages are Hindi and English. Sanskrit originals are preserved. Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, and Tamil follow, then other commonly spoken Indian languages. Authoritative regional-language sources are retained when they carry distinct tradition or meaning; translation does not erase them.

## Product and business choices

- One responsive browser/PWA, rich on desktop and good-quality smartphones; progressive loading and caching adapt to bandwidth.
- Guest preview: a few places and one short Sarthi exchange; account required to continue.
- One paid subscription with monthly/annual billing. Free accounts show ongoing value; full Atlas, unified library, and sustained personalized Sarthi are premium.
- One conversational onboarding learns language, location, motivation, and interests. Deeper context emerges naturally.
- Selected SEO/share previews are public; the full product remains gated.
- Social assets meet current high-end AI-filmmaker standards. Internal provenance is complete; no per-asset AI disclaimer is required unless law/platform rules require it.
- Broad visual traditions are valid inspiration, but Devam must not copy protected characters/scenes/branding or imitate a named living artist.

## Deferred until after MVP

Native apps, bulk audio/video acquisition, open social networking, commerce/bookings/donations, predictive astrology, complete offline mode, detailed temple travel/live operations, full cost governance, and a mature habit-loop system. Devam-created media is added as it is produced.
