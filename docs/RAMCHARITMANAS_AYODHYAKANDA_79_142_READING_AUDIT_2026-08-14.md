## Adversarial consumer review

- Sita's choice is not inferred from silence: the batch preserves her explicit
  answer to Sumantra and connects it to her earlier repeated decision.
- Dasharatha's collapse, death language, Sumantra's distress, and the citizens'
  lament remain narrative speech, not medical or self-harm advice.
- Rama's departure from sleeping citizens preserves their lack of participation
  in that decision and is not generalized as a model of public accountability.
- Lakshmana's karma, dream, detachment, and divine-identity teaching is labelled
  devotional philosophy, not universal therapeutic or financial guidance.
- Guha, Kevat, Kol, Kirat, villagers, guides, and travellers retain agency,
  hospitality, boundaries, voluntary choices, and local expertise. Community
  labels and period hierarchy do not become moral essence or caste ranking.
- Kevat's refusal of fare is his chosen devotional act and never authority for
  unpaid labour. His foot-washing request is story, not universal ritual vidhi.
- Ganga's voice, transformations, divine builders, omens, and forest harmony
  remain traditional or poetic claims rather than verified history or science.
- Prayag, Yamuna, Shringaverapura, and Chitrakoot are traditional narrative
  geography here. The pack does not claim that one exact ancient route has been
  verified against modern maps.
- Period modesty, marriage ideals, appearance descriptions, and Valmiki's
  practice list remain contextual. They are not control over women, intrusive
  traveller etiquette, compulsory rites, or a ranking of readers.
- Chitrakoot hospitality is reciprocal. Kol and Kirat communities are not
  romanticized as a hereditary service class, and Lakshmana's family care is
  not converted into a demand for self-erasure.

No invented dialogue, modern temple claim, ritual universality, geographic
certainty, or reconciliation across different Ramayana expressions was added.

## Reading-product conclusion

Ramcharitmanas and Hanuman Chalisa are complete-reading-work lanes. A user may
read one page, passage, doha, chaupai, or larger section per day, but each mode
must advance through the same full canonical sequence and resume at the exact
next position. A summary, isolated explanation, or feed of excerpts never
completes the lane. The existing payload-free schema already models sequence,
passage, source unit, bilingual text, and exact user progress; no new migration
or hosted database change was needed for this data checkpoint.
