# Devam

Devam is building one home for the knowledge, traditions, stories, places, practices, and wisdom of Sanatana Dharma and Indian civilization.

The product has three doors:

- the Living Atlas: a cinematic 2.5D world for free exploration, curated journeys, and challenges;
- Sarthi: a warm, source-grounded companion who turns the library into useful guidance; and
- Search/API: exact retrieval and grounded synthesis.

The launch MVP develops four deep hero universes—Ganesha, Durga/Navaratri/Durga Puja, Ramayana, and Diwali/Deepavali—inside a broader civilizational library, together with the complete September-December Panchang and ritual layer.

Start with the [product brief](docs/PRODUCT_BRIEF.md), [experience design](docs/MVP_EXPERIENCE_DESIGN.md), [technical design](docs/MVP_TECHNICAL_DESIGN.md), [data model](docs/MVP_DATA_MODEL.md), [implemented data foundation](docs/MVP_DATA_FOUNDATION_IMPLEMENTED.md), [first Ganesha knowledge pack](docs/KNOWLEDGE_PACK_GANESHA.md), and [implementation plan](docs/IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md).

The Diwali vertical already keeps Dhantrayodashi, Yama Deepam, Bengali Kali Puja, Tamil Deepavali, ISKCON Govardhana/Annakut, North India Bhai Dooj, and West India Lakshmi Puja as distinct calendar and practice lanes rather than flattening them into one national sequence.
